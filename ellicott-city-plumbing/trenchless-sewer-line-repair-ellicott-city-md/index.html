    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Ellicott City, MD

    CIPP stands for cured-in-place pipe. The crew saturates a felt tube with two-part epoxy on a roller table at the truck, invert it into the cleaned line with air pressure, and hold it against the old pipe wall until the resin hardens. Cure time runs a few hours. The finished liner seals cracks and leaking joints in the original pipe.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Lining in Ellicott City, MD

    Cast iron rusts from the inside out, and the bottom of the pipe goes first. Plumbers call that channel rot, a groove that eats through the bottom. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or carbide cutters spinning on a flexible shaft, which brings the wall back to bare metal so a liner can bond to it. Under a slab, lining beats breaking up the floor in two rooms.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Replacement in Ellicott City

    Orangeburg is bituminous fiber pipe that was laid in houses mostly from the late 1940s into the early 1970s. Over time it softens, goes oval and blisters on the inside, so a liner has nothing round to bond to and bursting is usually the better fix. Clay is different. Clay that still holds its round shape is a good candidate for a liner.

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow Trenchless Systems in Ellicott City

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow both make lining systems we use, each with its own resin chemistry and installation equipment. Both are built to ASTM standards for cured-in-place pipe. The brand matters less than the prep, because a liner bonded to a dirty, scaly pipe wall will not hold the way the manufacturer rates it. We clean to bare pipe first.

    Cost Comparison of Trenchless vs Traditional in Ellicott City, MD

    Depth changes the math. A shallow line in open lawn is cheap to dig, but a lateral eight feet deep that runs under the driveway and a porch costs far more to excavate than to burst or line from two pits. We quote both ways. Then you pick.

    Commercial Trenchless Sewer Repair in Ellicott City, MD

    Office and retail buildings often have a cast iron building drain under the slab that has rotted along the bottom. Lining it from a cleanout avoids saw-cutting the floor inside an occupied tenant space. Work gets scheduled around tenant hours, nights included. A typical commercial lining job runs one to three days, depending on footage and how many branch connections need to be reinstated with a cutter.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacements in Ellicott City

    A bursting head is a steel cone, slightly wider than the old pipe, pulled through the line by a hydraulic unit or a winch cable. It cracks the old pipe outward into the soil while a string of new HDPE pipe follows right behind it. Fused joints, no gaps. We use it when the old line is too broken or flattened to hold a liner.
