    Sump Pump Types We Install in Ellicott City, MD

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Ellicott City

    Nor'easters, tropical storm remnants and summer downpours can overwhelm storm drains across Maryland and push water toward basements. A primary pump on household current does nothing once the breaker panel goes dark. The fix is a second, 12-volt pump on its own float, set a little higher than the main pump and wired to a charger and deep-cycle battery. It also covers a stuck primary switch. A high-water alarm lets you know the backup has taken over.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Ellicott City, MD

    Zoeller cast iron pumps, the M53 and M63 in particular, are what we pull off the truck most often because they take heat and hard cycling better than plastic housings. If you have a Zoeller now, we can usually match the discharge outlet and switch style so the swap goes quickly. Need more lift? We can move you up to a half-horse model like the M98.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Ellicott City, MD

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They are handy when the basin is too small for two pumps side by side. We replace the whole assembly or just the failed side.

    Light Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Ellicott City, MD

    Light commercial work, think retail basements and small office buildings, usually means a 1/2 HP or larger cast iron pump with a 2-inch discharge and a panel alarm. We replace like for like or upsize. Grinder and ejector pumps are a separate job.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Ellicott City

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. A check valve that leaks lets the discharge column fall back into the pit, so the pump runs again for the same water and wears itself out early. New valve every time. In a tight pit we use a vertical switch, and in a wide pit a tethered float.

    Choosing a Backup Battery in Ellicott City

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. Most backups use a sealed AGM or deep-cycle battery around 75 to 100 amp-hours, which covers several hours of on-and-off pumping. We ask how often your pump runs in a storm and size the battery to match. Batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Ellicott City, MD

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. You will hear one kick on from upstairs. We set the new column plumb, secure it so vibration does not walk it across the basin, and adjust the float rod stops for the cycle you want. They fit pits as narrow as 10 inches.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Ellicott City, MD

    Yard drainage pits and window well pumps can sit dry from fall to spring and then seize the first time a big storm fills them. We swap in a corrosion-resistant pump and clear out leaves and silt. Discharge goes away from the foundation. A bucket test every spring catches problems early.

    Sump Pump Replacement Near Me in Ellicott City, MD

    Local drainage matters. We look at the grading around your foundation and where the discharge line dumps, because water that comes right back toward the house keeps the pump running all night. A longer discharge run is sometimes all it takes.

    Speed counts on a sump call. When a pump fails mid-storm, a basement can pick up inches of water fast, which is why the nearest tech gets the call. The right pump is usually already on the truck.
