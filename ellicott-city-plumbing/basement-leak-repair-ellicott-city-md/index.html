    How We Fix Basement Leaks in Ellicott City, MD

    New Sump Pump Installation in Ellicott City

    Installing a new sump means breaking out a section of slab, setting a lined pit, and running the discharge out through the rim joist. Most of our installs get a cast iron pump, a check valve and 1-1/2 inch discharge. Discharge cannot go into the sewer in most places. It has to discharge outside, far enough from the foundation that it does not cycle back into the pit, often ten feet or more.

    Interior Drainage Systems in Ellicott City

    Where digging outside is not practical, an interior drain system does the same job from inside. A strip of slab comes out along the walls, perforated pipe goes in on stone, and it all drains to a sump. The wall gets a dimpled membrane. Seepage runs down behind it and into the pipe.

    Crack Injection for Basements in Ellicott City

    Injection works on poured concrete walls, not block. Ports go along the crack, the face gets sealed, and resin goes in from the bottom up until it fills the full depth. Epoxy adds strength. For a crack that is actively wet, polyurethane works better since it reacts with water.

    Wall Leak Solutions in Ellicott City, MD

    Damp walls without a crack often point to water soaking through porous block or concrete. Tape foil to the wall for a full day, and if the room side is wet it is condensation, not a leak. Condensation needs a dehumidifier. Seepage needs drainage outside or a channel inside.

    Water Damage Assessment in Ellicott City, MD

    Before any repair, we figure out where the water came from and how far it spread. Moisture meters on drywall and baseboards show the wet area, and a thermal camera picks up cold spots where water is wicking behind the finish. Guessing costs money. We give you the readings and photos for your insurer.

    Exterior Waterproofing Solutions in Ellicott City, MD

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing, cleaning the wall, and applying a membrane with a drainage board over it. It is the most thorough fix and the most disruptive one. Expect a few days of work. We bring in a waterproofing partner for the membrane and handle the footing drain and its tie-in to a sump or daylight outlet ourselves.
