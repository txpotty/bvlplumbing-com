    P-Trap Replacement in Ellicott City

    Thin chrome traps are the ones we replace most. They rust inside and their nuts seize, so we cut them out and fit heavy-wall PVC or polished brass. Then it gets a full flow test. Total time is often under an hour.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Replacement in Ellicott City

    Kitchen drains start failing at the basket strainer. Old strainer putty dries out and the locknut rusts, so water runs down the tailpiece and looks like a trap leak. We replace the strainer and trap together. Disposal sinks get a new flange instead.

    Leaking P-Trap Replacement in Ellicott City

    Traps leak at the joints first. Slip nut washers flatten and harden with age, and a trap opened often for clogs usually has stripped threads. Trap kits are cheap. Labor is most of the bill.

    Pop-Up Drain Replacement in Ellicott City

    Lift-rod pop-ups are the most common bath drain. The old body comes out, the seat gets cleaned, and the new flange goes in sealed with a clevis set for easy lifting. Finish matches the faucet. Overflow-style drains are standard.

    Vessel and Grid Drain Replacement in Ellicott City

    Vessel sinks use a different drain. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted grid or umbrella drain, or water leaks out the slots. Mismatched drains are common. Swapping it takes under an hour.

    Emergency Sink Drain Service in Ellicott City

    A trap that falls apart sends a sink of water into the cabinet.     Hard-Water Drain Damage Repair in Ellicott City, MD

    Most municipal water across Maryland runs moderately hard, so scale slowly builds inside water heaters, faucet aerators and showerheads over the years. Scale grows inside pop-up bodies and on stopper rods until the linkage seizes. Once it locks, someone forces the lift rod, the pivot snaps, and the stopper ends up stuck down for good, and by then the scale has also narrowed the tailpiece enough to slow the drain. We replace the whole scaled assembly. Smooth PVC sheds buildup better.

    Push-Button Drain Installation in Ellicott City, MD

    Push-button drains get rid of the lift rod. They open and close with a press on the cap, and we check that the sink has an overflow before choosing a slotted body, since a slotted drain in a bowl without an overflow leaks out the slots. Cleaning is simpler with no linkage. Hair still collects, so pull the cap now and then.
