    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Ellicott City

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Many homes built from the 1980s through the early 2000s have one or the other. We can usually identify it from the pipe at the water heater, and full replacement makes the house easier to insure and sell.

    Drywall Patching and Restoration in Ellicott City, MD

    Repiping needs small openings in walls and ceilings to reach fixtures and pull pipe. Our crew cuts neat rectangles, covers nearby surfaces and patches each opening after the pressure test. We match the texture. We match knockdown, orange peel or smooth walls, so the patches are ready to prime and paint.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Ellicott City

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. That is often the first sign. Replacing galvanized with PEX or copper ends the rust, brings pressure back and stops the leaks that start at the threaded joints where galvanized is thinnest. We disconnect and abandon the old pipe where removing it would mean tearing out finished walls.

    Drain Line Repiping in Ellicott City

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Repeat clogs, sewer smells and several slow drains at once often mean the drain pipe itself is failing. We camera the lines first. Failing sections are replaced with PVC or ABS sloped and vented to code, which drains faster and resists buildup much better than the pipe it replaces.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Ellicott City, MD

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Flexible PEX soaks up some of the pressure spike behind water hammer, and its long fitting-free runs leave fewer weak points. Attic PEX gets kept away from flues and out of UV.

    Commercial Repipe Services in Ellicott City

    Older commercial buildings in Ellicott City often have galvanized risers, copper that has pinholed or plastic pipe that no longer meets code.     Slab Leak Reroutes in Ellicott City

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab, which costs far less than a repipe and avoids breaking the floor. This works well for a first leak. After multiple leaks, we will show you why a full repipe may cost less over the next ten years.
