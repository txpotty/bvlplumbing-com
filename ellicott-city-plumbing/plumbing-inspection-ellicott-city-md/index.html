    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Ellicott City

    Most municipal water across Maryland runs moderately hard, so scale slowly builds inside water heaters, faucet aerators and shower valves over the years.     Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Inspection in Ellicott City

    A standard plumbing inspection in Ellicott City, MD usually costs $150 to $350. Size changes the price.     You can do a basic check by looking under sinks and watching your water meter. The tools make the difference. A professional inspection adds sewer camera video, pressure readings, leak tracing and code knowledge, which together catch problems that a visual check simply cannot see.

    A general home inspector checks basic function, while a plumber goes deeper with sewer cameras, pressure tests and pipe identification. Plenty of buyers use both.

    Every one to two years works for most homes. Some houses need it yearly. If your home has original supply pipe, large trees near the sewer or a history of leaks, a yearly inspection catches problems while they are still small and cheap to fix.

    No permit is needed for an inspection. Repairs may need one. Permits come into play for repairs, replacements or new installations that follow from the inspection, and if any of those apply, we will tell you up front and handle the paperwork if we do the work.

    Plan on one to two hours for most residential inspections. Big homes and long sewers add time.

    Looking for drips and watching the meter is worth doing on your own. It has limits. A professional inspection uses cameras, pressure gauges and leak detection tools to check the parts you cannot see, including the sewer line and the pipe inside walls, which is where the expensive problems usually hide.

    High water bills, repeat clogs, slow drains and discolored water are common signs. Banging pipes count too.
