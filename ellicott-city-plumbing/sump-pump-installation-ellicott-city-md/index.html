    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Ellicott City

    Sump pump installation in Ellicott City, MD runs about $800 to $2,500 for most homes. The biggest swing comes from whether we are swapping a pump in an existing pit or cutting a new pit through the slab, which adds concrete work and several hours of labor. A battery backup raises the total. You get a written price after we look at the space.

    A submersible sits in the pit under water, so it runs quieter and takes no floor space. A pedestal pump keeps the motor on a column above the pit. Both work. We help you pick based on your basement.

    Our trucks carry Zoeller, Liberty, Wayne and Flotec pumps. The right one depends on how far the water has to rise, how fast it comes in and what you want to spend.

    Submersibles are quieter and stronger, so they suit heavy use. Pedestal pumps cost less and are easy to service, though they are louder.

    Swapping a pump in an existing pit takes about two to three hours. A new pit can take a full day.

    Discharge should end at least ten feet from the foundation, on ground that slopes away. Plenty of cities ban discharge into the sanitary sewer.

    Odd noises, rust, constant cycling or a pump that fails to start are the common signs. Any pump past seven years is worth a closer look, even if it seems fine, because the switch is usually the first part to go. We can test it.

    Most standard installs take three to six hours. If we have to cut the slab, dig a new pit and patch the concrete, plan on most of a day, with the patch curing overnight. Swaps go faster.
