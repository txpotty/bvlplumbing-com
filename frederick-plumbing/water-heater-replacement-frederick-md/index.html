    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Frederick, MD

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Frederick, MD homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Every joint gets checked.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Frederick, MD

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. The space runs cooler.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Frederick

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is a popular move, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. You see both numbers.
