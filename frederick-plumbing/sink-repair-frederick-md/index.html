    What We Fix Under the Sink in Frederick, MD

    Garbage Disposal Repair in Frederick

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is usually jammed. From below we turn the flywheel with a 1/4 inch hex key, hit the reset button and look for a bent impeller. Leaks are a different story. A cracked housing means a new disposal, usually an InSinkErator or a Waste King.

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in Frederick

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. It usually means worn O-rings on a swivel spout or a failed base gasket, and on some faucets it means the body has cracked and the whole faucet needs replacing. Every faucet type rebuilds differently. We identify yours before ordering parts.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Frederick

    Check the slip nuts first. Overtightened plastic slip nuts crack, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until each joint weeps. The whole trap gets replaced as a kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Frederick, MD

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We look past the wall with a longer cable or a camera for a sag or for buildup where the sink line meets the tub. Now and then it is a plugged vent. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    Cracked or Loose Basin Repair in Frederick

    Undermount clips let go more often than people think. Failed epoxy lets the bowl sag a little, the caulk splits, and counter water runs right into the cabinet. We reset the bowl on new clips. Hairline cracks in porcelain near the drain mean replacement.

    Office and Restaurant Sink Repair in Frederick

    Office breakroom sinks get abused in ways a home sink never sees. Coffee grounds and paper towels go down the drain all day, and the trap behind the cabinet door may not get looked at until it overflows onto the carpet. We clear it and add a strainer. For restaurants, we keep three-bowl sinks running between the lunch and dinner rush.

    After-Hours Sink Leak Repair in Frederick, MD

    Leaks do not keep office hours. A supply line that bursts at night can put several gallons a minute on the floor, so we answer after hours and send a plumber with the parts to stop it. While you wait, shut off the valves under the sink. If those will not hold, use the main.

    Hair Clog Clearing in Frederick

    Hair is the bathroom sink's main problem. First the stopper comes out and gets cleaned, then a hand auger goes through the trap and wall arm, with no chemicals. The overflow channel gets flushed too. That slot smells when it fills with slime.
