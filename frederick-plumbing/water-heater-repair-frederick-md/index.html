    Not every leak means a dead tank. Relief valve, drain valve and supply line drips are cheap fixes. Water from the tank body or bottom seam usually means the lining has failed, and replacement is the only real fix. We protect your floors either way.

    Tankless Unit Repair in Frederick

    Navien, Rinnai, Noritz and Rheem tankless units show error codes on the front panel that help us narrow the problem quickly. Common causes are scale on the heat exchanger, a dirty inlet filter, a failed flow sensor or a weak igniter. Descaling uses a small pump and approved solution. Most municipal water across Maryland runs moderately hard, so scale slowly builds inside water heaters, faucet aerators and showerheads over the years.

    Gas Burner and Venting Repair in Frederick, MD

    Combustion is the part of a gas heater that matters most for safety. Yellow flame tips or soot at the draft hood mean the burner is not getting enough air to burn cleanly. The burner gets cleaned, and draft gets tested at the hood. Backdrafting is dangerous.

    Relief Valve and Fitting Leak Repair in Frederick, MD

    T&P valves drip when the tank is overheating or the house pressure spikes, so replacing the valve alone may not stop it. We check the thermostat setting and the house pressure first. A missing expansion tank is a common cause on homes with a check valve or pressure regulator. Loose fittings get tightened or replaced.

    Water Heater Brands We Service in Frederick

    Electric Tank Repair in Frederick, MD

    Four parts can fail on an electric tank, two elements and two thermostats. Hard water piles scale on the lower element until it burns out. After testing resistance and voltage, we replace what failed, reset the high limit and watch it heat up. A breaker that keeps tripping gets traced first.

    Electric Water Heater Element Repair in Frederick

    Lukewarm water or hot water that runs out in five minutes usually means a burned-out element or a stuck thermostat. Each element gets an ohm reading. Readings tell us whether an element is open or shorted, which explains the breaker trips. Common elements and thermostats are stocked.

    Scale is the main enemy of a tankless heater. Mineral buildup on the heat exchanger triggers overheating codes and shutdowns. A descaling flush, a clean inlet screen and a check of the igniter and flow sensor cover most tankless calls. All major brands.
