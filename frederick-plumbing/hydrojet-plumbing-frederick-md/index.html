    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Frederick

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. Cabling only cuts a narrow channel. A spinning nozzle removes the grease all around the pipe so it flushes away cleanly.

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. A root-cutting nozzle spins through the mass. The jet then flushes the debris to the main, and the camera marks the joints that let the roots in.
