    Propane Gas Line Service in Frederick, MD

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. Our work covers propane yard lines, regulator replacement and fitting repairs, plus a check that every appliance has its propane orifices installed. A natural gas burner running on propane will burn rich and soot up. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Frederick, MD

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. Many tankless heaters are rated at 150,000 to 199,000 BTU, which can be more than double what an old tank heater used, so the line feeding it often has to be upsized back toward the meter. We calculate the full load first. Then we size the new piping so every appliance in the house still gets full pressure at the same time.

    Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in Frederick

    Adding a gas fireplace, moving a laundry room or switching a range from electric to gas usually means extending the gas line. Sizing comes first. After that comes routing, the tie-in and the test.

    Gas Leak Detection in Frederick

    A rotten egg smell, hissing near a line, dead grass in a stripe across the yard or a gas meter that moves with everything off are the common leak signs. Trust your nose.     CSST Repair and Bonding in Frederick, MD

    Newer homes often have corrugated stainless steel tubing running through the attic. We repair damaged sections with manufacturer-approved fittings and verify bonding before we leave.
