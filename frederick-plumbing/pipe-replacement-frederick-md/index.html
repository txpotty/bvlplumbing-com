    What Goes Into a Repipe in Frederick, MD

    Whole-Home Re-Piping in Frederick

    A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture, including the water heater connections. Plan on a few days for a typical house. We run new trunk lines through the attic or crawlspace, branch down to fixtures through small wall openings, with service restored each evening when possible. Then patching.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Frederick, MD

    Removing every piece is rarely needed. It is disconnected and capped, while new lines run beside it, which keeps drywall damage low. Only accessible pipe comes out.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair and Replacement in Frederick

    Water under a slab shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a sound of running water, or a meter that spins with everything off. We locate the leak first. Then you have a choice between breaking the slab to fix that spot or rerouting the line through walls or the attic, and rerouting often costs less and avoids future slab leaks on that line. We explain both.

    Pipe Replacement for Multi-Unit Buildings in Frederick

    Apartments and condos share risers. One leak can affect several units. We plan riser replacement in phases, one stack at a time, notify tenants ahead of shutoffs, and test each section before moving on. Organized work.

    Copper Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Frederick, MD

    Older homes sometimes have copper that is thin from decades of use. Small leaks appear in random spots. We replace it with new Type L copper or PEX, and fixing awkward runs from past remodels. Better flow.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Frederick

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout. Pipes in exterior walls, unheated crawlspaces and garages are the most common trouble spots when a deep cold snap settles in. It often survives freezing where copper would crack.
