    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Frederick, MD House

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Frederick

    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. A second blower failure on an older tank is usually the point where a new heater makes more sense. We confirm a dedicated outlet first.

    Commercial Gas Tank Replacement in Frederick

    Laundromats and multi-unit buildings work commercial tanks so hard that replacement comes on a predictable schedule rather than as a surprise. We plan the swap around your slow hours. Some jobs need added vent or gas work.

    Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in Frederick, MD

    Big families with soaking tubs often outgrow a 50 gallon tank and need a 75 gallon heater or a high-recovery 50 at around 65,000 to 75,000 BTU. A bigger burner needs more gas. We check the meter and pipe size first, because a starved burner never reaches its rated recovery.

    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in Frederick

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon atmospheric tank is what most homes have, and swapping one like for like is a two to three hour job when the venting and gas line are in good shape. We drain the old tank, cap the gas, and set the new heater on a stand or pan. Every install gets new gas and water connectors. We soap-test the gas joints and check the draft hood with the burner lit.

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Frederick

    Bradford White tanks are sold only through plumbing wholesalers, not big-box stores, and plenty of Maryland plumbers install them for that reason. Replacement parts are easy to find. Installing to their specs keeps the tank warranty intact.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Service in Frederick, MD

    Higher BTU inputs on commercial units mean bigger gas lines and larger vent sizes, plus a permit and inspection in most jurisdictions. We handle all of it. Downtime stays short.

    No Hot Water Calls in Frederick

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. Those get tested before anything else. On a tank past ten years, a new heater usually beats a new valve.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Service in Frederick, MD

    Rheem tanks and their Ruud twins share most parts, which helps when a gas valve fails a few years in. Either one works. Setup follows the factory manual, including the draft test.
