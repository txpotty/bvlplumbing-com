    Types of Toilet Installation in Frederick, MD

    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Frederick, MD

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. Cast iron flanges rust through at the bolt slots. Plastic flanges crack from overtightened bolts, and a stainless repair ring screwed over the old one usually gives a solid anchor again. Seal goes on last.

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Frederick

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. A flange belongs flush with or just above finished flooring. Set it too early and the tile setter has to cut around it, which leaves the flange low and forces a thick seal or an extender later on. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in Frederick, MD

    New tile over old vinyl can raise a bathroom floor by half an inch or more. That leaves the old flange sitting low. We bring the flange up with a stacking extender or replace it outright, and if you are moving the toilet on a slab, that means cutting concrete to reroute the drain and patching it afterward. Moving it on a wood floor is easier.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Frederick, MD

    Wall-hung toilets need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep, or a furred-out chase built in front of an existing 2 by 4 wall. The floor underneath stays open. Cleaning gets easier, and the bowl height can be set anywhere within the carrier range, useful for a tall family. Service happens through the flush plate.

    Commercial Restroom Toilets in Frederick, MD

    Restaurants and offices get heavy-duty fixtures and accessible stalls built to code. Downtime costs a business money. Work gets scheduled before opening or after close, with fixtures staged on site, so each stall is out of service only briefly. We work with your GC on inspections.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Frederick, MD

    Comfort height bowls stand about two inches taller than standard ones. Consider who uses the bathroom first. Shorter adults and kids sometimes find the taller seat leaves their feet dangling, while anyone with bad knees or a hip replacement usually finds it much easier to sit and stand. Installation is the same as a standard bowl.

    Commercial Toilet Installation in Frederick, MD

    Commercial restrooms take a beating, so we install vitreous china bowls with flushometer valves rather than tank toilets. Flushometers want a 1 inch supply line and steady pressure. Before setting fixtures in an office or shop, we check that the building supply can deliver the flow the valve is rated for, often 20 to 25 gallons per minute at the fixture. Fixture counts follow code.

    Rough-In Toilet Installation in Frederick, MD

    Rough-in is the plumbing that goes in before walls and floors close up. For a toilet that means a 3 inch drain with a vent, plus a cold supply stub. We set the drain 12 inches from the framed wall plus the thickness of drywall, so the finished rough-in lands right where the fixture expects it. Then the inspector sees it open.
