    What Goes Wrong on a Disposal, and How We Fix It in Bel Air South, MD

    Leak Detection and Repair in Bel Air South, MD

    There are four usual leak points on a disposal: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Most leak points can be repaired. When the housing leaks, the unit has to go.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Bel Air South

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A constant high whine means something else. That whine often comes from worn motor bearings, and once bearings start to fail the noise only gets worse until the motor seizes. Odors usually trace to the splash guard. We pull it, clean the chamber walls and replace the guard if it is torn.

    Grinding Noise Repair in Bel Air South

    Loud metal-on-metal sounds may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove any debris, then run the unit empty to hear whether the motor itself is making the noise. Bad bearings mean replacement.

    Leaking Disposal Fix in Bel Air South, MD

    When the drip starts at the top, the putty under the sink flange is usually dried out. We drop the unit, pull the flange, clean off the old putty and reset it with fresh putty and new gaskets, then hang the disposal back up. Most top leaks stop there. Side leaks are usually the discharge gasket or the dishwasher hose clamp.

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Bel Air South

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps wedge between the impeller lugs and the grind ring, and the motor stalls. We shut off power, insert a 1/4 inch hex key into the socket on the bottom of the unit and work it back and forth until the flywheel turns freely. Next we pull the object with pliers. Long stalls can overheat the motor, so we run it under load before we finish.

    Emergency Disposal Repair in Bel Air South, MD

    If water is reaching the floor, that comes first. Turn the disposal off, stop the dishwasher if it is running, and close the angle stops under the sink so the faucet cannot add more water to a backed up bowl. Then call us. Most times we stop the leak that day and either fix the disposal or price a new one on the spot.

    InSinkErator Disposal Repair in Bel Air South, MD

    The InSinkErator lineup runs from Badger units to the Evolution series, and we repair both. Badgers are simple single-stage units, while Evolution models have multi-stage grinding and, on some models, a separate stage that can jam on its own. Common parts are on board. A failed motor means a new unit.
