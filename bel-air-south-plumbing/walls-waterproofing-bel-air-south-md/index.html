    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in Bel Air South, MD

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Bel Air South, MD

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Homes built after the war usually have copper supply lines, while newer construction typically uses PEX and plastic drain piping. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. A rigid coating on soft brick traps moisture and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with a new interior drain. We repoint failed mortar joints before sealing.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Bel Air South, MD

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst. We swap them for braided stainless hoses, put a drain pan under the washer piped to a floor drain where one is available, and install a quarter-turn shutoff or an automatic leak valve on the supply box. Water heaters get a pan too. The wall behind them can take a moisture-resistant board and sealer.

    Commercial Wall Waterproofing in Bel Air South

    Restaurant kitchens, gym locker rooms and salon wash stations put water on walls all day. We waterproof commercial showers, mop sink walls and restroom wet walls with membranes listed for continuous wet use, and we repair the supply and drain piping behind them when we find a leak during demo. We can work after hours.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Bel Air South, MD

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized to the water coming in, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. Without one, the discharge water runs back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed at least 10 feet from the house.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Bel Air South

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, cement board with a membrane or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. We reinforce corners, niches and valve penetrations with fabric or preformed pieces.

    Cost-Effective Waterproofing Solutions in Bel Air South, MD

    Not every wet wall needs a $15,000 excavation. Sometimes the fix is a downspout extension and regrading the soil so it slopes away from the house, six inches of drop over the first ten feet, and that costs a few hundred dollars. We start with the cheapest fix that will actually work. Every option comes written out with its price.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Bel Air South, MD

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Water heaters work hardest during the cold months, when incoming water temperatures drop and household demand for hot water climbs. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. Most homeowners notice the musty smell is gone within a few weeks. The weep holes empty into interior drain tile when there is one.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Bel Air South, MD

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and run it to a sump basin so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. The difference is that the water no longer ends up on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and makes sense when a patio or driveway sits against the foundation.
