    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Bel Air South, MD

    Kitchen Sink Repair in Bel Air South

    Kitchen sinks take the most abuse in the house. Strainers lose their putty seal, sprayer hoses split inside the cabinet, and disposal flanges vibrate loose over the years. The wet part is the one we fix. A cracked composite or porcelain bowl is a replacement, and we will say so.

    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Bel Air South

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather.     Office and Restaurant Sink Repair in Bel Air South

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Bel Air South, MD

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. In a warm cabinet that core can fail after five to ten years and burst with no warning, and the multi-turn stop above it often will not close when you need it. Lines and stops get replaced together. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Bel Air South

    Check the slip nuts first. Plastic slip nuts crack when they are overtightened with pliers, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until water seeps out at every joint along the trap. We replace the trap as one kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Repair or Replace? Sink Costs in Bel Air South, MD

    Repair is cheaper nine times out of ten. Replace the sink only if the bowl is cracked, the glaze is chipped through, or the drain has rusted into the bowl. When it is close, you get both prices. Some people repair now and remodel next year.
