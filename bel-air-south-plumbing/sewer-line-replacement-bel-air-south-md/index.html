    Upgrading Old Sewer Pipe Materials in Bel Air South

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all fail in their own ways. Roots get into clay joints, cast iron rusts out along the invert, and Orangeburg fiber pipe squashes flat over the years. Modern plastic pipe avoids all three problems. HDPE is fused into one continuous piece with no joints for roots to find.

    What Sewer Replacement Costs in Bel Air South, MD

    Four things set the price: length, depth, access and method. Open trench through an open lawn is usually the cheapest per foot, while bursting costs more per foot but can save thousands in concrete and landscaping repair. Driveways change the math fast. After the camera inspection you get a written price for each method that will work.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement in Bel Air South, MD

    Two holes instead of one long trench. With bursting, a steel head splits the old pipe into the soil and pulls new HDPE into the same path. The other option is a cured-in-place liner. Lining puts a resin tube inside the old pipe and cures it hard, but only if the host pipe is still mostly round and has no major sags.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Bel Air South

    No replacement quote without video. The camera goes in at the cleanout and travels out to the main, recording every crack, offset and root ball as it goes. A sonde in the camera head lets us mark the depth on the lawn. That one step decides between a spot repair and a full run.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Bel Air South

    Cast iron rots from the inside out. The bottom of a cast iron pipe goes first, and the camera will show a trench rusted through even when the top looks fine. Rough scale catches everything. Outside the foundation we replace it with PVC or HDPE. Under-slab cast iron gets a separate look at lining or rerouting.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Replacement in Bel Air South, MD

    Orangeburg is tar-soaked wood fiber. It was installed in a lot of homes in the decades after the war, and over time it softens, blisters and squashes into an oval that catches everything going by. Clay holds up better, except at the joints. Several bad spots on one run usually make full replacement the better buy.

    Sewer Pipe Materials We Install in Bel Air South, MD

    PVC covers most of our trenched work. SDR 35 is the common sewer grade for buried laterals, Schedule 40 shows up where code or the inspector wants a heavier wall, and HDPE is our pick for bursting. Basements are common in much of the state, while homes on the flat coastal plain more often sit on crawlspaces or slabs because of a high water table. Cast iron still gets used indoors on some jobs. We will go over which one fits your depth, soil and budget.
