    Pelican Water Softener Installation in Bel Air South, MD

    Pelican sells both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners, often direct to homeowners. When a homeowner has already bought one, we handle the plumbing, the bypass and the startup flush the manual requires. Skipping the flush causes trouble. We follow it step by step.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Bel Air South, MD

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. Their parts are easy to find and the valves can be rebuilt instead of replaced, so the system lasts. We program yours to your water. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Water Softener Replacement in Bel Air South, MD

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. While the old system is out, we inspect the loop and the drain, then install a newer metered unit. Metered valves save salt. Performance comes back right away.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Bel Air South

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. A new metered unit often uses far less salt and water than an older model on a timer, which shows up on both bills. The savings add up. Several models are available.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Bel Air South, MD

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. BVL Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. They work best on very hard water.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Bel Air South, MD

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter placed ahead of the softener removes those chemicals and protects the resin, since chlorine slowly breaks resin beads down over the years. You will taste it. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Bel Air South

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. We follow the Ecodyne manual for the bypass, drain and overflow connections, then program hardness and regeneration settings for your water. Bring your own unit. We set it up for you.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Bel Air South

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Little upkeep is needed. They work best on moderate water.
