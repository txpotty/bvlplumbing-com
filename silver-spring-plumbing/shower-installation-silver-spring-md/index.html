    Most shower installations in Silver Spring, MD fall between $1,000 and $5,000. Simple tub-shower combos come in low, and tiled walk-ins with upgraded valves and drains come in high. Plumbing changes drive the spread. We quote after a site visit.

    Mistakes in shower plumbing end up hidden in walls and floors. A licensed plumber is the safer choice, particularly when permits apply.

    Center drains and linear drains cover most showers. Center drains need four-way slope, and linear drains allow a single slope that works well with big tile. Tile size often decides.

    Labor and materials are both covered. Our workmanship warranty is spelled out in your agreement, and the fixtures have their own maker's warranties. Keep the paperwork.

    A pressure-balancing valve watches pressure. When someone flushes a toilet and the cold side drops, the valve cuts hot flow to match, so the mix stays close to where you set it. A thermostatic valve watches temperature itself. It suits showers with several outlets.

    Pressure-balance valves keep the hot and cold ratio steady when pressure changes. Thermostatic valves control the actual temperature and allow separate volume control, which works well for multi-head showers.

    Both stop scalding, but they work differently. Pressure-balancing valves keep the ratio steady, and thermostatic valves hold a set temperature regardless of pressure. Thermostatic costs more.
