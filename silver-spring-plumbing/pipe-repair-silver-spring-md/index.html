    Frozen and Burst Line Repair in Silver Spring

    Pipes in exterior walls, attics and crawlspaces freeze first. Frozen water cracks it. The split usually does not leak until the ice thaws, which is why many burst pipes are found hours after the cold snap. We repair the split and add insulation. Heat tape is an option on problem runs.

    PEX and Copper Pipe Repair in Silver Spring, MD

    PEX and copper fail in different ways. Copper gets pinholes. Copper thins from the inside and develops pinholes, while PEX usually fails at fittings or where it was kinked during installation, and our plumbers carry soldering, press and crimp tools so they can repair either one with the right connection. Each repair is pressure tested.

    PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in Silver Spring, MD

    Plastic supply pipe like PEX and CPVC is common in homes built over the last few decades. Each fails its own way. PEX can fail at crimp rings, older brass fittings or where it was exposed to UV light in an attic, while CPVC turns brittle with age and heat and cracks when bumped, so we replace failed fittings and damaged sections with parts matched to each material. Brittle CPVC needs a gentle hand.

    Galvanized Pipe Repair in Silver Spring

    Threaded steel pipe rusts from the inside until flow drops and threaded joints start to leak. It can be patched. The trouble is that every fitting you disturb on old galvanized can start a new leak, so we replace failing sections with PEX or copper using dielectric fittings and talk through a full replacement plan when the rest is close behind. The schedule is yours.

    Pipe Repair vs Repipe Cost in Silver Spring, MD

    A single pipe repair costs far less than a whole-house repipe. Spot fixes suit healthy systems. If you are seeing repeated leaks, low pressure or rusty water, the total cost of patching can pass the cost of repiping within a few years, which is why we price both and give you our honest take. The decision is yours.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair in Silver Spring, MD

    Slab leaks start when pipe under the concrete wears through or gets stressed by soil movement. Soils in Maryland range from heavy clay in the central counties to sandy, low-lying ground near the shore, and each affects buried lines differently. A hot spot underfoot is common. We isolate the leaking line with pressure tests and find it with acoustic and thermal equipment, and you pick between a targeted floor repair and a reroute overhead. We quote each way.

    Underground Pipe Repair in Silver Spring

    Buried pipe takes stress from settling soil, roots and vehicles. You rarely see them. We locate underground leaks with acoustic equipment and tracer gas, and either spot repair at the leak or replace the line by pulling new pipe through. Every repair gets a pressure test.

    PEX Pipe Repair and Installation in Silver Spring

    PEX is flexible and handles freezing better than rigid pipe. It still fails. Most PEX leaks happen at a crimp ring that was not fully seated, an early brass fitting that dezincified or a section damaged by sunlight in an attic, and we cut out the bad fitting and replace it with a crimp, clamp or expansion connection rated for the pipe. Usually a quick fix.
