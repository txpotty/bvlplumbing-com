    Water Heater Flush and Inspection in Silver Spring

    Popping or rumbling means sediment. Hard water drops minerals that settle on the bottom of the tank, where they trap heat, waste fuel and make that crackling sound every time the burner or elements fire. Flushing clears it.     Hidden Leak Checks in Silver Spring, MD

    A leak you cannot see still costs money. We shut off every fixture and watch the meter's leak indicator, then use acoustic and thermal tools to track hidden leaks in walls, under slabs and in buried lines. Most leaks we find are small and cheap to fix.

    Hot Water Tank Service in Silver Spring, MD

    Gas and electric tanks need different checks. On gas units we look at the burner flame, the flue draft and the gas connection for leaks, and on electric units we test the elements and the thermostats and check the wiring for heat damage. Both get flushed. Scale on the tank floor wastes energy and shortens tank life.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Silver Spring
