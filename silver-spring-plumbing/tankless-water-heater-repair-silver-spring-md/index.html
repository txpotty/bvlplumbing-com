    What Our Tankless Techs Fix in Silver Spring, MD

    Ignition Failure Repair in Silver Spring, MD

    No ignition codes have four usual causes: a dirty flame rod, a bad igniter, low gas pressure or a blocked intake. Undersized gas pipe is the one people miss. A tankless burner can need 150,000 to 199,000 BTU, and the half-inch line that fed a 40,000 BTU tank often cannot carry that, which is why it fires at low flow and quits when two showers are on.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Repair in Silver Spring, MD

    Whole-house electric tankless units pull a lot of current, often 100 to 150 amps on two or three dedicated breakers. When a breaker trips under load, the cause can be a shorted element or a loose, scorched lug, and both get tested with a meter before anything is replaced. Element resistance and supply voltage get measured. Scorched wire gets cut back.

    Igniter & Flame Rod Repair in Silver Spring, MD

    Gas tankless heaters light with a hot surface or spark igniter, then prove flame with a flame rod. An oxidized flame rod gives a weak microamp reading and the board shuts the gas valve after a few seconds. We clean or replace the rod, check the igniter for cracks or weak spark, and confirm the gas valve opens on call. Five-minute fix sometimes.

    No Hot Water Tankless Repair in Silver Spring

    No hot water at all usually points to ignition, gas supply or a heat exchanger choked with scale. We start with the error history in the service menu, then test the igniter, flame rod and gas pressure in that order, because a weak flame signal and low pressure can look identical on the display. We look at the vent run as well. Common igniters and flame rods ride on the truck.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Repair in Silver Spring

    Navien condensing units use a secondary stainless exchanger to recover heat from the flue gas. That makes condensate, and a plugged trap or a full neutralizer will shut the heater down with a drain or flue error. We clear the trap, check the PVC vent pitch and test the circulation pump on NPE-A models with built-in recirculation. Then we fire it at low and high flow.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Repair in Silver Spring

    Pressure at full fire matters on a gas tankless burner, not just at the moment it lights. We put a manometer on the inlet test port and run every hot tap in the house, because pressure that drops below the manufacturer's minimum under load causes flame loss and cold slugs. Then we check the exhaust. Codes come off when the numbers are right.

    Navien Condensate and Fan Repair in Silver Spring

    Navien's E016 is a heat exchanger overheat, and it usually traces to scale or a backed-up condensate line. We also read fan speed from the service menu and compare it to spec, since a fan that cannot reach target RPM will cause flame and exhaust faults on the same unit. We clean the trap and screen, then retest.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnostics in Silver Spring, MD

    Every tankless brand flashes its own codes, and the same number can mean different things on two units. A Rinnai 11 is no ignition, a Navien E003 is ignition failure, and a Noritz 11 is also an ignition fault, but the tests behind them differ. We read the code, pull the history and test the part tied to it. Guessing gets expensive.
