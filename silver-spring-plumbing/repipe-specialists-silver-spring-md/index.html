    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Silver Spring, MD

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Brown water after the house sits overnight, weak flow at upstairs showers and leaks at threaded joints are the usual signs that galvanized is near the end. We replace it with PEX or copper. Owners usually notice stronger flow the first day.

    PEX Repiping in Silver Spring, MD

Our PEX-A installs use expansion fittings that do not shrink the flow path. Flow stays strong at the last shower.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Repipe Specialists in Silver Spring

    Most PEX repipes for Maryland homes cost $5,000 to $12,000, depending on size, bathrooms and access. Copper typically adds 20 to 40 percent.

    Most municipal water across Maryland runs moderately hard, so scale slowly builds inside water heaters, faucet aerators and showerheads over the years. Hard water deposits calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe over time. Flow drops as the bore narrows. In metal pipe, scale also speeds up corrosion, so repiping with PEX-A and adding a softener or filter during the job can protect the new plumbing and your fixtures for years.

    Both work well, and the best choice depends on your house and your water. PEX is cheaper. PEX is flexible, handles a freeze better and resists scale, while copper is rigid, tolerates sunlight and has a long history, but it costs more and can pinhole in aggressive water.

    Whole-home repipes require a permit and inspection in most Maryland jurisdictions. Our office handles the permit and inspections.

    For copper, we use domestic Type L from mills such as Mueller and Cerro. For PEX, we use Uponor PEX-A most often. Viega and Zurn PEX show up on some jobs, but we keep one brand throughout each house so the warranty holds.

    We commonly use Uponor PEX-A with ProPEX fittings and Type L copper from domestic mills. We also use Viega ProPress fittings for copper work.

    PEX resists scale and pinhole corrosion and handles expansion well, which makes it a strong choice for hard water. Copper is durable but costs more and can pinhole in aggressive water.

    Most repipes in Silver Spring, MD take three to five working days for an average house. Big houses take more time. Copper takes a little longer than PEX because every turn needs a fitting, and the drywall patching usually adds a final day after the plumbing passes inspection.
