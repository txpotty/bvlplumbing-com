    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. We install duplex systems with alternating pumps. Downtime costs money. We plan the install around your business hours.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Silver Spring, MD

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. We pull the old pump and inspect the pit. Undersized pumps get upsized. Testing is done with a full pit.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Silver Spring, MD

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. A battery backup pump sits beside the primary and runs on its own float. Most run five to seven hours on a full charge. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Silver Spring

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. That saves some labor. We set them in homes that want two layers without a bigger pit.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Silver Spring, MD

Swapping a pump in an existing pit is the cheapest job, while a new pit means saw cutting, digging, gravel and a concrete patch, which roughly doubles the labor. Battery backups and alarms add to the total. We list each option on a written quote.
