    Waterproofing Costs vs. Repeat Water Damage in Silver Spring, MD

    Paying once beats paying every two years.     Shower Wall Waterproofing in Silver Spring

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, cement board with a membrane or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. We reinforce corners, niches and valve penetrations with fabric or preformed pieces.

    Commercial Wall Waterproofing in Silver Spring

    Restaurant kitchens, gym locker rooms and salon wash stations put water on walls all day. We waterproof commercial showers, mop sink walls and restroom wet walls with membranes listed for continuous wet use, and we repair the supply and drain piping behind them if demolition turns up a leak. After-hours scheduling is available.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Silver Spring, MD

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the waterline and check it a day later. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a clamping drain tightened onto the liner. We flood test the new pan before any tile goes down.

    Cost-Effective Waterproofing Solutions in Silver Spring, MD

    Not every wet wall needs a $15,000 excavation.     Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Silver Spring, MD

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst. Water heaters get a pan too. The wall behind them can take a moisture-resistant board and sealer.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Silver Spring

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles.     Crawl Space Encapsulation in Silver Spring

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. Drier air under the floor also cuts down on the musty smell in the house.

    Schluter Kerdi, Custom RedGard and Laticrete Hydro Ban cover most of our showers. Sheet membranes like Kerdi suit tight schedules because there is nothing to cure, while liquids like RedGard and Hydro Ban go on in two coats and need to dry between them. We follow each maker's steps exactly.

    Look low on the wall first. Damp patches, a musty smell, mold on the bottom of the drywall, a white powder on block or brick called efflorescence, and puddles after rain all point to water coming through. Stair-step cracks and bowing walls also deserve a closer look.
