    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Silver Spring

    Homes built after the war usually have copper supply lines, while newer construction relies on PEX and plastic drain piping.     Gas Water Heater Replacement in Silver Spring, MD

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Silver Spring, MD homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Every joint gets checked.

    Conventional Tank Water Heater Replacement in Silver Spring

    Standard tank heaters are still in most homes, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment. A leak at the base, loud popping or rusty hot water means it is time, and a like-for-like or resized swap usually takes two to four hours. You shower tonight.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Silver Spring

    Electric water heaters in Silver Spring usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. Elements are cheap to swap. Tank leaks are the end, so we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to your existing circuit. Hot water is back the same day.

    Water Heater Replacement Cost Comparison in Silver Spring, MD

    A standard tank replacement is usually the most affordable option in Silver Spring, MD. We price all three.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Silver Spring, MD

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.
