    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Silver Spring, MD House

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Upgrades in Silver Spring, MD

    Condensing gas tanks run around 90 percent efficient or better and vent through PVC, while a standard atmospheric tank sits closer to 60 percent. They cost more and need a condensate drain and an outlet. Big households see payback sooner.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Silver Spring, MD

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. We never swap a natural gas tank onto propane without the right conversion. We confirm pressure with a manometer.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Replacement in Silver Spring

    Atmospheric vent heaters last 10 to 12 years on average, and the end usually shows up as rusty hot water or a slow drip from the bottom. We disconnect the old unit and reconnect the new one to the existing gas line and flue. Combustion air matters. A tank in a tight closet needs louvered doors or a ducted air supply, and we check that before lighting it.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Replacement in Silver Spring, MD

    Commercial gas heaters in restaurants and offices often run 75 to 100 gallons at 75,000 BTU or more, and they need a gas line sized for that load. Commercial code is tighter. We size by peak demand and recovery, not tank volume alone.

    Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in Silver Spring, MD

    Big families with soaking tubs often outgrow a 50 gallon tank and need a 75 gallon heater or a high-recovery 50 at around 65,000 to 75,000 BTU. A bigger burner needs more gas. We check the meter and pipe size first, because a starved burner never reaches its rated recovery.

    Gas Water Heater Efficiency Ratings in Silver Spring

    Efficiency ratings on tank heaters are listed as UEF, uniform energy factor, and a higher number means less gas per gallon heated. Basic tanks land near 0.60 UEF. Condensing models top 0.80, and some qualify for utility rebates where those are offered.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Replacement in Silver Spring, MD

    Rheem Performance and Performance Platinum tanks cover most of the range we install, from basic 6-year models to 12-year tanks with better anodes. The longer warranty usually means a bigger anode. Choosing is simple.

    No Hot Water Calls in Silver Spring

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. Those get tested before anything else. On a tank past ten years, a new heater usually beats a new valve.
