    Why Choose BVL Plumbing for Grease Trap Cleaning in Silver Spring, MD?

    Skipped pump-outs usually announce themselves at the floor drain first, with gray water pooling around the grate after the dish machine drains. The smell shows up next. A rancid, sour odor means the grease cap has started to break down and give off hydrogen sulfide. The same gas corrodes concrete, which shortens the life of an in-ground interceptor.

    • Flexible Scheduling, We work before you open or after close, so the kitchen keeps cooking.
    • Extended Lifespan, Less hydrogen sulfide in the tank means less corrosion on concrete and baffles.
    • Prevent Backups, Pumping on schedule keeps grease from leaving the trap and hardening in the lateral.

    Citywide Coverage

    Hospitals and hotels cook around the clock, so there is no closed window. That is fine with us. We set the time with facilities, pump during the quietest hour, and rope off the lid area until the cover is back and locked.

    Experienced Team

    Each technician carries a sludge judge and writes down real depths. The depth never gets eyeballed. With those readings we can say, based on numbers instead of a hunch, whether your schedule is too tight or too loose.

    Safety-First Approach

    An open interceptor hatch in a parking lot is a serious fall hazard. We set cones before lifting it. The lid stays off only as long as the hose is in the tank, and before we leave we check that it is seated flat and, on locking lids, bolted down.

    Residential Grease Trap Cleaning in Silver Spring, MD

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Cleaning in Silver Spring

    Gravity interceptors in the parking lot often hold 750 to 2,000 gallons across two or three compartments. We pump each compartment on its own.     Commercial Grease Trap Pumping in Silver Spring

    Fryer-heavy menus load a trap fastest. Two weeks is enough for a wings place to fill a 50-gallon trap. The trucks have the hose length for traps behind walk-ins or in basements, plus tank room for interceptors over 1,000 gallons. We pump every one to the floor.

    Under-Sink Grease Trap Maintenance in Silver Spring, MD

    Under-sink hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, usually 10 to 50 gallons per minute, and they hold far less than people expect. Busy kitchens need them cleaned every week or two. The service covers grease and solids removal, a baffle scrape, a look at the inlet flow control fitting, and a new lid gasket if the old one is flattened. A bad gasket is why most of these smell.
