    Burst Pipe Repair and Replacement in Towson, MD

    Disconnecting garden hoses and shutting off outdoor spigots before the first hard freeze prevents many burst-pipe calls each winter. Frozen pipes split when water inside expands.     Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Towson

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, which can cause insurance headaches. Replace it.
