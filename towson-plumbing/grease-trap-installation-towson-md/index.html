    What Goes Into a Grease Trap Install in Towson, MD

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in Towson, MD

    Hydromechanical traps use baffles and a vented flow control to pull grease out of water moving at a set rate, usually 20 to 100 gallons per minute. You can put one under a sink, beside a dish station or in a pit in the slab. The flow control is not optional. Skip it and a full sink drain overruns the rating, which sends grease right past the baffles. We look for units certified to PDI G-101 or ASME A112.14.3.

    In-Ground Gravity Interceptor Installation in Towson, MD

    Buffets, grocery delis and kitchens doing several hundred covers a night usually end up with an in-ground gravity interceptor of 1,000 gallons or more. We excavate, set the tank level on compacted gravel, and tie in the inlet and outlet. Basements are common in much of the state, while homes on the flat coastal plain more often sit on crawlspaces or slabs because of a high water table. We plan the dig and the backfill around what the soil report and the open hole actually show us. Risers bring the lids to grade.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Towson

    Inside a hydromechanical interceptor, baffles do the separating while a flow control fitting with an air intake holds incoming water to the rated flow. The air intake has to be vented to code, not left open in the kitchen. Get that wrong and the trap siphons. We also set the unit level within a quarter inch, since a tilted trap short-circuits the baffles and loses capacity.

    Gravity Grease Trap Installation in Towson

    A gravity interceptor works on time, not machinery. Wastewater slows down in the tank long enough for grease to float and solids to settle. We size by fixture count and retention time, then match the tank to what the local pretreatment office for your building will accept. No moving parts to fail.

    Food Truck Commissary and Ghost Kitchen Traps in Towson, MD

    Commissary kitchens see a dozen trucks dumping wash water in the same two hours, which is a different load from a restaurant spreading it over a long shift. We size for that spike. Ghost kitchens with four or five brands under one roof often share a single trap, so the fixture count can be higher than the floor plan suggests. Each brand's prep sink counts.

    Grease Trap Brands We Install in Towson

    Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura cover most of what we set in commercial kitchens, plus precast concrete tanks for the big outdoor interceptors. Each brand has its own lid gasket and baffle layout. Parts availability matters. We lean toward units with parts a local supply house keeps on the shelf, so a cracked lid does not close you for a week.

    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Towson

    Flow rating is the number that matters on a hydromechanical trap, and it has to match the fastest-draining fixture combination in your kitchen. A 50 GPM trap on a line that sees 75 GPM will pass grease every lunch rush. We do the math on paper. Each sink compartment's volume, the drain size and a one or two minute drain time give us the GPM figure the code uses.

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Installation in Towson

    Outdoor gravity interceptors run from 750 gallons to well past 2,000, and setting one means an excavator on site and a traffic-rated lid if it sits in the drive lane. Before anything ships, we check that the tank and covers carry an H-20 traffic rating where trucks will roll over them. Most go in over two to four days. The inlet invert has to sit a couple of inches above the outlet, and we shoot both with a laser level before backfill.
