    Commercial Sump Pump Maintenance in Towson, MD

    Commercial pits are often bigger and deeper, and many have two pumps on an alternating controller. We test both pumps, the alternator and the high-water alarm. Property managers get a written log of every reading we take, which helps at insurance renewal and during code inspections. Quarterly visits are common.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Towson

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. A backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or quits. Our plumber load tests the battery, tests the charger and alarm, then runs the backup alone with the primary unplugged. Most batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Cost in Towson, MD

    A new sump pump, installed, often runs $400 to $1,200 depending on horsepower, pit work and whether you add a battery backup. Routine maintenance costs a small fraction of that. Most pumps last seven to ten years with care. We will tell you plainly whether yours has years left or is ready to retire.

    Elevator Pit and Garage Pumps in Towson

    Elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms all collect water that has nowhere to go by gravity, so a pump has to lift it out. Elevator pit pumps often need oil-detecting controls so hydraulic fluid never gets pumped out with the water. We test duplex systems and high-water alarms on a set schedule. Logs go to the property manager.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Towson, MD

    Backup pumps sit in the pit next to the main pump and run off a 12-volt battery when the power drops or the primary fails. Our plumber load tests the battery instead of just reading its voltage. Then we unplug the main pump and let the backup empty the pit on its own. Alarms get tested too.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Towson

    A working sump pump and a backwater valve are worthwhile protection for any home with a finished basement. We test the pump a few weeks before the wet season by filling the pit with a hose until the float trips. We time the drawdown and listen for bearing noise. That leaves time to replace a weak pump before the first big storm, not during it.

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Towson

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. Our plumber pulls the unit out so we can see the cord and the shaft seal. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. We also clean the intake screen and check the housing for corrosion, which shows up fast on pumps that sit in standing water for years.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in Towson, MD

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the water, so the wear shows up in the shaft, the bearings and the float rod. We check the rod for bends and the stops for slipping. Bearings get a listen. A motor that whines or rattles on startup is usually close to done.
