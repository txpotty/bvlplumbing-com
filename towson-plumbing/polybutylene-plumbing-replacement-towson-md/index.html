    Why Polybutylene Fails in Towson

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Most municipal water across Maryland runs moderately hard, so scale slowly builds inside water heaters, faucet aerators and showerheads over the years. Heat makes it worse. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.

    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Towson

    Most poly replacements we do use PEX-A, a flexible tubing that runs long distances with few fittings. For slab houses, new PEX-A runs through the attic and drops down interior walls, so none of it sits under the floor. It also tolerates freezing better. Expansion fittings keep the bore full size, which helps pressure at the last fixture on the line.
