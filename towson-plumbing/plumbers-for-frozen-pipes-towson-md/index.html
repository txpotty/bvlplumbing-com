    Costs range from about $200 for simple thawing to $1,500 or more for a burst pipe inside a finished wall. Where the pipe sits matters most.     Check which fixtures work. Ice is usually between a working fixture and a dead one. A dead sink on an outside wall usually means ice in that wall, which a thermal camera can confirm.

    Thawing and small pipe repairs usually do not need a permit. Bigger work might. Repiping a section, rerouting a line into a heated wall, or replacing a backflow preventer may require a permit in many jurisdictions, and we pull it when needed so the work is on record.

    A short, exposed section usually thaws in 30 to 60 minutes. Pipes inside walls take more time. Lines in walls or plastic pipe can take a few hours with gentler heat.
