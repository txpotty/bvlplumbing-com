    Why Choose BVL Plumbing for PEX Pipe Installation in Towson, MD?

    Copper splits when the water in it freezes solid, and PEX usually swells and survives instead. Pipes in exterior walls, unheated crawlspaces and garages are the most common trouble spots when a deep cold snap settles in. It still needs protection. Exterior wall runs get insulated, crawlspace runs get sleeved, and we route around foundation vents and other cold drafts any time the framing allows.

    • Freeze Tolerance, PEX stretches as ice forms and usually holds where copper would crack.
    • Chlorine Rated, Listed PEX is rated for treated municipal water at hot-water temperatures.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Towson, MD

    Price on a repipe depends on a short list: bathroom count, square footage, how many stories, and how easy the attic and walls are to work in. PEX almost always comes in below copper because the material is cheaper and a crew can run a house in two to four days instead of a week. Patching is sometimes quoted on its own line. Ask and we will price all three, PEX-A, PEX-B and copper, side by side.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Towson, MD

    Gray polybutylene went into millions of homes from the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. Chlorine in treated water attacks the plastic and the acetal fittings from the inside, so it can look fine from outside right up to the day a joint lets go. Many insurers ask whether you have it. Swapping it for PEX can help with insurance and with resale.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Towson, MD

    Opening a wall for a kitchen remodel is the cheapest time to replace old supply lines. While the studs are exposed we can run PEX back to a manifold or a sound trunk, set fresh stub-outs for the sink and dishwasher, and add a fridge line. Additions get their own sizing. A new bath at the far end of the house may need a three-quarter inch feed to keep pressure up.

    Commercial PEX Pipe Solutions in Towson, MD

    Apartment buildings, small offices and churches in Towson, MD can benefit from PEX as much as houses can. With a manifold and shutoff for each unit, a leak in one apartment only takes that apartment offline, not the whole riser. Fire-rated walls need care. Every penetration through a fire-rated wall gets a listed firestop sleeve or sealant.

    PEX Main Water Line Installation in Towson

    The main from the meter to the house feeds every fixture you own. A leaking old service line shows up as a soggy patch in the yard, a higher water bill and weak pressure everywhere. New PEX goes in its own trench. At the house we add a new full-port ball valve for the main shutoff.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Towson

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. It is a five-second check. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.

    Leak Repair with PEX in Towson, MD

    A single bad section is not always a reason to repipe. We can cut out a split or corroded stretch of copper, transition to PEX with a listed fitting, and have the water back on in an hour or two. That works for one leak. Once you are on the third patch, we will tell you straight whether the rest of the copper is heading the same way.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in Towson

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside out. After 40 or 50 years the bore is so choked with scale that a half-inch line delivers a trickle, and the water comes out tinted brown after it sits overnight. A full PEX repipe replaces all of it. Pressure comes back at the fixtures and the rust stops.
