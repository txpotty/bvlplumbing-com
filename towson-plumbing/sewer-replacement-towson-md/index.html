    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Towson

    Cast iron sewer pipe lasts a long time, though not indefinitely. Over decades it scales up inside and the bottom wears through. We replace it with PVC, and where cast iron remains under the house we use a shielded no-hub coupling at the transition. Clean seal.

    Emergency Sewer Replacement in Towson, MD

    Wastewater backing into the house is urgent. Call (443) 719-1650. We open the cleanout to relieve pressure, camera the line to find the break, and make a section repair when that is enough to get the house working, then sit down with you about whether the rest of the line needs replacing.

    Advanced Diagnostics for Sewer Issues in Towson, MD

    Diagnosis comes before price. Video tells us the problem and the sonde tells us the spot. We sometimes add a hydro-jet cleaning first, because a line full of grease can hide a crack, then camera again so we are looking at the pipe itself. Guesswork costs money.

    Emergency Sewer Line Replacement in Towson, MD

    Sewage coming up a floor drain or tub is an emergency. Stop running water in the house right away.     Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Towson, MD

    Roots find sewer lines by following moisture. A hairline crack is enough. Once inside, they grow into a mat that catches paper and grease, and cutting them out with a machine only buys a few months, because the opening is still there. New PVC with glued joints gives them no way in.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Towson

    Bursting works best on clay, cast iron and Orangeburg. A hydraulic rig at the receiving pit pulls the bursting head through the old line, splitting it into the soil, while a continuous length of fused HDPE follows behind so the new pipe has no joints at all between the pits. Roots cannot get into a joint that does not exist.

    Code-Compliant Sewer Replacement in Towson

    Code sets the minimum slope, the cleanout spacing and the pipe type. The inspector looks at fall, cleanouts and material before backfill. We set fall at a quarter inch per foot unless the run requires otherwise, place cleanouts at the house and at major direction changes, and use pipe and fittings listed for underground drainage. Paperwork stays on file.

    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in Towson

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all failed for different reasons. Modern PVC does not rust, flatten or let roots in at glued joints. Schedule 40 or SDR 35 PVC is what most codes call for on a lateral, and we match the grade to the depth and load over the pipe. Glued joints stay sealed.
