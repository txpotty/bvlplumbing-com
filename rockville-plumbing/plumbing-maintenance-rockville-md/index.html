    Water Heater Maintenance in Rockville

    Sediment is the tank's slow killer. Most municipal water across Maryland runs moderately hard, so scale slowly builds inside water heaters, faucet aerators and showerheads over the years. We drain and flush the tank, pull and check the anode rod, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, and look over the gas or electric connections for corrosion and wear. Flushing takes about an hour. Less sediment means quicker heating and a longer tank life.

    Drain Cleaning as Maintenance in Rockville

    Slow drains rarely fix themselves. Camera checks and cleaning on problem lines keep grease and roots from causing a holiday backup. We jet greasy lines. Rooted lines get regular cutting.

    Repipe Assessments in Rockville, MD

    Polybutylene and old galvanized pipe leak more.     Sump Pump Testing in Rockville, MD

    Basements flood when the pump fails during a storm. Nor'easters, tropical storm remnants and summer downpours can overwhelm storm drains across Maryland and push water toward basements. We pour water into the pit to make sure the float lifts and the pump kicks on, check the discharge line for clogs or freezing risk, and inspect the check valve so water does not drain back in. Backup pumps get tested too.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Rockville

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger, especially with hard water, and the unit starts throwing error codes, running lukewarm or cutting out mid-shower long before it actually wears out. A small pump circulates descaler. The inlet filter, venting and gas line get checked too.
