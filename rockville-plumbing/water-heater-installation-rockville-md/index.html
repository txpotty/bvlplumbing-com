    Water Heater Installation Cost in Rockville, MD

    Up front, a tank heater costs less than tankless. Tankless jobs cost more mostly because of gas line and vent work, while attic installs, moves and code updates add labor to any type. Your quote lists permits. We compare operating costs so you can choose with real numbers.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Rockville

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. An attic heater needs a pan with its own drain line, a properly routed relief line and enough access to get the old tank out and the new one in. Ceilings are at stake. If moving it makes sense, we can relocate the heater.

    Commercial Water Heater Installation in Rockville

    Restaurants, salons and offices burn through hot water at rates no household tank can keep up with. We install commercial tanks over 100,000 BTU and racked tankless systems, with the venting and gas supply those loads require. We schedule around your hours. Installs can happen after close.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Rockville, MD

    Water on the garage floor and no hot water usually means the tank has rusted through. Shut the cold supply valve on top of the tank and turn the gas knob to pilot or flip the breaker, then call us. We carry common sizes. Most emergency swaps finish the same day.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Rockville

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. We install a new flex connector with a sediment trap, check vent pitch and draft and test every joint with a gas leak detector. Venting is what keeps CO out. We follow code on all of it.

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Rockville

    Each manufacturer publishes its own install rules, and they are not identical. Clearances, maximum vent lengths and relief valve rules differ by brand, and warranty claims get denied when an installer skipped them. We follow them exactly. That protects your coverage.

    Major Brand Water Heater Installation in Rockville, MD

    Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz are the brands we install most. Each has its own install manual, and following it to the letter is what keeps the tank warranty valid if something fails in year six. We know those manuals well. We help you compare models on warranty length and recovery rate.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Rockville

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. We size the tank by first-hour rating, not just gallons, because that number tells you how much hot water it delivers in a busy morning. Gas or electric both work.
