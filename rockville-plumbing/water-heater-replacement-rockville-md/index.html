    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Rockville

    Electric water heaters in Rockville usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. Elements are cheap to swap. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Hot water is back the same day.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Rockville, MD

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Rockville, MD homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Then we leak test.

    Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith in Rockville, MD

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith on most tank jobs. Bradford White is trade-only and durable, Rheem offers range and parts, and AO Smith brings plenty of sizes, so the pick comes down to fit, warranty and stock. All three hold up.

    Businesses in Rockville run their water heaters harder than homes do, so commercial replacements need more capacity and faster recovery.     Rheem and Ruud Water Heater Replacement in Rockville, MD
