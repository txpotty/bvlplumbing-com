    Typical Plumbing Emergencies in Rockville, MD

    Emergency Sump Pump Service in Rockville

    A basement pump that quits halfway through a heavy rain may let inches of water into the basement in an hour. Common causes are a stuck float switch, a burned-out motor or a check valve that let go. We check the float, motor and check valve and swaps the pump if needed. Then we trace the discharge line outside. A frozen or crushed discharge pipe sends water right back in.

    Gas Line Leak Repair in Rockville

    Rotten-egg smell near the range or a hiss by the water heater is your signal to get everyone outside. Do not flip switches. From the yard, call 911 or the gas utility, then call us once they clear the scene, and our certified plumber will find the leak with a combustible gas detector, replace the bad section and pressure test the line to code before any appliance gets relit.

    Emergency Slab Leak Response in Rockville, MD

    The sound of running water with everything off is a textbook slab leak symptom. So is a hot spot on the floor. We isolate the hot or cold side first, which keeps some water running for the night, then locate the leak with acoustic and thermal gear. Repairs include a direct cut through the slab, a reroute through the walls or attic, or a partial repipe of that branch.
