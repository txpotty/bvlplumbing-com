    How We Install Showers in Rockville, MD

    Shower Stall Installation in Rockville

    A one-piece stall needs a level, fully supported base. We set fiberglass and acrylic bases in a bed of mortar or setting compound so the floor of the unit does not flex underfoot, since flex is what cracks a base within a couple of years. Then the drain goes in. We check the flow at the head once the trim is on.

    Budget Shower Upgrades in Rockville, MD

    Budget showers can still be built right. A fiberglass or acrylic unit with a quality pressure-balance valve costs far less than a tiled shower, and when it is set on a mortar bed with a sealed drain, it lasts decades. Spend on the valve. You get the full price before we start.

    Custom Shower System Installation in Rockville, MD

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. A standard pressure-balance valve can feed one head, but rain heads, body jets and handhelds need a thermostatic valve with separate volume controls and supply lines sized up to 3/4 inch. We plan the layout first. The water heater gets checked for capacity too.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Rockville, MD

    Prefab units are the fastest route to a new shower. The base goes on mortar, the drain and valve get connected and the walls are fastened so they stay rigid. Good for rentals and guest baths. Most installs finish in a day.

    Emergency Shower Replacement in Rockville

    A cracked pan or a failed valve can force a replacement fast. When water is getting under the floor or into a ceiling below, we pull the damaged unit, dry what we can and install a standard stall so the bathroom is usable again within a day or two. Custom work can wait. We check the framing before the new unit goes in.

    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Rockville, MD

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. We install pressure-balancing, thermostatic and diverter valves, set to the depth your trim kit needs and plumbed with integral stops when the valve has them. Trim goes on after tile. Waiting keeps the trim clean during tiling.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Rockville

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator within the maker's distance limits, plumb its supply and drain and run the steam line to the head inside the enclosure. An electrician runs the circuit. The enclosure needs a sealed door and a sloped ceiling.

    Commercial Shower Installation in Rockville, MD

    Locker rooms need showers that take hundreds of uses a day. Commercial installs get tamper-resistant trim and heads, plus drains sized for heavy simultaneous use. We work around your business. A master mixing valve can protect the whole room from scalding.
