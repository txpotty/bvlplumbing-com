    Septic Pump and Alarm Repair in Rockville

    Pumped systems move effluent uphill or to a far field, and the pump sits in its own chamber with a float tree and an alarm. An alarm going off means the water rose past the high-level float. Our tech meters the pump and tests each float before replacing anything. Zoeller and Liberty parts ride on the truck.

    After-Hours Septic Backup Calls in Rockville

    Late-night backups usually start in the lowest drain in the house, often a basement floor drain or a first-floor shower. Our on-call tech carries a jetter and spare floats, so most nights end with water flowing again. Bad nights end with a pump truck. Either way, you know what failed before we leave.

    Drain Field Repair in Rockville, MD

    Green stripes over the trenches and a wet lawn in dry weather are the classic signs of a field in trouble. The cause is usually biomat, a black layer of slime that seals the soil. Compaction and hydraulic overload from a running toilet can do it too. Testing always comes first. Resting or jetting can bring a field back.

    Leach Field Restoration in Rockville, MD

    Before we condemn a leach field, we open the distribution box and look at how evenly it splits the flow. A box that has settled or cracked can push all the effluent into a single trench. Leveling or replacing the box often brings the field back. Some fields are past saving. For those, we explain the design and permit steps.

    Septic Alarm Troubleshooting in Rockville

    A septic alarm sounding is the system's way of saying the water is too high in the pump chamber or the aerobic unit. Hit the mute switch and go easy on the water. We check whether the pump runs and whether a float is hung up on its cord. A tripped breaker is the cheapest answer.

    Septic Line Clog Clearing in Rockville

    A slow toilet and a gurgle in the tub usually point to a clog between the house and the tank. We run a camera down the line first, then clear it with a jetter sized for 4-inch sewer pipe. Grease and wipes cause most of these, and what we do next depends on what comes out of the pipe. Roots mean a joint has opened.

    Camera Inspection of Septic Baffles in Rockville, MD

    Some tanks have no riser, so we run a push camera through the small inspection port to see the baffles. That saves digging up the whole lid. If the outlet tee is cracked or missing, we dig at that end only, then cut in a new PVC tee and an effluent filter housing. Concrete lids get a riser while they are open.

    Emergency Septic Overflow in Rockville

    Raw sewage coming up through a tub drain carries bacteria you do not want on your skin or your floors. Keep everyone out of the area and shut off water use. We find the blockage and get flow moving, sometimes with a jetter and sometimes with a pump truck. Cleanup guidance comes with it.
