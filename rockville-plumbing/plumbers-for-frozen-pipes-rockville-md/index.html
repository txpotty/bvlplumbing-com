    Frozen Water Line Diagnosis in Rockville, MD

    Finding the frozen spot is the hard part. A thermal camera speeds it up. Fixture checks plus a thermal scan usually narrow the ice to a few feet of pipe.

    Burst Pipe Repair in Rockville, MD

    Water expands as it freezes, and that pressure can split copper, crack PVC fittings and pop PEX off a crimp ring. The split section comes out. Where one split appears, others often follow along the same run, so we pressure test the full branch and replace any section that bulged or thinned instead of patching only the obvious leak.

    Heat Cable for Exposed Pipes in Rockville

    Some pipes cannot be rerouted or insulated enough on their own, like a supply line under a manufactured home or a pipe through an unheated porch. Heat cable handles those. Old-style constant-wattage heat tape can overheat and is a known fire risk, so we install self-regulating cable listed for the pipe material and replace worn or brittle heat tape we find.

    Same-Day Frozen Pipe Thawing in Rockville

    Time matters once a line freezes, because the longer ice sits, the more pressure builds behind it. Crack open the faucet it serves. Most frozen lines flow again within an hour of arrival, and every one gets a pressure test.

    Thawing Drain Lines and Sewer Pipes in Rockville

    Drain lines can freeze too, mostly in crawlspaces and outside walls. You will see the backup first. We thaw drains with a steam or hot-water jetter fed through the cleanout, then check the slope, because a drain that holds water in a sag is the kind that freezes again next time.
