    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Rockville, MD

    One slow drain is a branch clog. More than one usually means the main. Water rising in the shower when you flush is a strong sign the clog is past the point where those two lines join, so the pattern of slow drains tells us where to go in. Starting in the right place matters.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Rockville, MD

    Jetting sends water at thousands of psi through a nozzle that sprays backward and drives itself down the pipe. It scrubs the whole wall. A cable punches a hole through a grease plug, and a jetter clears the coating that starts the next clog. The camera goes in before the jetter. Badly cracked pipe gets a gentler approach.

    Hydro-Jetting for Stubborn Clogs in Rockville

    Some lines get cabled three times a year and still clog. Cabling alone is not enough. Those lines are usually coated in grease or scale, and a cable just cuts a small hole that closes back up. Jetting removes the coating down to the pipe wall. It lasts much longer.

Ongoing root problems may call for a treatment schedule or lining.
