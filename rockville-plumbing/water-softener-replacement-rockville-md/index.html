    Picking the Right Softener for Rockville, MD Water

    Replacing Pelican Water Systems in Rockville

    Pelican systems usually combine a carbon filter with either a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner in one setup on the main line. Depending on how old it is, we swap media or the full unit. Installed to spec, the warranty stays valid.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Rockville, MD

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. They do keep scale from sticking inside pipes and water heaters. They need no drain line. We can replace one or switch you to a true softener.

    Upsizing a Whole-Home Softener in Rockville

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. A 32,000-grain unit that regenerated weekly for a family of three may be running dry every three days now. We size for the house you have today.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Rockville

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. Old clock-timer units come out and metered valves go in. Salt use usually drops. The brine setting gets matched to your hardness so the unit is not wasting salt with every cycle.

    Salt-Free Alternatives in Rockville, MD

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. In those cases a TAC conditioner works, though we are upfront that it stops scale and does not make water feel soft. Low-sodium diets are another reason.

    Softener Loop Installation in Rockville, MD

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. Homes without one get a loop cut in, with shutoffs and a bypass. Hose bibs stay off it. The drain goes to a laundry box or floor drain with an air gap.

    Commercial Water Softener Replacement in Rockville

    Restaurants and laundromats run softeners hard, and a commercial unit often needs a 1.5-inch or 2-inch valve and twin tanks to keep up with flow. We size it to peak gallons per minute. Installs happen before opening or after close.

    Softener Failure Diagnosis in Rockville, MD

    A clogged injector, a dead motor or a wrong setting can make a good softener look finished. Hardness gets tested on both sides of the unit, and the cheap fixes get tried before we talk replacement. Obsolete valves are another story.

    Nearby Water Softener Replacement in Rockville, MD

    Local water varies. Hardness can be very different between a city supply and a private well a few miles out, and iron or chlorine changes what else the system needs. The system matches your test, not a guess.

    Experience with Maryland water supplies matters because hardness and iron levels decide both the resin size and whether a prefilter belongs ahead of the softener. BVL Plumbing plumbers in Rockville test for both. Programming usually happens the day of install.
