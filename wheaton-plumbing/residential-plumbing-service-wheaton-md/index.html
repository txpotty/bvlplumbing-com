    Water Softener and Filter Service in Wheaton

    Most municipal water across Maryland runs moderately hard, so scale slowly builds inside water heaters, faucet aerators and showerheads over the years. Scale shows up first as white crust on faucet aerators and a rumbling water heater. We put in and maintain whole-house softeners and carbon filters, including Kinetico and Pelican units, sized from a hardness test we run at your kitchen sink, not a guess. Then we set the regeneration schedule.

    Faucet, Toilet and Shower Valve Repair in Wheaton, MD

    Hard water chews through faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. Shower valves get rebuilt, cartridges replaced and tub spout leaks sealed, and if a fixture is too corroded to save, we install the replacement and haul the old one away so it is not sitting in your garage. A slow drip still adds up on the bill.

    Drain Cleaning and Main Line Clogs in Wheaton

    Slow drains in more than one fixture point to the main line. A single slow sink is usually a trap or branch clog, but when the tub gurgles as the washer drains, the blockage is further out and needs a machine cable or a jetter run from the cleanout. Older clay and cast iron lines often have roots.

    Sump Pumps and Basement Drainage in Wheaton

    A sump pump only gets tested when it rains hard. We install and replace cast iron submersible pumps, check float switches and replace worn check valves, and we can add a battery backup so the pit keeps draining when the power goes out in a storm. You can test yours with a five-gallon bucket of water.

    Leak Repair and Slab Leak Service in Wheaton

    On a slab foundation, a supply leak can run for weeks unseen. The usual clues are a warm patch of floor, a meter dial moving with everything off, or a sudden jump in the water bill. We pinpoint it before quoting. Repeat leaks usually point to a repipe instead of another patch.

    Slab Leak Location and Repair in Wheaton

    Slab leaks hide under concrete until something gives them away. Basements are common in much of the state, while homes on the flat coastal plain more often sit on crawlspaces or slabs because of a high water table. Our techs find them with ground microphones and thermal imaging, so the only concrete we break is the small patch right over the leak, not half the kitchen floor. After that, you choose the repair. Options include a spot repair through the slab or a reroute through the attic.

    Pipe Repair and Repiping for Older Homes in Wheaton, MD

    Galvanized steel and polybutylene are the two pipes we replace most. Homes built after the war usually have copper supply lines, while newer construction relies on PEX and plastic drain piping. We repipe with PEX or Type L copper, running new lines through the attic and walls with small access holes, and the water stays on at night for most of the job. A typical repipe takes two to four days.
