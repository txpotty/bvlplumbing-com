    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Wheaton, MD

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. Expect more motor noise than a submersible. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Wheaton

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. They fit pits where two separate pumps would crowd each other. That saves some labor. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Wheaton

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. We install duplex systems with alternating pumps. Downtime costs money. Work is scheduled around your operating hours.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Wheaton

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Wheaton, MD

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. We install their 250 and 280 series often. Replacement switches are easy to get. A small weep hole in the discharge pipe keeps the pump from air locking.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Wheaton, MD

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. Our trucks carry 1/3 and 1/2 HP submersibles, check valves and fittings, so in most homes we can pull the old pump and set a new one in one visit. Figure on two hours for a typical swap. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Wheaton, MD

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. A battery backup pump sits beside the primary and runs on its own float. Most run five to seven hours on a full charge. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Wheaton

    No pit yet? We saw-cut the slab at the low corner, break out the concrete and dig down through soil, hardpan or rock to set an 18 by 22 inch basin. We backfill around the basin with washed gravel so groundwater has a path in. The slab gets patched with new concrete. Last come the pump, the lid and the discharge line.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Wheaton

    Most Wheaton, MD sump pump jobs land between $600 and $1,800. That range covers a quality pump, a new check valve, the discharge fittings and the labor to set and test it in a pit that already exists. New pits cost more. We write it all down before work starts.

    Most last seven to ten years. Pumps that sit idle for long stretches can fail sooner, so test yours a few times a year.

    Zoeller, Little Giant, Wayne and Liberty make up most of what we install. Each of those brands has parts available locally, which matters when a switch fails on a Saturday and you need it fixed that weekend. We pick by pit size and lift.

    Test it every month by pouring a bucket of water into the pit. Once a year, scoop out silt, check the float for free movement and walk outside to make sure the discharge outlet is clear of leaves and ice. It is quick.

    Most homeowners are better off hiring it out. Mistakes in the wiring or the discharge can flood the basement. Water and electricity share the pit.

    Grinding, rattling or a pump that runs with no water in the pit are all warning signs. Visible rust, a float that sticks or a pump that cycles every minute during dry weather usually means the end is near. If the basement flooded anyway, it is time.

    The pump has its manufacturer warranty, and BVL Plumbing warranties the installation labor. We spell out the terms in your quote.

    Submersibles are quieter and stronger, so they suit heavy use. Pedestal pumps cost less and are easy to service, though they are louder.
