    Water Main Underground Repair in Wheaton

    Your water main is the pipe between the meter and the house. The homeowner side is yours. When it breaks, we locate the leak without digging, expose the pipe and cut out the bad section and join in new pipe with buried-rated fittings. Rusted steel lines rarely patch well.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Wheaton

    Polybutylene was used in a lot of homes built in the 1980s and early 1990s. It fails without warning. The gray plastic turns brittle as chlorine in the water attacks it, with failures most common at fittings and tight bends. Replacement is the only lasting fix. We use PEX or copper.

    HOA and Multifamily Underground Repair in Wheaton

    HOA common areas and apartment complexes have long buried runs under parking and sidewalks. Tenants still need to get in. We work with your schedule.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Wheaton, MD

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. The new line takes its place. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, while a continuous HDPE pipe is pulled in behind at the same diameter or bigger. It works on badly damaged lines. Lining cannot do that.

    Sewer Line Underground Repair in Wheaton, MD

    Roots and settling soil cause most buried sewer failures. Old pipe gives up. We offer open trench repair, pipe bursting and cured-in-place lining, and the camera footage decides which one fits the depth, the damage and what sits on top of the line. All work meets Maryland plumbing code.

    Commercial Underground Pipe Repair in Wheaton

    Commercial properties run larger and longer buried lines. Leaks get expensive. We locate leaks under parking lots, sidewalks and landscaped common areas carefully so the concrete cuts stay small and tenants are not shut out. We can work nights.

    Underground Pipe Repair Cost Comparison in Wheaton, MD
