    What Goes Into a PEX Install in Wheaton, MD

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Wheaton

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and it really depends on the house. PEX-A makes tighter bends, can be heat-fixed if it kinks, and its fittings flow better, while PEX-B is cheaper and its crimps hold just as well when each one is gauged. Both last decades. In a cramped attic, we usually pick PEX-A.

    Hydronic Heating with PEX in Wheaton

    Radiant floor loops are PEX too, but a different kind you drink from. Heating work calls for oxygen-barrier PEX, coated so oxygen cannot seep through the pipe wall and corrode the boiler, the circulator and the iron parts. Loops have length limits. Too long and the far end runs cold.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Wheaton

    Pinhole leaks in copper tend to come in clusters. Water chemistry, high flow velocity and contact with soil under a slab can all pit the pipe wall from the inside, and once a few pinholes appear, more are usually on the way. A water test is a smart first step around Wheaton before choosing a treatment system, because hardness and minerals vary from one supply to the next. A switch to PEX ends it. We use listed transition fittings anywhere PEX ties into copper that stays.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Wheaton, MD

    Gray polybutylene was installed widely from the late seventies into the mid nineties. Chlorine in treated water attacks the plastic and the acetal fittings from the inside, so it can look fine from outside right up to the day a joint lets go. Many insurers ask whether you have it. Swapping it for PEX can help with insurance and with resale.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Wheaton, MD

    Opening a wall for a kitchen remodel is the cheapest time to replace old supply lines. With the studs bare, we can run PEX back to a manifold or to the nearest good trunk, set new stub-outs for the sink and dishwasher, and add a line for the fridge ice maker. An addition gets sized on its own. A new bath at the far end of the house may need a three-quarter inch feed to keep pressure up.

    Leak Repair with PEX in Wheaton, MD

    A single bad section is not always a reason to repipe. We can cut out a split or corroded stretch of copper, transition to PEX with a listed fitting, and have the water back on in an hour or two. It is fine for an isolated leak. Once you are on the third patch, we will tell you straight whether the rest of the copper is heading the same way.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Wheaton, MD

    Think of a manifold like an electrical panel, but for water lines. A central header feeds each fixture on its own line with its own valve, which lets you shut off one toilet and leave the kitchen running. It uses more pipe. What you get back is very few hidden fittings and steadier pressure when two people shower at the same time.
