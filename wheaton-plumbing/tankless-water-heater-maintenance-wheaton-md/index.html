    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Wheaton, MD

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. Rinnai LC codes point to scale, Navien E003 is a failed ignition and Noritz 90 points at combustion air or exhaust trouble. First we read the code history. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.

    What Tankless Maintenance Saves in Wheaton

    Scale on an exchanger acts like insulation. A unit with a thick layer burns noticeably more gas for the same shower than a clean one, and the extra cost adds up month after month. You get the maintenance price beside the likely repair cost, and the decision is yours.

    Home Tankless Flushing in Wheaton

    Homes on private wells, especially in the low coastal counties, often deal with rusty mineral staining, odors or acidity that call for filtration or treatment. Inside a tankless heater, that water runs through a small copper or stainless coil while the burner fires, and every minute of hot water leaves a little calcium behind. A pump on the service valves pushes descaling solution through the coil for roughly an hour. The flow returns. Temperatures stop swinging, and the unit quits shutting down on high-limit.

    Annual Tankless Checkup in Wheaton, MD

    Condensate and Neutralizer Service in Wheaton

We clean the trap and look at the neutralizer media. Spent media gets replaced. An outdoor run of condensate line gets checked for support and protection.
