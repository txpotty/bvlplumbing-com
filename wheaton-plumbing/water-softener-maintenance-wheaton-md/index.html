    Control Valve and Timer Diagnostics in Wheaton, MD

    The control valve runs every stage of regeneration, from backwash through brine draw to the final refill. We advance it manually through each cycle and watch for a motor that stalls, a drive gear that skips, a piston that drags or a drain that never shuts off. Worn pistons turn up a lot.

    We test with a titration kit, counting drops until the sample changes color, because test strips are too rough for setting a valve. We take the raw sample from an unsoftened hose bib or with the softener on bypass. Softened water comes from the kitchen tap.
