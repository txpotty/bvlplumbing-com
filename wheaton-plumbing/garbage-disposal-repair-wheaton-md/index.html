    Leak Detection and Repair in Wheaton, MD

    There are four usual leak points on a disposal: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Three of the four are fixable. When the housing leaks, the unit has to go.

    Disposal Odor and Buildup Cleaning in Wheaton, MD

    Food buildup and grease on the chamber walls and under the splash guard are behind most disposal odors. Most municipal water across Maryland runs moderately hard, so scale slowly builds inside water heaters, faucet aerators and shower valves over the years. Scale gives that food something to stick to. Cleaning the guard and chamber, checking the drain and swapping a torn guard often solves a smell that lemon peels never fixed.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Wheaton

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A constant high whine means something else. A steady whine usually points to failing motor bearings, and the noise keeps building until the motor locks up. Most smells start at the splash guard. The guard comes out, the chamber gets cleaned and a torn guard gets replaced.

    Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in Wheaton

    A disposal that hums but will not grind is getting power but is stuck, while one that is silent has no power at all. They are different problems. We check the breaker, the reset button and the switch for silent units, and the flywheel and motor windings for humming ones, then decide if a repair makes sense.

    Noisy Disposal Repair in Wheaton

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough all point to something inside. Loose mounting rings rattle too. We check the mount first, then the chamber for debris, then listen to the motor with the chamber empty, because a motor that whines with nothing in it has bad bearings.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Wheaton, MD

    Leaks show up at the flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube or body. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. Water from the bottom usually means a failed internal seal, which makes replacement the smarter move. That seal cannot be replaced.
