    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Potomac

    Hard water builds scale on tankless heating elements, which cuts efficiency and can trigger error codes. Most municipal water across Maryland runs moderately hard, so scale slowly builds inside water heaters, faucet aerators and shower valves over the years. A softener or scale filter helps, and the service valves we install make yearly descaling simple. It is a short service.
