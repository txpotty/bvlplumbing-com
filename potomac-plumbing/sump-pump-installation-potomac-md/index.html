    Sump Pump Installs in Potomac, MD

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Potomac

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. Lids close flat over them. Each install gets a sealed lid and a grommet around the cord to keep out radon and odor.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Potomac, MD

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. The M53 and the M98 are the two we set most, depending on lift and inflow, and both come with mechanical float switches that hold up for years. Backup and combo units are available too. Pit size and lift decide which model we recommend.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Potomac

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Potomac, MD

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. Our trucks carry 1/3 and 1/2 HP submersibles, check valves and fittings, so in most homes we can pull the old pump and set a new one in one visit. Figure on two hours for a typical swap. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Potomac, MD

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. When we replace a pump we also check the basin for silt, look at the check valve flapper and run water through the discharge line to find any clogs. If the old one could not keep up, we size up. We fill the pit and watch it run.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Potomac, MD

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary runs on house power and handles the everyday inflow, while the battery unit waits above it on a separate float. People with carpet downstairs buy these. They are worth it anywhere a few inches of water would mean new drywall.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Potomac

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi models send a text too. Rentals need these. They can go on a new pump or one that is already working.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Potomac

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. For those areas we set an exterior basin with a submersible pump and run the discharge to a safe point, often a pop-up emitter in the yard. Lots that slope toward the house need this most. We keep the outlet away from your foundation and the neighbor line.
