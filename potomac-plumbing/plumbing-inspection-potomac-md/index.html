    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. Venting and gas connections get checked.     Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Potomac, MD

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. We test each valve. Stops and the main valve tend to seize with age, and gurgling drains can mean a vent problem, both of which are simple fixes once found.

    Rental and Commercial Property Inspections in Potomac

    Landlords and property managers use our inspections between tenants or before buying an investment property. We document each unit.
