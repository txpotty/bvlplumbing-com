    Tank or tankless, the failures repeat. A tank heater starts leaking at the bottom or rumbling from sediment, and tankless units throw error codes when scale builds up in the heat exchanger or the venting is wrong. We flush, descale and repair both types.

    Sump Pumps and Basement Drainage in Potomac

    A sump pump only gets tested when it rains hard. We install and replace cast iron submersible pumps, check float switches and replace worn check valves, and we can add a battery backup so the pit keeps draining when the power goes out in a storm. Pour a bucket in to test it.

    Water Heater Repair and Replacement in Potomac, MD

    Thermocouples, gas valves, elements and relief valves are the common water heater repairs.     Pressure Regulators and Shutoff Valves in Potomac, MD

    Water pressure over 80 psi wears out hoses and valves early. We test static pressure with a gauge on a hose bib, and if it reads high we install or replace a pressure reducing valve and set it in the 50 to 70 psi range most fixtures are rated for. We also replace main shutoffs that will not turn.

    Pipe Repair and Repiping in Potomac, MD

    Pinhole leaks and rust-colored water from the tap usually point to supply pipe near the end of its working life in an older house. We patch single leaks with a copper repair or PEX transition. Repeat leaks mean a repipe. Freeze splits get the same treatment, cut out and replaced with new pipe rated for the job.

    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Potomac

    Slab leaks hide under concrete until something gives them away. Basements are common in much of the state, while homes on the low coastal plain more often sit on crawlspaces or slabs because of a high water table. We locate them with acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera, so the only concrete we break is the small patch right over the leak, not half the kitchen floor. Then you pick the fix. Options include a spot repair through the slab or a reroute through the attic.
