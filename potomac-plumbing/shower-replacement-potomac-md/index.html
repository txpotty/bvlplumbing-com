    Replacing Showers Damaged by Hard Water in Potomac, MD

    Most municipal water across Maryland runs moderately hard, so scale slowly builds inside water heaters, faucet aerators and shower valves over the years. Scale shows up first as a weak showerhead and a stiff handle. Over enough years, it can lock a cartridge in place, pit a valve body and etch glass doors past the point where cleaning helps, and that is when replacement makes sense. A softener helps the new one. We can recommend one.
