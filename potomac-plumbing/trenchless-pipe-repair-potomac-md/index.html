    CIPP Lining for Clay and Cast Iron in Potomac

    Older Potomac homes often have clay or cast iron sewer pipe. Both fail with age. Roots get into clay joints and cast iron rusts from inside, and a liner seals over both with jointless pipe that should outlast the original. Only one or two access points are needed.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Potomac

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Potomac yard.

    Sectional Point Repair in Potomac

    Sometimes only one joint or a few feet of pipe is bad. That calls for a sectional liner. Guided by camera, a short liner is placed over the bad section and cured, so you pay for a few feet instead of the full line. It suits isolated damage in Potomac sewer lines.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Potomac, MD

    Pipe Bursting Services in Potomac, MD

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. The old pipe gets split. As a winch drags the head through, the old pipe fractures into the soil and new HDPE is pulled into the same path behind it. HDPE does not rust or leak at joints in Potomac, MD soil.
