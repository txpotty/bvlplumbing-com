    What Our Water Line Service Covers in Potomac, MD

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Potomac

    Trenchless work makes sense when the line runs under a driveway, patio or a row of mature shrubs. We dig a pit at the meter and another at the house. Then the new pipe gets pulled through underground. A pneumatic boring tool or a cable puller drags HDPE or PEX through the soil, or through the old pipe if it is a straight run, usually in one working day from start to backfill.

    Emergency Water Line Repair in Potomac, MD

    First thing on a burst line is shutting the water off, at the house valve if it works and at the curb stop if it does not. Free-running water washes soil out from under walks and slabs fast. BVL Plumbing sends a plumber with a curb key, a pump and repair couplings. We can usually stop it within the hour. If the repair will run long, we rig a temporary bypass so the house has water while we work.

    Water Pressure Restoration in Potomac

    Low pressure at every fixture points to the service line or the regulator, not the faucets. First step is a pressure gauge on the outside spigot. Then we check the pressure reducing valve, the main shutoff and the curb valve for anything half closed, and look at the pipe itself. Galvanized pipe can lose half its opening to rust scale. The fix might be a new valve or a new line.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Potomac, MD

    Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground. Homes on private wells, especially in the low coastal counties, often deal with rusty mineral staining, odors or acidity that call for filtration or treatment. Rust and scale close the pipe from the inside. Rusty water at the first tap in the morning is a classic sign, along with pressure that drops when two fixtures run at once. Replacing the full run with copper or PEX clears all of it up. Usually a one-day job.

    After-Hours Water Line Breaks in Potomac, MD

    Water lines tend to break on a Saturday night. Ours is a 24 hour line. BVL Plumbing sends a plumber who can shut off the curb stop, pump the hole and make a permanent repair on the spot, which saves you from a weekend on a temporary patch. If the break is on the utility side of the meter, we tell you right away and help you get the provider out. It is more common than most people expect.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Potomac

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Some older neighborhoods still have lead service lines, so replacing one is worth considering when other plumbing work is already underway. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. We pinpoint the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or skip the old under-slab pipe and repipe overhead with PEX. You get a price on every option.

    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Potomac

    Underground leaks hide well. Many people find out from the bill before they see anything in the yard. Others notice a patch of grass that grows greener than the rest, or ground that stays soft long after the sprinklers shut off. We confirm the leak at the meter, trace the pipe route and listen along it. The dig follows the sound.

    Main Water Line Replacement in Potomac, MD

    Replacement beats repair once a line has leaked two or three times, or when it is galvanized or polybutylene. Patches on those materials rarely hold. We run new Type K copper or PEX from the meter to the house, typically finishing in one day whether we trench or pull the pipe. We pull the permit, set up the inspection and pressure test the new line before it is covered.
