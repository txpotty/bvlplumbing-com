    Jet Pump System Repair in North Bethesda, MD

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Air leaks on the suction side cause most failures. BVL Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and confirms it builds pressure. Nonstop running without pressure usually means lost prime or an air leak.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in North Bethesda

    Bladder tanks hold a cushion of air that lets the pump rest between cycles. A failed bladder lets the tank waterlog, and the pump begins to short cycle. The bladder, the switch and the tank fittings all get checked. Proper sizing adds years to a pump.

    Control Box and Surge Repair in North Bethesda, MD

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. Low-lying waterfront areas also face tidal flooding, which can surcharge drains and affect septic systems near the shore. Surges from lightning are the usual cause of control box failures. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection, replace what failed and recommend a surge arrestor at the box if one is missing. An arrestor costs little.

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have built-in electronics that protect the motor and report faults. Most Grundfos calls involve a sensor or a drive fault. BVL Plumbing reads the fault code, checks the wiring and tests the pressure sensor before replacing anything in a North Bethesda, MD well. Electronics fail less often than people think.
