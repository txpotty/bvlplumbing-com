    Some softeners keep a little water in the brine tank by design. Excess standing water usually means a clogged injector, a kinked brine line or a stuck float valve.

    A clogged drain line or a failed control valve stops a lot of softeners. Salt bridges, worn-out resin, power outages that wipe the timer settings and mineral buildup inside the valve body cause most of the rest. Diagnosis pins it down.
