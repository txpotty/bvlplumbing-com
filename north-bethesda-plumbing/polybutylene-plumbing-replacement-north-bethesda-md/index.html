    Full Home Repiping with PEX in North Bethesda

    Most poly replacements we do use PEX-A, a flexible tubing that runs long distances with few fittings. On a slab home, we route the new lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, which keeps every supply line out of the concrete. PEX handles a freeze better than copper.     PEX Pipe Installation in North Bethesda, MD

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Labor runs lower with PEX.
