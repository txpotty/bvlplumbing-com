    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in North Bethesda

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. Crushed pipe cannot be lined. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. We see this most in older neighborhoods with clay pipe. We stage the work so you lose sewer service for a few hours, not days.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in North Bethesda, MD

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Paper and solids settle there. Clay that swells when wet and shrinks when dry can stress sewer laterals and water service lines over time. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and reset the slope to the quarter inch per foot most codes call for on residential laterals. A shallow belly sometimes just needs regular cleaning.

    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in North Bethesda, MD

    A spot repair is the cheapest fix when the rest of the line is sound. Lining costs more per foot. It avoids excavation, though, and on long runs under driveways or patios the savings on concrete and landscaping often make it the cheaper total job. Full replacement is right for Orangeburg or a line broken in several places. You get each option in writing with a number attached.

    Multifamily buildings and offices put several units' worth of flow through one lateral. Small defects turn into big backups.     Pipe Burst and Collapse Repair in North Bethesda, MD

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. Every drain backs up. Collapses usually come from corroded cast iron, crushed clay under a driveway or tar-paper pipe that has finally flattened, and the camera tells us exactly where the pipe gave way and how deep it sits. We dig at the mark and replace the broken length with PVC. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.

    Root Intrusion Removal in North Bethesda, MD

    Roots enter through hairline cracks and loose joints, then grow into a mat that catches paper and grease. They come back fast. We clear the line with a root cutting head or a jetter with a chain flail, camera it afterward to see the condition of each joint, and show you whether a liner or a spot repair would stop the cycle for good. Mature trees in North Bethesda yards make this a common call.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in North Bethesda

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. A leaking joint invited them in. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. Lining or a joint replacement keeps them from coming back.
