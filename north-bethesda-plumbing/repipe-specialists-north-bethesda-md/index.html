    Repiping for Hard Water in North Bethesda, MD

    Homes on private wells, especially in the low coastal counties, often deal with rusty mineral staining, odors or acidity that call for filtration or treatment. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. In hard water homes, PEX-A is often the better pick since it resists scale, and a softener loop or filter can be added during the job. Planning it now saves a second visit later.

    Type L Copper Repiping in North Bethesda

    Copper has been plumbing homes for generations, and done right it lasts a long time. We use Type L, not the thinner Type M, and we support it with the right hangers so it does not rub or rattle in the walls. All joints use lead-free solder. Copper can pinhole in aggressive water, so we ask about your water and any past leaks before recommending it over PEX.
