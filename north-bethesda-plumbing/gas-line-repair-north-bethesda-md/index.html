    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in North Bethesda

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. We replace them with new coated stainless connectors sized for the appliance and add an accessible shutoff valve if the old one is missing or seized. Usually under an hour per appliance.     Gas Appliance Connector Repair in North Bethesda, MD

    Weak flames or a smell near one appliance often point to the connector or shutoff behind it. Uncoated brass connectors from decades ago crack at the flare fittings and should be replaced on sight. We replace them with coated stainless connectors. Each appliance also gets a shutoff you can reach and a sediment trap where required, and we check that the connector length and diameter match the appliance rating.

    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in North Bethesda

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. We shut off every appliance and watch the meter's test dial, then close branch valves one at a time to find which section is leaking. An electronic detector does the rest. Soap solution at the suspect fitting confirms it with bubbles, and then we repair the joint, replace the section or swap the connector before testing again.

    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in North Bethesda

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. We back out the leaking joint, clean the threads, apply gas-rated thread sealant and reassemble it, or replace the fitting or valve if the threads are damaged. Every repair gets a soap test and a system pressure test before we leave.

    Emergency Gas Line Repair in North Bethesda

    If you smell gas, leave the building, skip the light switches and call your gas utility or 911 from outside. Then call us. After the gas company secures the meter, our plumber finds the leak, repairs it and runs the test the utility needs to see before turning gas back on. We relight pilots when service returns. The truck carries enough pipe and fittings to finish most emergency jobs in one trip.

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in North Bethesda, MD

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Years of vibration from a furnace or a house settling can loosen threaded fittings, and pipe in a damp garage or an exterior wall can rust from the outside in. Bad sections get replaced with new pipe. Rusted pipe that runs outside gets painted or replaced with coated steel so it does not come back as the same problem.

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in North Bethesda, MD

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. The new pipe gets sized so every burner in the house still has full pressure at once.
