    Picking a Tankless for Your North Bethesda, MD Home

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in North Bethesda

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. A small under-sink unit needs one 20 or 30 amp circuit and suits a distant bath or break room. Swaps take an hour or two.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Replacement in North Bethesda

    Rinnai units we pull are usually 12 to 18 years old, and the failure tends to be a leaking heat exchanger or a dead control board. A current Rinnai often mounts on the same bracket. Less drywall work that way. Venting stays the same, or switches to PVC if you move to a condensing unit.

    Gas Line Testing on Tankless Installs in North Bethesda, MD

    Every gas connection on a tankless gets a soap test and a manometer reading at full fire before we leave the house. A line that holds pressure at rest can still sag under full load. We test both.

    Tank to Tankless Conversion in North Bethesda, MD

    Converting from a tank to tankless takes a full day in most homes. The work usually includes a larger gas line, new venting through a wall or roof, and a new spot on the wall for the unit. We cap or remove the old flue.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in North Bethesda

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. Most need at least a 3/4-inch line. Pipe length and meter capacity get checked against the sizing tables, and we confirm pressure at full fire. An undersized line causes ignition faults.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in North Bethesda

    A cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board on an older tankless is usually not worth repairing past the ten-year mark. We match the new unit to your gas line, vent type and mounting spot whenever we can. Most swaps take four to six hours.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Replacement in North Bethesda

    Sizing a tankless starts with how many fixtures run at once and how cold the incoming water is, not the square footage of the house. Run two showers and the dishwasher together and you are near 8 GPM. That number picks the unit. Any gas, water or vent changes the new unit needs get done to current code.

    Navien Venting and Install in North Bethesda

    Navien allows 2-inch or 3-inch PVC venting on its condensing units, with maximum lengths that drop as you add elbows. Our vent runs stay within spec. Go past them and the warranty suffers.
