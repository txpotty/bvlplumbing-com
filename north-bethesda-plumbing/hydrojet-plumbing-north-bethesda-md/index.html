    Scheduled Jetting Maintenance in North Bethesda

    Properties with chronic drain issues benefit from scheduled jetting. Restaurants jet every three to six months. Homes with root-prone sewers usually jet every year or two, and we keep a camera record from each visit so you can see whether the roots are coming back slower, faster or the same.

    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in North Bethesda, MD

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. A jet scours the entire pipe wall. Snaking suits a one-time clog, while jetting fixes a line that clogs repeatedly.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in North Bethesda, MD

    Restaurant kitchen lines send warm grease downstream where it cools and sticks. By the time it reaches the interceptor, a lot of it has already coated the pipe. Short jetting passes keep loosened grease from plugging the line downstream. Quarterly works for most.

    Whole-House Drain Jetting in North Bethesda

    Slow drains in several rooms point to buildup in the main or a branch line. The main gets jetted from the cleanout, and branches from vents or fixtures. Most homes take two to three hours.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in North Bethesda

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. Cabling only cuts a narrow channel. Jetting with a spinning nozzle strips the grease from the full circumference so it flushes downstream instead of re-forming a plug at the next fitting.

    Chemical-Free Drain Cleaning in North Bethesda, MD

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Caustic cleaners can harm old pipe. The debris flushes away as normal waste, leaving nothing behind to harm pipe or septic bacteria.

    Storm and Yard Drain Jetting in North Bethesda, MD

    Yard drains, channel drains and downspout lines fill with sand, silt and leaves between storms. Low-lying waterfront areas also face tidal flooding, which can back up drains and affect septic systems near the shore. A clogged yard drain sends that water back toward the house, so we jet the lines from the outlet end and flush the catch basins before the wet season starts. We also check the pop-up emitters.

    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in North Bethesda

    Preventive jetting makes sense when the same line clogs more than once a year. Camera findings set the interval. A line with heavy grease might need jetting every six months, while a sewer with a few root entry points might only need it every 18 to 24 months, and the footage tells us which.
