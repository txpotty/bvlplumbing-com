    Jetting Versus Cabling in Dundalk, MD

    Cabling and jetting solve different problems. A sectional cable is built to chop and pull a clog out. Jetting strips grease and scale off the walls and carries it out of the line, so we cable first on a hard root mass and jet afterward when the line has years of buildup on it.

    Main Line Drain Cleaning in Dundalk

    Toilets bubbling while the washer drains? The main line is the likely spot. Every fixture shares that one pipe, so the lowest drain in the house is where water comes up, and the usual causes are roots, a crushed section of old pipe or a heap of wipes caught on a bad joint, which we find with a camera and clear with a sectional cable or a jetter.

    Hydro Jetting for Drains in Dundalk

    Jetting does what a cable cannot. A jetter sprays the full circumference of the pipe at high pressure. A cable punches a hole, while a jetter cleans the pipe back to near its original diameter. It works best on a kitchen or main line that keeps clogging every few months no matter how often it gets cabled.
