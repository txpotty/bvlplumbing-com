    Utility Sink Faucet Replacement in Dundalk

    Utility faucets in garages and laundry rooms take hard use. People hang buckets on the spout and yank hoses off the threads, which loosens the mounting and cracks the supply connections over time. Brass bodies hold up best here. The new faucet is anchored tight to the wall or the tub.

    Outdoor Spigot Replacement in Dundalk, MD

    Outdoor spigots wear out from sun, hoses and hard closing. Disconnecting garden hoses and shutting off outdoor spigots before the first hard freeze prevents many burst-pipe calls each winter.     Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Dundalk

    Kitchen faucets usually fail at the hose first. The pull-down hose wears where it bends over the spout neck, and once it splits, water runs down the inside of the faucet and into the cabinet without anyone seeing it. Sometimes a new hose is enough. If the body is also worn, the whole faucet gets replaced along with the supplies.

    Touchless Faucet Upgrade in Dundalk

    Swapping a standard faucet for a touchless one takes a little more planning. Touchless models need a dry spot on the cabinet wall for the control box and either batteries or an outlet within reach of the cord. Power gets checked first. We adjust the sensor range at the end.

    Corroded Faucet Replacement in Dundalk

    Green crust at the base means the faucet body is corroding. A pitted body will not let a new cartridge seal, so the drip comes back a few weeks after each repair. At that point, replace it. Even with fused nuts, we get it out and install a faucet with a ceramic disc cartridge.

    Budget-Friendly Faucet Replacement in Dundalk, MD

    Not every faucet swap needs a designer model. A mid-range faucet from Moen, Delta or Pfister with a ceramic cartridge and a brass body will outlast a pricey import with a plastic waterway.
