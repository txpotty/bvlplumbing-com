    Commercial Grease Trap Repair in Dundalk

    Restaurants, school cafeterias, hospital kitchens and bakeries all push more grease than a small trap can hold. We see undersized traps fail in the same few places every time.     Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Dundalk

    A cable machine punches a hole through grease. A jetter scrubs the whole pipe wall instead.     Flow Control Device Repair in Dundalk

    Hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, like 20 or 50 gallons per minute, and the flow control fitting is what holds the water to that rate. Take it out and a three-compartment sink dump blows grease right past the baffles. Inspectors look for it. We also clear the air intake, since a plugged intake on the flow control fitting lets the trap siphon.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Dundalk

    Inside a concrete gravity interceptor the baffle is usually a poured or precast wall with a crossover opening. When that wall spalls, grease slips through to the second compartment. We can rebuild the wall with form work and a sulfate-resistant mix or set a polyethylene baffle panel, depending on the tank. Both require a full pump-out and a dry tank.

    Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in Dundalk, MD

    Lids crack when someone sets a stockpot on them or a dolly rolls across a floor-mounted unit. A cracked lid lets odor out and lets mop water in. We replace the lid and gasket with the manufacturer part, and on in-floor units we check that the lid sits flush so nobody catches a toe or a cart wheel on it. Baffle damage often shows up at the same visit.
