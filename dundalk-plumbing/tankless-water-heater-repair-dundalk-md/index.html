    Tankless Water Heater Descaling Service in Dundalk

    Scale is calcium and magnesium carbonate that drops out of hard water when it heats, and a tankless exchanger heats water fast through narrow passages. Homes on private wells, especially in the low coastal counties, often deal with iron staining, odors or acidity that call for filtration or treatment. We hook a small pump and hoses to the isolation valves and circulate descaler for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with clean water. The inlet screen gets cleaned too.

    Commercial Tankless Repair in Dundalk, MD

    Hotels and gyms burn through hot water all day, so their tankless racks scale up two or three times faster than a home unit. We service rack systems with manifold kits, pulling the fault log and testing the flow sensor on every heater in the rack, and set a descaling schedule by run hours. Quarterly is common.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Dundalk, MD

    Condensing tankless heaters pull extra heat out of the exhaust and make acidic condensate that has to drain somewhere. That condensate runs through a trap and, where code or the manual calls for it, a neutralizer filled with limestone or marble chips before it reaches the drain. Clogged traps shut the unit down. We clean the trap, refill the neutralizer and check the PVC vent for sag or the wrong slope.

    Tankless Repair vs Replacement Cost in Dundalk, MD

    Rinnai owners usually call about a code 10, which points to an air supply or exhaust blockage.
