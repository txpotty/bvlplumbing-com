    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Dundalk

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. We reseal the threads. Handheld hoses and slide bars can go in during the same visit.

    Slow Shower Drain Repair in Dundalk, MD

    Standing water at your ankles is the first sign. Minerals bind soap scum and hair into a tough mat that clings to the pipe walls and gets thicker with every shower. An auger cuts through it. For stubborn buildup in the branch line, we jet the line with water instead of using acid cleaners.

    Commercial Shower Service in Dundalk

    Hotels and dorms cannot wait days on a shower. We carry commercial cartridges and thermostatic parts, and we can stage repairs room by room so only one unit is down at a time. After-hours work is available. We set and test the limit stop on each valve before the next room.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Dundalk, MD

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. The Moen 1222 runs the Posi-Temp line, and the Delta RP46074 runs the Monitor 13 and 14 series, and both wear out on a schedule you can almost set a calendar by. We stock both. Original owners can often get the cartridge free under warranty.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Dundalk

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. Water that keeps coming out after the handle is off has to be getting past a worn cartridge or seat, so replacing the showerhead alone will not stop it. The fix is in the valve. Drips from the arm threads just need fresh tape and a snug turn.

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Dundalk

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water running down the inside of the trim gets behind the tile at the valve opening, and over a few months it soaks the drywall around the valve without ever showing on the shower side. The trim comes off first. It is usually a loose bonnet nut or a worn O-ring.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Dundalk, MD

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. That points to the head, the flow restrictor or the cartridge, not the main or the water heater, and it is usually something we can fix within an hour. We start with the head. If every fixture is weak, we check the regulator and the main.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Dundalk, MD

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. Because everything is linked, a weak diverter starves the head and a leaky valve drips from the spout and the head alike. Each part gets tested. The overflow gasket gets a look too, since it is a common hidden leak.
