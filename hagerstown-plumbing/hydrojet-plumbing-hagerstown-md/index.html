    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Hagerstown

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. We jet the full main to the street tap. Four-inch mains get a heavy nozzle and high flow to push debris downstream.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Hagerstown, MD

    Older cast iron drains rust and scale from the inside, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. Jetting can remove that buildup. Badly corroded cast iron with thin walls or cracks needs gentler settings or no jetting at all, and the camera tells us which before we start, along with whether the pipe is a candidate for lining.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in Hagerstown

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. A root-cutting nozzle spins through the mass. The jet then flushes the debris to the main, and the camera marks the joints that let the roots in.

    Root Removal Jetting in Hagerstown

    Shade trees and shrubs send roots toward small cracks in clay and cast iron pipe. A root nozzle cuts them out. Roots come back through the same cracks, so the camera shows where a repair or liner would stop them.

    Same-Day Jetting for Backups in Hagerstown

    Sewage in a tub or floor drain is a main line blockage. Stop using water first. Our technician opens the line, then jets it to remove the cause, and a camera pass after jetting shows whether the backup came from buildup alone or from a broken or root-filled section that needs repair.

    Whole-House Drain Jetting in Hagerstown

    Slow drains in several rooms usually mean buildup in a main or branch line. We jet the main from the cleanout and the branches from roof vents or from fixture access points as needed for that layout. Most homes take two to three hours.

    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Hagerstown, MD

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. A jet scours the entire pipe wall. Snaking suits a one-time clog, while jetting fixes a line that clogs repeatedly.
