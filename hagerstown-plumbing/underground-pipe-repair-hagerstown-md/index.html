    Pipe Bursting Services in Hagerstown, MD

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. New pipe follows the old path. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, and fused HDPE follows right behind it, often at the same size or one size larger than the original. It works on badly damaged lines. Lining cannot do that.

    Main Water Line Repair in Hagerstown

    Your main water line runs from the meter box to the house under constant pressure. Small leaks grow. A spinning meter dial with every fixture off, falling pressure or a wet spot that never dries all point to a main line leak, and we locate it, expose the pipe and splice in a new section with approved fittings. Old galvanized usually gets replaced.
