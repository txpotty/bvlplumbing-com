    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Hagerstown, MD

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Most municipal water across Maryland runs moderately hard, so scale slowly builds inside water heaters, faucet aerators and showerheads over the years. Scale coats the heat exchanger, cuts efficiency and triggers error codes, and left alone it can crack the exchanger and end the warranty claim before it starts. A yearly flush prevents it.
