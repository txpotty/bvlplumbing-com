    What Goes Into a Well Tank Swap in Glen Burnie, MD

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Glen Burnie, MD

    Switch contacts pit and burn over time. Worn contacts can fail to open or close. When we swap a tank we look at the contacts and the sensing port underneath, and we usually recommend replacing a switch older than the tank it served. Cheap part.

    Well-Rite Pressure Tank Installation in Glen Burnie, MD

    Well-Rite tanks use a replaceable diaphragm design in many models. Common sizes are easy for us to get. Like any tank, the key is setting pre-charge before it fills, then confirming the gauge, switch and drawdown line up with the pump you have. A good tank installed badly fails early.

    Preventative Well Pressure Tank Maintenance in Glen Burnie, MD

    Once a year, test the pre-charge. Shut off the pump, drain the system to zero, and put a tire gauge on the valve at the top of the tank, topping it up to two psi below the cut-in if it reads low. Water out of the valve means a torn bladder.

    Well-X-Trol Pressure Tank Installation in Glen Burnie, MD

    Amtrol's Well-X-Trol line uses a diaphragm design. We put in plenty of them. Its stainless or polypropylene water connection keeps water off the steel shell, limiting internal corrosion, and the factory pre-charge still gets checked and adjusted to your switch on install.

    Diaphragm Pressure Tank Replacement in Glen Burnie, MD

    Diaphragm tanks use a flexible sheet clamped inside the shell. The diaphragm splits air from water. When it fails, symptoms match a bladder tank, with short cycling, pressure swings and water spitting from the air valve. Replacement is the usual answer.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Glen Burnie

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Threads on old tees weep. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, so we replace a crusted or corroded one rather than trust it. Whole set, one visit.

    Amtrol Pressure Tank Replacement in Glen Burnie

    Replacing an Amtrol tank with another Amtrol is often straightforward. Fittings tend to line up. Even so, we confirm the model is still the right size for your pump, because the old tank may have been too small all along. Check before swapping.

    Residential Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Glen Burnie

    Most homes on a well have a tank between 20 and 86 gallons sitting near the pressure switch. We shut off power to the pump first. Then we drain the system, unthread the old tank from the tank tee, set the pre-charge on the new one, and plumb it back in with fresh thread sealant and new fittings. Usually a morning.
