    What Goes Into a PEX Install in Glen Burnie, MD

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Glen Burnie, MD

    Think of a manifold like an electrical panel, but for water lines. A central header feeds each fixture on its own line with its own valve, which lets you shut off one toilet and leave the kitchen running. It uses more pipe. What you get back is very few hidden fittings and steadier pressure when two people shower at the same time.

    New Construction PEX Plumbing in Glen Burnie, MD

    On a new build, BVL Plumbing runs PEX supply lines while the framing is still open in Glen Burnie, MD. Plates get drilled, the manifold goes up, and every stub-out lands at the plan heights before the rough-in inspection and its pressure test. Steel nail plates cover every stud crossing. Builders like the pace.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Glen Burnie, MD

    Opening a wall for a kitchen remodel is the cheapest time to swap out the old pipe. With the studs bare, we can run PEX back to a manifold or to the nearest good trunk, set new stub-outs for the sink and dishwasher, and add a line for the fridge ice maker. An addition gets sized on its own. A far-end bathroom may need a three-quarter inch feed to hold pressure.

    PEX for Water Line Replacement in Glen Burnie

    Your service line runs from the meter or well to the house, usually 18 to 36 inches down. A PEX main comes off a coil in one piece, so there are no buried joints between the meter and the foundation wall. Clay that swells when wet and shrinks when dry can stress sewer laterals and water service lines over time. We bed it in sand. Sharp rock against plastic pipe is how a new line fails early.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Glen Burnie

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and it really depends on the house. PEX-A makes tighter bends, can be heat-fixed if it kinks, and its fittings flow better, while PEX-B is cheaper and its crimps hold just as well when each one is gauged. Either one lasts for decades. In a cramped attic, we usually pick PEX-A.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Glen Burnie

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool and a cold-expansion ring. We stretch the pipe and ring, seat the fitting, and within seconds the plastic shrinks back around the barb into a tight seal. No crimp tool to calibrate. Because the fittings are wide inside, each joint costs very little flow.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Glen Burnie, MD

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. Every one is listed for potable water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in Glen Burnie

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside out. Give it four or five decades and the inside is so packed with rust that a half-inch pipe barely flows, and the first draw in the morning runs brown. A full PEX repipe replaces all of it. Pressure comes back at the fixtures and the rust stops.
