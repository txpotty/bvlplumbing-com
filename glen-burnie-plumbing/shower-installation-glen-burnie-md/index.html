    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Glen Burnie, MD

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. We pull the tub, move the drain from the end of the tub to where the shower pan needs it and raise the valve to shower height, usually around 48 inches off the floor. On a slab, we cut concrete. No more stepping over a tub wall.

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Glen Burnie

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. Without a curb to hold water back, the floor has to pitch a quarter inch per foot toward the drain, and the waterproofing has to run well past the shower opening. We set that slope. We add blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Glen Burnie, MD

    Prefab units are the fastest route to a new shower. We set the base on mortar, connect the drain, install the valve and trim and make sure the walls are fastened so nothing flexes when someone leans on them. Good for rentals and guest baths. Usually a one-day job.

    Custom Shower Enclosure Plumbing in Glen Burnie

    Body sprays and steam units add lines and planning. Every body spray needs a supply line, and the sizing has to be right so the farthest outlet still has good pressure. Pressure varies by house. A gauge reading comes first.

    Shower Stall Installation in Glen Burnie

    A one-piece stall needs a level, fully supported base. Fiberglass and acrylic bases go down on a mortar bed so they cannot flex, and flex is the usual reason a base cracks early. The drain connects from below. We check the flow at the head once the trim is on.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Glen Burnie

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. Valve, head, handheld and drain go in first, then the pan is sloped a quarter inch per foot to the drain. Testing follows. The valve gets a pressure test and the pan a 24-hour flood test before tile.

    Shower Valve and Fixture Installation in Glen Burnie, MD

    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower. Thermostatic valves step up from there, holding an exact temperature and letting you set volume separately, which suits rain heads, handhelds and body sprays. Either one protects against scalding. The outlet count usually decides it.

    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Glen Burnie, MD

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. Each valve is set to the depth its trim requires, and we include integral stops whenever the valve supports them. Handles and the head come later. That timing keeps the trim from getting scratched or caked with thinset while the tile is set.
