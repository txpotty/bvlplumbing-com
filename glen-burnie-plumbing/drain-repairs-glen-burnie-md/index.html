    Water coming up somewhere other than where you are draining. It is the clearest sign.     Repeat clogs come from something in the pipe catching debris. Grease or scale is common. Poor pipe slope, a missing vent or a cracked fitting with a rough edge catches debris over and over, even after the line is cleared. A camera shows which one you have.

    Clearing a simple clog takes an hour or two. Repairs run longer. Replacing a section of main line or opening a wall to swap a cast iron stack can take a full day or more, depending on access and whether a permit inspection is needed. We give you a timeline with the price.

    Hydro-jetting scours the pipe with water at several thousand psi. It cleans wall to wall. A snake only punches a hole through a clog, while a jetter removes grease, scale and fine roots from the full circumference of the pipe. We camera first on old or brittle pipe.

    A trap swap or a fixture drain fix is usually an hour or two. Bigger jobs run past a day. Replacing a section of main line can take a full day or several days, especially if permits, inspections or excavation under a slab are involved. We give you the timeline up front.

    Unclogging a drain does not need one. Bigger changes might. Replacing a stack, relocating a drain or replacing a section of the building drain in Glen Burnie may require a plumbing permit, depending on local rules and how much of the system is being changed. We pull it when one applies.
