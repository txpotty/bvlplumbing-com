    Yearly Water Softener Tune-Ups in Glen Burnie

    A tune-up starts with two hardness readings and ends with a full manual regeneration that we watch from start to finish. Between those, the salt level, brine well and float all get a look. It takes about an hour on most homes.

    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in Glen Burnie, MD

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. The beads turn rusty brown and stop grabbing hardness. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we shorten the time between regenerations so the iron does not build back up.

    Resin Bed Checks and Regeneration Settings in Glen Burnie

    Resin does the actual softening. Each bead trades sodium for calcium and magnesium until it runs out of capacity. We compare the softened hardness against the gallons used since the last regeneration to see whether the bed still holds its rated capacity.
