    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Glen Burnie, MD

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. A jet scours the entire pipe wall. For a single clog in an otherwise clean line, snaking is cheaper and works fine, but for a line that clogs every few months, jetting removes the buildup that keeps catching debris.

    Chemical-Free Drain Cleaning in Glen Burnie, MD

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Caustic cleaners can harm old pipe. The debris flushes away as normal waste, leaving nothing behind to harm pipe or septic bacteria.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Glen Burnie, MD

    Older cast iron drains rust and scale from the inside, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. Jetting can remove that buildup. Badly corroded cast iron with thin walls or cracks needs gentler settings or no jetting at all, and the camera tells us which before we start, along with whether the pipe is a candidate for lining.

    Main Line Jetting in Glen Burnie, MD

    The lateral from your house to the street handles every drain in the house, so buildup there causes backups everywhere at once. We jet it from the cleanout to the tap. Settled low spots get extra passes, and we mark them on the video for future reference.

    Commercial and Restaurant Hydrojetting in Glen Burnie

    Hotels, apartment complexes and shopping centers rely on drains that handle heavy daily use. A backup closes rooms or stores. We schedule around slow hours and keep video records for property managers.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Glen Burnie, MD

    Restaurant kitchen lines send warm grease downstream where it cools and sticks. By the time it reaches the interceptor, a lot of it has already coated the pipe. Short jetting passes keep loosened grease from plugging the line downstream. Quarterly works for most.

    Residential Hydrojetting in Glen Burnie

    Most home jetting calls involve a greasy kitchen line or a root-filled sewer. Whenever possible, we work from the outside cleanout and keep the hose out of the house. Some kitchen lines get jetted from under the sink. We lay down floor protection first.

    Root Removal Jetting in Glen Burnie

    Shade trees and shrubs send roots toward small cracks in clay and cast iron pipe. Root-cutting nozzles clear them. Jetting does not stop roots from returning, because the crack is still there, but it clears the pipe completely and lets us see on camera whether a spot repair or a liner would stop the regrowth.
