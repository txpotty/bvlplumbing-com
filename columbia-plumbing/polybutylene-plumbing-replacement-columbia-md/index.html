    Copper Replacement for Polybutylene in Columbia, MD

    Some homeowners in Columbia, MD want copper when they take out polybutylene. That works fine. Our copper replacements use thicker Type L pipe with lead-free soldered or pressed joints, not the thinner Type M. Copper costs more and takes longer, since each turn needs a fitting, but it tolerates heat and sunlight well and has a long track record.

    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Columbia, MD

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. We use Uponor PEX-A with expansion fittings that do not restrict flow. Every fixture, appliance and the water heater gets new pipe from the main, and most houses are finished in two to four days with water on each evening.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Columbia, MD

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Faster install means lower labor cost. We keep PEX out of direct sunlight, away from water heater flues and recessed light housings and support it at the spacing the manufacturer calls for, which protects both the pipe and the warranty.

    Full Home Polybutylene Repiping in Columbia

    A full replacement takes every poly supply line out of service, not just the sections that have failed. Partial jobs leave risk behind. We run new lines from the main shutoff to every sink, toilet, tub, washer, ice maker and water heater, add new quarter-turn stops and test the whole system before patching.

    Partial Polybutylene Replacement in Columbia, MD

    Full replacement is the right long-term fix, but it is not always possible right away. Budget and timing matter. Replacing the riskiest parts first, near the heater and in the attic, lowers the odds of a burst while you plan the rest. You will know exactly what remains.
