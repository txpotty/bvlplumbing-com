    What We Fix on Well Systems in Columbia, MD

    Jet Pump Replacement in Columbia, MD

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. A two-pipe deep-well jet can reach further down at the cost of efficiency. The common failure is losing prime. Usually a leaking foot valve or a cracked suction line lets the water fall back down the well, so we check both before we blame the pump.

    Emergency Well Pump Replacement in Columbia

    Dry faucets on a well-only property mean no flushing or cooking, so we treat those as same-day calls whenever the crew and the weather allow. We keep 1/2 and 3/4 HP submersibles on the truck. Splice kits and pressure switches ride along too.

    Agricultural and Ranch Well Pumps in Columbia

    Irrigation and stock wells often run 3 to 10 HP pumps, sometimes on three-phase power with a motor starter and overload protection at the panel. We replace those pumps and the starters that protect them. We schedule around watering times.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Columbia

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes a healthy pump look sick. Short cycling, where the pump starts every few seconds of water use, usually means the tank bladder has failed and the pump is paying for it. A tire gauge on the air valve tells us a lot. The air charge should read 2 psi under the switch cut-in.

    No-Water Calls in Columbia

    Pump trouble rarely gives notice. Usually the faucet spits air, then quits, after the motor has been limping along for weeks. We test the control box and pressure switch before pulling anything.

    Control Box and Pressure Switch Replacement in Columbia, MD

    Not every dry faucet means a dead pump. Pressure switches with burned contacts or a clogged pickup tube can leave the pump sitting idle while the tank drains to zero. The switch itself is a cheap part. You get to see the old part.

    Well Pump Brands We Install in Columbia, MD

    Goulds, Grundfos, Franklin Electric and Sta-Rite cover nearly every well pump we install. Each brand has strengths, and we choose based on your well. Parts for Goulds and Franklin are easy to get, which matters when a control box dies on a weekend.
