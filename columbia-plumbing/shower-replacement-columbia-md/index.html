    Shower Replacement Jobs We Handle in Columbia, MD

    Budget Shower Replacement in Columbia, MD

    Replacing just the shower can freshen a bathroom without a full remodel. An acrylic unit and a new valve cost far less than tile and hold up for years when set right. We price options side by side. Then you pick.

    Commercial Shower Replacement in Columbia

    Gyms and hotels need showers that survive heavy use. Commercial replacements get heavy-duty valves, tamper-resistant trim and properly sized drains to code. We phase the job. That way some showers stay open while others are replaced.

    Shower Pan Replacement in Columbia, MD

    Cracked pans damage subfloors, walls and nearby rooms. With slab floors, water can move under flooring into the next room, so we trace it first. Then we remove the pan. The new pan gets proper slope, waterproofing and a flood test.

    Leaking Shower Pan Replacement in Columbia, MD

    Leaking pans soak the subfloor and the ceiling below. Whether it is a prefab base with a crack or a tiled pan with a failed liner, we tear it out, replace any rotted subfloor and install a new pan that gets flood-tested for a full day. We replace the drain as well. It ends the cycle.

    Old Shower Valve Replacement in Columbia

    Two- and three-handle valves from decades back often leak and lack scald protection. Working from behind the wall when possible, we pull the old valve, install a new one and use a large trim plate to hide the old holes. Tile often stays put. It is a big safety upgrade.

    Custom Tile Shower Replacement in Columbia, MD

    Custom tile replacement starts with careful demolition. Demo goes to the studs, damaged wood gets replaced and a new waterproofed pan goes in with the valve set to tile depth. The slope has to be right. A flood test comes before tile.

    Walk-In Shower Conversion in Columbia, MD

    Walk-in conversions are one of the most requested bathroom jobs we do. The drain moves to the pan's center, the valve goes up to shower height and the slope keeps water from escaping the entry. Grab bar blocking is easy now. Accessibility standards are followed on request.

    Shower Replacement Options and Costs in Columbia, MD

    A valve-only swap costs far less than a full rebuild. That only works if the pan and walls are fine, since a new valve cannot fix a pan leak. New pans usually mean new tile. We match the option to the shower's condition.
