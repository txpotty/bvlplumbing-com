    Scale builds slowly inside drain pipe, narrowing it a little more each year.     Scheduled Jetting Maintenance in Columbia

    Properties with chronic drain issues do better on a jetting schedule. Restaurants jet every three to six months. Root-prone home sewers usually jet every year or two, with camera records to track regrowth.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Columbia, MD

    Restaurant kitchen lines carry hot grease that hardens as it cools. By the time it reaches the interceptor, a lot of it has already coated the pipe. We jet the line from the kitchen to the interceptor in short passes, which keeps loosened grease from plugging the pipe farther down, and we coordinate with your pump-out company. Quarterly works for most.
