    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Columbia

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. That is often the first sign. New PEX or copper stops the rust, restores pressure and ends leaks at the threads, which is where galvanized fails first. Old pipe stays in walls when pulling it would wreck finished surfaces.

    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Columbia, MD

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average house often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper costing more. One slab leak with access and repair often costs $1,000 to $4,000. The numbers add up quickly. By the second or third leak, a repipe usually pays for itself, and we put both options side by side so you can decide.

    Copper Repiping in Columbia, MD

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes, with lead-free solder or ProPress fittings. Expect a slightly longer job. Most copper jobs run a day longer than PEX, since each turn needs its own fitting.

    Type L Copper Repiping in Columbia

    Copper has been plumbing homes for generations, and done right it lasts a long time. We use Type L, not the thinner Type M, and we support it with the right hangers so it does not rub or rattle in the walls. All joints use lead-free solder. Copper can pinhole in aggressive water, so we ask about your water and any past leaks before recommending it over PEX.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Columbia, MD

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. Brittle pipe walls and cracked fittings mean one polybutylene leak usually signals risk across the whole house. Full replacement is the fix. Insurers often ask about it at renewal.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Columbia

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water and long attic runs. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open the pipe end and let it shrink back over the fitting for a full-bore joint. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. Most homes are repiped with PEX-A in two to three days.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Columbia, MD

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Low pressure, brown water and leaks at threaded joints are the typical signs. Old galvanized gets removed or abandoned and replaced with PEX or copper, and most homes see better pressure and clearer water right away.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Columbia

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. It holds up well, does not mind sunlight in an exposed run and has a long track record behind it. It costs more. We sweat joints with lead-free solder or press them, hang the pipe at proper support intervals and test the whole system before patching.
