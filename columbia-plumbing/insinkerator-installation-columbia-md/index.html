    Evolution Series Installation in Columbia

    Evolution units grind in two or three stages and carry extra sound insulation around the motor housing, which is why they sit taller and weigh more than a Badger. Clearance under the bowl matters here. We measure from the sink bottom to the trap arm before the install so the unit does not crowd the trap. The Compact fits tight cabinets, and the Excel needs the most room.

    Badger Disposal Install in Columbia

    Most replacement disposals in local kitchens are a Badger. A 1/3 to 3/4 HP Badger handles normal scraps and fits almost any sink base cabinet. The unit hangs on its quick-lock mount, the discharge tube goes on, a cord kit gets added if needed and we finish with a cold-water test grind. About an hour, usually.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Columbia

    An air switch puts a push button on the countertop or sink deck instead of a wall switch. Pressing it sends air down a tube. That pulse trips a switch in a control box plugged in under the sink, so no live wiring sits anywhere near the water, which is the main reason people like them. We drill the counter or use the spare faucet hole.

    New Construction Disposal Installs in Columbia

    New builds and gut remodels get the disposal in two trips. During rough-in, the drain stub height gets set and the electrician leaves a switched outlet in the sink cabinet. The disposal itself waits. After the sink and faucet are set, we return to hang the unit, tie in the dishwasher and run the full water test.

    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Columbia, MD

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. Expect the new unit to sit lower in the cabinet. That extra height can put the discharge outlet below the trap arm, so we measure the drop before install day and lower the trap or the arm if the numbers do not work. We also confirm the circuit can carry the larger motor.

    Commercial Kitchen Disposer Service in Columbia

    Commercial kitchens put more through a disposer in a day than a house does in a month. Downtime costs money.
