    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Columbia, MD House

    Gas Water Heater Efficiency Ratings in Columbia

    Efficiency ratings on tank heaters are listed as UEF, uniform energy factor, and a higher number means less gas per gallon heated. Basic tanks land near 0.60 UEF. Condensing models top 0.80, and some qualify for utility rebates where those are offered.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Upgrades in Columbia, MD

    Condensing gas tanks run around 90 percent efficient or better and vent through PVC, while a standard atmospheric tank sits closer to 60 percent. You will need a condensate drain and a nearby outlet. Big households see payback sooner.

    No Hot Water Calls in Columbia

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. We check those first. An old tank with a bad valve usually gets replaced.

    Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in Columbia, MD

    Big families with soaking tubs often outgrow a 50 gallon tank and need a 75 gallon heater or a high-recovery 50 at around 65,000 to 75,000 BTU. A bigger burner needs more gas. We check the meter and pipe size first, because a starved burner never reaches its rated recovery.

    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in Columbia

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon atmospheric tank is what most homes have, and swapping one like for like is a two to three hour job when the venting and gas line are in good shape. The old tank gets drained and hauled off, and the new one goes on a pan. Every install gets new gas and water connectors. Then we leak test with soap solution and check draft at the hood.

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Columbia

    Bradford White tanks are sold only through plumbing wholesalers, not big-box stores, and plenty of Maryland plumbers install them for that reason. Parts are easy to get. Installing to their specs keeps the tank warranty intact.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Columbia, MD

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. A natural gas unit does not go on propane unless the manufacturer allows a conversion. We confirm pressure with a manometer.

    A.O. Smith Gas Water Heater Service in Columbia

    A.O. Smith makes tanks under its own name and also builds the State line, so parts overlap a lot between the two brands. We handle the sizing and the gas connection. We confirm the vent meets their specs.
