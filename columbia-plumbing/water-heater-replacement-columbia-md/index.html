    Conventional Tank Water Heater Replacement in Columbia

    Standard tank heaters remain the most common type, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment.     Electric Water Heater Replacement in Columbia

    Electric water heaters in Columbia usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. Elements are cheap to swap. Tank leaks are the end, so we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to your existing circuit. Hot water is back the same day.

    Rheem and Ruud Water Heater Replacement in Columbia, MD

    Ruud and Rheem are built by the same maker and share many parts. Our technicians install both, and since the parts overlap, a Ruud thermostat or element is easy to source when a repair makes more sense than a full replacement on a newer tank. We will say when repair works.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Columbia

    Some older neighborhoods still have lead service lines, so replacing one is a sensible upgrade when other plumbing work is already underway.
