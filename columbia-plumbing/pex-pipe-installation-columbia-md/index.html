    What Goes Into a PEX Job in Columbia, MD

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Columbia

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. Since a crimp is only as good as the tool that made it, every ring gets a go/no-go gauge check, and any ring that fails gets cut off and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.

    Emergency PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Columbia, MD

    PEX rarely fails on its own. When it does, the cause is usually a nail or screw driven through it, a rodent chewing on an attic run, or a fitting that was never crimped all the way. We carry the common sizes. Most emergency PEX repairs in Columbia, MD come down to a cut and a splice, usually under an hour after the wall is open.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in Columbia

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside out. Give it four or five decades and the inside is so packed with rust that a half-inch pipe barely flows, and the first draw in the morning runs brown. We replace every foot of it with PEX. You get real pressure back, and the discolored water goes away with the old pipe.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Columbia, MD

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A bends the easiest and uses expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and uses crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C handles more like B. Every one is listed for potable water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Columbia, MD

    With PEX-A, the tech slips an expansion ring onto the pipe and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting is pushed in while the pipe is open, and the plastic's memory shrinks it back around the barb, sealing tighter during the first minute. In the cold, it takes longer to shrink back. We let joints set before testing.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Columbia

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool plus a ring slipped over the pipe end. The tech expands the pipe and ring together, pushes the fitting in, and the plastic shrinks back around the barb over the next few seconds, tighter than when it started. There is no crimp gauge to check. The fittings have a wide bore, so flow loss through each joint stays low.

    Commercial PEX Pipe Solutions in Columbia, MD

    Apartment buildings, small offices and churches in Columbia, MD are good candidates for PEX as much as houses can. With a manifold and shutoff for each unit, a leak in one apartment only takes that apartment offline, not the whole riser. Fire-rated walls need care. Where PEX passes through a fire-rated assembly, we use listed firestop sleeves or sealant so the rating holds.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Columbia, MD

    Gray polybutylene was installed widely from the late seventies into the mid nineties. Chlorine in treated water attacks the plastic and the acetal fittings from the inside, so it can look fine from outside right up to the day a joint lets go. Many insurers ask whether you have it. Replacing it with PEX can make the house easier to insure and sell.
