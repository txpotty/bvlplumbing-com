    Brand Tub Faucet Replacement in Columbia

    Brand matters more on tub valves than anywhere else. Each maker uses its own cartridges and trim threads, so a same-brand trim kit usually bolts on without plumbing changes. Cross-brand trim seldom fits. We find the logo or the casting mark first.

    Hard Water Tub Faucet Replacement in Columbia

    Most municipal water across Maryland runs moderately hard, so scale slowly builds inside water heaters, faucet aerators and showerheads over the years. Scale packs into cartridges and spout diverters until handles stiffen and the diverter will not lift. Soaking and cleaning buys time on a lightly scaled valve, but once the brass is pitted or the cartridge has fused to the body, replacing the faucet is the only fix that lasts. We pull a cartridge to check.

    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Columbia, MD

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. Replacement inside the wall makes sense when parts are discontinued, the valve body is cracked or pitted, or you want anti-scald protection on an old three-handle faucet that has none. You see both prices. Most people choose repair first.

    Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in Columbia, MD

    Water running from the spout during a shower means a failing diverter. On a single-handle setup the diverter is usually the lift gate in the spout itself, and on a three-handle faucet it is the middle stem, and both wear out until water splits between the spout and the head. Spout diverters are a quick swap. Middle stems need a seat too.

    Tub Faucets in Older Homes in Columbia

    Older homes hide odd tub plumbing. Expect rusted galvanized supplies, valves with no stops, and steel spout nipples that snap. Adapters for old pipe ride along. Replacing a short run of galvanized is common.

    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Columbia

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. Single-handle drips mean worn cartridge seals, and multi-handle drips mean worn washers and seats. Replacement parts stop it. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Columbia

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. Supplies have to rise through the floor, from a slab cut or a crawlspace, with a bracket anchored below. Deck fillers mount on the tub deck. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.
