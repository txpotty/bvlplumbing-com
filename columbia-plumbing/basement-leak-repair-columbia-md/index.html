    Wall Leak Solutions in Columbia, MD

    Damp walls without a crack often point to water soaking through porous block or concrete. Tape foil to the wall for a full day, and if the room side is wet it is condensation, not a leak. Condensation needs a dehumidifier. Seepage needs drainage outside or a channel inside.

    Interior Drainage Systems in Columbia

    Where digging outside is not practical, we handle the water from inside instead. We cut the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and slope it to a new or existing sump pit before patching the floor with fresh concrete. The wall gets a dimpled membrane. Seepage runs down behind it and into the pipe.

    Waterproofing for Older Maryland Homes in Columbia, MD

    Urgent Basement Leak Service in Columbia, MD

    Crack Injection for Basements in Columbia

    Injection works on poured concrete walls, not block. We set ports along the crack, seal the face with epoxy paste, and pump in polyurethane or epoxy from the bottom port up until resin shows at the next port. Epoxy bonds the two sides. Polyurethane is better for active leaks because it foams on contact with water.
