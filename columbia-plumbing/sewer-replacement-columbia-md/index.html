    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Columbia, MD

    Roots find sewer lines by following moisture. They need only a tiny gap. Once inside, they grow into a mat that catches paper and grease, and cutting them out with a machine only buys a few months, because the opening is still there. New PVC with glued joints gives them no way in.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Columbia, MD

    A lot of older houses have no outside cleanout. Every clog then means pulling a toilet. When we replace a sewer line we add a two-way cleanout near the foundation, with a cap at ground level you can find, so future cleaning or camera work is quick and cheap.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement Options in Columbia, MD

    Bursting and pulling replace the pipe. Lining patches the inside of the old one. We use bursting when the old line is broken or undersized, since it puts a new pipe in place instead of reinforcing the old, and lining when the pipe is still round and mostly intact. Each has its place.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Columbia

    An open trench gives full access to the pipe from the house to the main. A small excavator does most of it, with hand digging near other lines. The old pipe comes out, the trench bottom gets a bed of gravel or sand, and new 4-inch PVC goes in at a steady quarter inch of fall per foot, checked with a level before backfill. Then the camera.

    Commercial Sewer Replacement in Columbia

    Bigger pipe, more flow, more rules. Most commercial lines are 6-inch or larger. We handle the permit, set up bypass pumping when the building cannot shut down, and arrange traffic control when the work reaches the road, keeping the outage as short as the job allows. Night shifts possible.

    Sewer Scope Inspections for Replacement in Columbia

    A sewer camera on a push cable goes in through the cleanout. It records every joint, crack and root on the way to the main.
