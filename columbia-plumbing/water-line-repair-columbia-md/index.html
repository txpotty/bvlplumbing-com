    Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Columbia, MD

    A full break can flood the yard and leave the house dry at the same time. We shut it down first.     Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Columbia

    Underground leaks hide well. The first clue is often a water bill that jumped for no reason. Others notice a patch of grass that grows greener than the rest, or a spot near the walk that stays muddy days after rain. We confirm the leak at the meter, trace the pipe route and listen along it. The dig follows the sound.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Columbia, MD

    PEX itself rarely fails underground, but the joints and the first few feet near the house take a beating. Rats and gophers sometimes chew it. A shovel or a fence post auger can nick it, and sunlight exposure before burial weakens the plastic over time. We cut out the damaged section and install new pipe with crimp, clamp or expansion fittings, matched to the PEX brand already in the ground.

    Commercial Water Line Services in Columbia, MD

    Besides repairs, BVL Plumbing handles service line upgrades when a commercial tenant needs more flow, like a restaurant adding a dish line or a second restroom. Bigger meters need bigger pipe. We size it to the fixture load.     Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Columbia

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. Straight runs are ideal. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, and a length of HDPE trails behind it in the same path. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Columbia, MD

    Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground. Homes on private wells, especially in the low coastal counties, often deal with rusty mineral staining, odors or acidity that call for filtration or treatment. Replacing the full run with copper or PEX clears all of it up. Most homes are done in a day.
