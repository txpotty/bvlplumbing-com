    What Fails on a Disposal, and How We Fix It in Columbia, MD

    Humming Disposal Repair in Columbia

    Humming with no spin almost always means a jam. Spoons, glass, bones and pits cause most of them. With the power off, we free the flywheel using the hex socket underneath, pull the object, and test the motor and reset with water running.

    Grinding Noise Repair in Columbia

    Loud metal-on-metal sounds may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove any debris, then run the unit empty to hear whether the motor itself is making the noise. Failing bearings are not worth fixing.

    Leaking Disposal Fix in Columbia, MD

    When the drip starts at the top, the putty under the sink flange is usually dried out. We drop the unit, pull the flange, clean off the old putty and reset it with fresh putty and new gaskets, then hang the disposal back up. Most top leaks stop there. Side leaks are usually the discharge gasket or the dishwasher hose clamp.

    Noisy Disposal Repair in Columbia

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough all point to something inside. Loose mounting rings rattle too. We check the mount, then look for debris, then run the motor empty, since a whine with an empty chamber means the bearings are going.

    Electrical Problems and Resetting in Columbia

    No hum at all usually means no power reaching the motor. We start at the red reset button on the bottom, then check the breaker, the wall switch and the outlet or junction box under the sink with a meter. It is wet under a sink. In that damp cabinet, wire nuts corrode and cord connections loosen, so we replace them instead of retightening.

    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Columbia, MD

    Good grinding but slow draining usually means the discharge tube or the trap arm is packed. Rice and coffee grounds are the usual culprits. The trap comes off, the tube and arm get cleared with a properly sized cable, and we confirm the arm drops about a quarter inch per foot.

    InSinkErator Disposal Repair in Columbia, MD

    The InSinkErator lineup runs from Badger units to the Evolution series, and we repair both. Badgers are simple single-stage units, while Evolution models have multi-stage grinding and, on some models, a separate stage that can jam on its own. Common parts are on board. Motors on these units are not sold separately.

    Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in Columbia

    A disposal that hums but will not grind has power but cannot turn, while one that is silent has no power at all. They are different problems. For a silent unit we test the breaker, reset and switch, and for a humming one we check the flywheel and motor before deciding if it is worth repairing.
