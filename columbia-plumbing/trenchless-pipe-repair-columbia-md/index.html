    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Columbia

    Tree roots, corrosion and soil movement wreck most sewer lines. Soils in Maryland range from heavy clay in the central counties to sandy, low-lying ground near the shore, and each affects buried lines differently. Small cracks get a sectional liner. For longer damage we line the whole run, and for a collapse we burst in new pipe, without trenching the yard or cutting the drive.

    Pipe Bursting in Columbia, MD

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. You'll see an entry and exit pit. A bursting head is pulled through, fracturing the old pipe outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind, and that HDPE is fused into one continuous length with no joints at all.

    Sectional Point Repair in Columbia

    Sometimes only one joint or a few feet of pipe is bad. That calls for a sectional liner. Guided by camera, a short liner is placed over the bad section and cured, so you pay for a few feet instead of the full line. It suits isolated damage in Columbia sewer lines.

    Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Columbia

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Both matter. Before lining, Picote heads mill out roots and scale, and after curing they reopen the lateral connections so each drain flows. Good prep makes a good liner.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Columbia

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Columbia yard.

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Columbia, MD

    A broken water service line wastes water and soaks the yard. It also drops pressure. We locate the break, then replace the line by bursting new HDPE through the old path or boring a new path, so the lawn and walkway stay put and you are back in water quickly. We pressure test before turning water on.
