    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Gaithersburg, MD House

    No Hot Water Calls in Gaithersburg

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. Those get tested before anything else. An old tank with a bad valve usually gets replaced.

    Tankless Gas Conversion in Gaithersburg

    Going tankless usually means a bigger gas line, since a whole-house tankless unit can draw 199,000 BTU against the 40,000 a tank burner uses. The 1/2-inch line feeding a tank is rarely enough. The venting changes too. We check meter capacity and pipe length before quoting so there are no surprises halfway through the job.

    Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in Gaithersburg, MD

    Big families with soaking tubs often outgrow a 50 gallon tank and need a 75 gallon heater or a high-recovery 50 at around 65,000 to 75,000 BTU. Those burners need more gas. We check the meter and pipe size first, because a starved burner never reaches its rated recovery.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Service in Gaithersburg, MD

    Rheem tanks and their Ruud twins share most parts, which helps when a gas valve fails a few years in. Either one works. We set them up per the factory manual and test draft.

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Gaithersburg

    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. When one fails twice on a tank that is already 8 or 9 years old, replacing the whole heater is usually cheaper over time. A dedicated outlet gets confirmed first.

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Gaithersburg

    Bradford White tanks are sold only through plumbing wholesalers, not big-box stores, and plenty of Maryland plumbers install them for that reason. Parts are easy to get. We follow their venting and clearance specs to the letter, which keeps the warranty valid.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Gaithersburg, MD

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. A standard outlet has to be within reach of the cord. Vent length and elbows stay inside the manufacturer's limits.

    40 and 50 Gallon Tank Replacement in Gaithersburg

    Forty and fifty gallon gas tanks are the workhorse in most Maryland homes, and a leak at the bottom seam means the tank is finished. Current tanks are often taller and wider. We measure the closet before ordering. Most of these swaps take two to three hours.
