    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Line Cleaning in Gaithersburg

    Cabling a sewer line in Gaithersburg usually costs $200 to $500. Hydro jetting costs more. A recorded camera inspection usually adds $150 to $350, depending on the length of the line and the access point we use.

    Sectional cables, root saws and jetters. Plus a sewer camera. We use cables for most jobs, jetters for heavy buildup and a camera to see the line before and after.

    Several drains backing up at once. That is the main sign. Odors, gurgling and water rising in the shower when the toilet flushes all mean the lateral is likely blocked.

    A simple clog takes 1 to 2 hours. Harder jobs take longer. Heavy root removal or a line with multiple access points can take 3 to 4 hours or more, and we give you a time estimate after the first inspection.

    Once or twice a year for homes with older pipe or big trees. Newer lines need less. Homes with frequent clogs or commercial properties with heavy use may need more frequent cleaning, timed before roots or grease close the line.

    Small drain clogs, maybe. Laterals need real equipment. Without the right machine and a camera, a DIY attempt often fails or damages the line.

    Water pooling in the yard over the line. Strong sewage odors too. Multiple drains backing up at once and gurgling from toilets or drains are the other main indicators that the lateral, not a single branch, is the problem.

    Drain cleaning covers single fixtures. Sewer cleaning covers the main. A sink or tub drain serves one fixture, while the sewer line carries all the wastewater from the house to the city sewer or septic tank, so a sewer clog affects every drain.
