    Copper to PEX Conversions in Gaithersburg

    Pinhole leaks in copper tend to come in clusters. Aggressive water, fast flow and soil contact under slabs can pit copper from the inside, and the first few pinholes are typically followed by more. Most municipal water across Maryland runs moderately hard, so scale slowly builds inside water heaters, faucet aerators and shower valves over the years. Converting to PEX stops the cycle. We use listed transition fittings anywhere PEX ties into copper that stays.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Gaithersburg

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A bends tighter, recovers from a kink with a heat gun, and its expansion fittings flow more water, while PEX-B costs less and its crimp joints are just as reliable when they are gauged properly. Both last decades. For tight attics we lean toward PEX-A.

    Whole-Home PEX Repiping in Gaithersburg

    Low pressure at every faucet, across the whole house, usually points at the pipe and not the fixtures.     PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Gaithersburg, MD

    Price on a repipe comes down to a few things: bathroom count, square footage, how many stories, and how easy the attic and walls are to work in.     PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Gaithersburg

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. It is a five-second check. Skipping it is how leaks start.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Gaithersburg, MD

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. All three are code-listed for drinking water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.
