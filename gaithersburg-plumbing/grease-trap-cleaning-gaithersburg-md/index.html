    Why Choose BVL Plumbing for Grease Trap Cleaning in Gaithersburg, MD?

    A grease trap works by slowing the water down long enough for fat to float and food to sink. Past a quarter full, that stops. The grease then slips past the outlet into the lateral, cools into a hard white crust, and a cable machine only bores a narrow hole through it.

    • Complete Pump-Outs, We pull grease, water and the solids on the floor every time.
    • Prevent Backups, Pumping on schedule keeps grease from slipping past the outlet and setting up in the line.
    • Extended Lifespan, Less hydrogen sulfide in the tank means slower corrosion of concrete and baffles.

    Citywide Coverage

    Interceptors under parking lots need room for the truck. The visit gets planned around your lot. If customers park over the lid during the day, we come before opening or after close so nobody has to move a car and the hose does not cross a busy drive aisle.

    Experienced Team

    Every tech on a BVL Plumbing truck is trained on confined-space rules before they touch a large interceptor. Nobody goes in a tank without a gas monitor. Grease tanks collect hydrogen sulfide, which can drop a person in a few breaths at high concentrations, so the monitor never comes off.

    Safety-First Approach

    We do not sell enzyme or bacteria additives as a replacement for pumping. Many programs do not allow it. Those products break grease into smaller droplets that pass through the outlet and re-form into hard deposits farther down the sewer, which is exactly the problem the trap exists to stop.

    Grease Line Jetting in Gaithersburg

    Grease that slips past a full trap coats the 4-inch line out to the main. The buildup forms rings that are thickest along the bottom.     Grease Trap Pumping and Hauling in Gaithersburg

    Trap waste is regulated as a liquid waste, so in most places it has to be hauled by a licensed hauler to an approved receiving facility, not dumped down a manhole or into a dumpster.
