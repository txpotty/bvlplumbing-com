    Wall-Hung Commercial Bowl Replacement in Gaithersburg, MD

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the tank, and the bowl is matched to that vessel's flush rate, so a bowl-only swap means finding the exact paired model.     Freeze-Cracked Bowl Replacement in Gaithersburg

    Disconnecting garden hoses and shutting off outdoor spigots before the first hard freeze prevents many burst-pipe calls each winter.
