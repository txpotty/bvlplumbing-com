    Scheduled Sewer Cleaning in Gaithersburg, MD

    History predicts the next backup. Repeat lines get a schedule. Homes with past root problems, older cast iron or a lot of kitchen grease do well on a cleaning every 12 to 24 months, timed before the growth or buildup closes the line.

    Commercial Sewer Cleaning in Gaithersburg, MD

    Commercial lines take a beating. Kitchens put grease into the line daily. Offices and retail buildings deal more with paper towels and foreign objects flushed in public restrooms, which snag on joints and catch everything after them, and our heavy jetters handle 6 inch commercial pipe without much trouble.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Gaithersburg

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Both have weak spots. Clay joints invite roots, and Orangeburg, a tar-paper pipe from the mid-1900s, flattens under the weight of the soil over time, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Gaithersburg

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. A cable only makes a path. Water at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI strips grease, scale and fine roots off the inside of the line and flushes it all toward the street, which leaves the pipe close to its original diameter.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Gaithersburg, MD

    Cast iron rots from the inside. Some older neighborhoods still have lead service lines, so replacing one is a sensible upgrade when other plumbing work is already underway. The bottom of the pipe wears first. Aggressive cutters can break through thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting made for older iron, which restores flow and gives a clear view of the remaining pipe wall on camera.

    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Gaithersburg, MD

    Cleaning cannot fix a broken pipe. It only removes what is inside. If your sewer backs up every few months, the camera usually shows a belly, a crack or a collapsed section, and we compare the yearly cost of cleanings to a spot repair, a liner or a replacement, with the numbers in writing.

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Gaithersburg, MD

    On a repeat backup, the camera goes in first. Guessing costs money. A waterproof color camera on a push rod shows the inside of the line live on a screen, so we can see the clog, measure its distance from the cleanout and spot any crack or sag.
