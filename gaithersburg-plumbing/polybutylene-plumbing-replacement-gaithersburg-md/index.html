    Frequently Asked Questions About Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Gaithersburg

    Most PEX replacements of polybutylene cost $5,000 to $12,000 for an average home. Copper costs more, and yard service lines often add $1,500 to $4,000.

    Gray or blue plastic pipe stamped PB2110 is the main sign. Cracked fittings are a warning. Since poly can look normal right before it splits, a home from roughly 1978 to 1995 with this pipe should plan a replacement even before any leaks.

    For PEX, we use trusted brands like Uponor, Viega and Zurn. Uponor is our usual pick. Copper replacements use Type L instead of thin Type M, since the heavier wall lasts longer.

    Replacing a whole supply system takes special tools, code knowledge and real repipe experience. It takes skill and time. Hidden leaks, failed inspections and voided warranties are common results of DIY repipes, so this one is best left to licensed plumbers.

    Most of our PEX replacements use Uponor AquaPEX with ProPEX expansion fittings. We also use Viega PEX. For copper, we use domestic Type L pipe, and we stick with one brand's pipe and fittings throughout a house so the manufacturer warranty stays valid.

    Both work well in Maryland homes. PEX is flexible, handles freezing better and usually costs less to install. Copper is stiff, long lasting and fine in sun and heat, and we will help you choose based on the house, water and budget.

    A full poly repipe in Gaithersburg, MD usually takes three to seven days. Bigger homes take longer. At the estimate you get a day-by-day plan, including which rooms come first and when patching happens.

    In most cases, yes, a plumbing permit is required for full repiping in Gaithersburg. We take care of it. That includes the application, the rough-in inspection with the walls open and the final sign-off, so you do not have to deal with the paperwork or the scheduling.
