    Call when a plunger does not work. Odors are a sign too. Water backing up into other fixtures, or multiple slow drains usually mean a main line issue. Those need a bigger machine.

    Slow draining is the first sign. Listen for gurgling too. Bad smells from the drain, water coming up in the tub when the toilet flushes and standing water that takes minutes to clear all point to a blockage. If several drains act up together, the main line is the likely problem.
