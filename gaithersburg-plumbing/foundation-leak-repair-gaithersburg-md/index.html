    Locating and Fixing Foundation Leaks in Gaithersburg, MD

    Residential Foundation Leak Repair in Gaithersburg, MD

    For a homeowner in Gaithersburg, MD, the first question is usually how bad it is. A pressure test answers it. A fast drop on the gauge means a big leak that needs work soon, while a slow drop over twenty minutes points to a pinhole and gives you room to weigh options. We also look for the cause, corrosion, ground movement or a bad fitting.

    Water Line Reroute in Gaithersburg

    A reroute leaves the old pipe under the slab and runs new line through the attic or walls. The floor stays whole. PEX is the usual choice since it flexes around framing and doesn't pit, and drywall openings stay small, mostly at fixture walls, for quick patching.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in Gaithersburg

    Many newer homes sit on post-tension slabs with steel cables under thousands of pounds of tension. Cutting one is dangerous. Radar scanning maps the cables before any cut, and when the leak is too close to a tendon, we reroute the line instead.

    PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in Gaithersburg

    Newer houses use PEX or PVC under the slab. It still leaks. Most failures we find are at a fitting that was crimped short or a joint that pulled apart when the soil moved, not in the pipe wall itself. BVL Plumbing makes new crimp, expansion or solvent-weld joints so the line under your Gaithersburg foundation is sound again.

    Foundation Leak Detection in Gaithersburg

    Locating comes before anything else. We split the system first. Hot and cold get tested separately, then we trace the losing line and walk it with the ground mic until the hiss is loudest. Thermal imaging helps verify hot water leaks by showing warm spots on the floor.
