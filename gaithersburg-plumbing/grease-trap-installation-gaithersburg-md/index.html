    What Goes Into a Grease Trap Install in Gaithersburg, MD

    Grease Trap Sizing and Placement in Gaithersburg, MD

    Sizing starts with fixture drain rates and ends with where the trap can physically go. A trap 40 feet from the sink lets grease cool and stick in the pipe before it ever reaches the unit. Closer is better. We also check that a pump truck can reach an outdoor tank with about 100 feet of hose, since a tank the hauler cannot reach costs you more on every service.

    Food Truck Commissary and Ghost Kitchen Traps in Gaithersburg, MD

    Commissary kitchens see a dozen trucks dumping wash water in the same two hours, which is a different load from a restaurant spreading it over a long shift. Sizing has to cover that short, heavy spike. Ghost kitchens with four or five brands under one roof often share a single trap, so the fixture count can be higher than the floor plan suggests. Each brand's prep sink counts.

    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Gaithersburg

    Flow rating is the number that matters on a hydromechanical trap, and it has to match the fastest-draining fixture combination in your kitchen. Put a 50 GPM unit on a 75 GPM load and grease goes out the back at every rush. We do the math on paper. The sink volume and drain-down time give the GPM number, and we show you the calculation.

    Automatic Grease Removal Units in Gaithersburg

    An automatic grease removal unit heats the trapped grease and skims it into a container on a timer, so staff empty a bucket instead of scraping a tank. The purchase price is higher than a passive trap. Plan for a dedicated circuit near the unit, which your electrician will need to run. Fry-heavy kitchens like them most.

    Emergency Grease Trap Services in Gaithersburg, MD

    A grease trap backing up into the floor sinks at the start of Friday dinner service means a closed kitchen and a health inspector who may not let you reopen until it is fixed. We send a tech with a jetter and a camera to find out whether the trap or the line on either side is the problem. Often it is the outlet. A failed trap can often get a stopgap unit that night and a proper replacement later in the week.

    Replacement and Upgrade Services in Gaithersburg, MD

    Steel traps from decades back rust through at the baffle seams, and by then the outlet is usually passing grease straight to the sewer. We run a camera down the outlet first to see how much grease got past the old unit. Sometimes the line needs jetting too. If your seats or menu grew since opening, the swap is the time to step up to a bigger GPM rating.

    Commercial Kitchen Grease Trap Installs in Gaithersburg

    Every commercial install starts with a fixture list. The tech walks the kitchen and writes down every fixture that drains to the trap and how fast each one empties. From there we pick a hydromechanical unit or a gravity tank and figure out where it can sit with a straight shot to the building sewer. Fewer bends, fewer clogs.

    Gravity Grease Trap Installation in Gaithersburg

    A gravity interceptor works on time, not machinery. Wastewater slows down in the tank long enough for grease to float and solids to settle. Sizing comes from your fixture units and the retention time your sewer authority wants, and we check that number with them before ordering a tank. No moving parts to fail.

    Size comes from flow, and flow comes from your fixtures. Outdoor tanks get sized by the sewer authority's formula, which we run for you.
