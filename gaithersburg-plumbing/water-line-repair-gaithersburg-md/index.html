    Underground Leak Detection in Gaithersburg, MD

    A ground microphone picks up the hiss of water escaping a pressurized pipe, even through a couple of feet of dirt. Quiet leaks are harder. For those we push a hydrogen and nitrogen tracer gas into the drained line and sniff for it at the surface, which finds leaks the microphone cannot hear. Clay that swells when wet and shrinks when dry can shift sewer laterals and water service lines over time. You get a written price as soon as the leak is marked on the ground.

    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Gaithersburg

    Copper and PEX fail in different places. Copper tends to pinhole where a rock rubs it or where the water chemistry eats the wall from inside. With PEX, the fitting is the weak point most of the time. We cut out the bad section of copper and sweat or press in a new length, and for PEX we use the matching crimp or expansion tool so the joint is made the way the manufacturer intends.

    Water Pressure Restoration in Gaithersburg

    Low pressure at every fixture points to the service line or the regulator, not the faucets. We put a gauge on a hose bib first. Then we check the pressure reducing valve, the house shutoff and the meter-side valve for a partly closed gate, and look at the pipe itself. Old galvanized can be choked to half its size with rust. The fix might be a new valve or a new line.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Gaithersburg, MD

    One leak on a newer copper or PEX line is a spot repair. Two or more leaks on galvanized is a replacement. Age matters too, since most service lines past 40 years old are living on borrowed time.     Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Gaithersburg, MD

    Galvanized steel service lines rust from the inside out. Once one spot leaks, the pipe on either side is usually just as thin.
