    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Bowie

    A collapsed run or a long belly usually means we dig. Where we can, the sod is cut and set aside before the digging starts. The inspector sees open pipe. We compact the backfill in layers, which keeps a dip from forming along the trench.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Line Replacement in Bowie

    A normal house lateral in Bowie usually costs about $4,000 to $15,000. Long runs, deep taps and lines under slabs or driveways can push the price higher, and trenchless work often falls around $80 to $250 per foot depending on soil and access. The camera tells us which.

    Clogs come back on a schedule when the pipe is the problem. Needing a rooter every few months in an older house usually points to roots or a break. A sinkhole over the line is a bad sign. Get a camera in first.

    The camera tells you. Collapse, open cracks, deep sags and big offsets mean a cleaning will not last. Light roots may just need cutting.

    Trench means one long dig along the whole line. With trenchless, we dig a pit at each end and either burst or line the pipe between them. Trenching can fix a belly.

    HDPE and PVC both hold up well underground. They resist corrosion, household chemicals and roots, which matters a great deal around homes with big trees close to the sewer path. HDPE is jointless.

    Properly bedded PVC or HDPE should last fifty years or more. Compare that with clay joints that leak roots and cast iron that rusts out. Bedding matters most.

    It depends on the yard. Trenchless is better under drives and trees, and trenching is better for bad grade or an open lawn. We price both.

    Backups in more than one drain at once are the big one. Watch for gurgling toilets, sewer smells outside, a lush wet strip of lawn or a dip over the pipe. A camera confirms it.
