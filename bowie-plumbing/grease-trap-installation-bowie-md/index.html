    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in Bowie, MD

    Hydromechanical traps use baffles and a vented flow control to pull grease out of water moving at a set rate, usually 20 to 100 gallons per minute. You can put one under a sink, beside a dish station or in a pit in the slab. The flow control is not optional. Skip it and a full sink drain overruns the rating, which sends grease right past the baffles. We look for units certified to PDI G-101 or ASME A112.14.3.

    Zurn Grease Trap Installation in Bowie

    Zurn's indoor grease interceptors cover the common sizes from small under-sink units up to 100 GPM, and we set enough of them to know where the gaskets give trouble. Most Zurn units come with a flow control fitting, and we mount it on the inlet side at the height the instructions call for.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Bowie
