    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Bowie

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Soils in Maryland range from heavy clay in the central counties to sandy, low-lying ground near the shore, and each affects buried lines differently. Rusted buried steel gets replaced with polyethylene and tracer wire, and we use a listed transition riser where it rises to the house. Exposed sections get coated steel. We test before backfilling so any problem joint is fixed while the trench is still open.

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Bowie, MD

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. We calculate the full load first. The new pipe gets sized so every burner in the house still has full pressure at once.

    Buried Yard Line Repair and Replacement in Bowie, MD

    Many older homes have steel gas lines running from the meter to the house or out to a pool heater. The wrap fails eventually. After the coating breaks down, corrosion starts, and constant moisture from irrigation or poor drainage makes it worse. We call 811 before digging. The new line is polyethylene with tracer wire, or coated steel where it has to be exposed, and we backfill and clean up the yard when the test passes.
