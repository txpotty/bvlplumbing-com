    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Bowie, MD

    CIPP stands for cured-in-place pipe. The crew saturates a felt tube with two-part epoxy on a roller table at the truck, invert it into the cleaned line with air pressure, and hold it against the old pipe wall until the resin hardens. Cure time runs a few hours. The finished liner seals cracks and leaking joints in the original pipe.

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow Trenchless Systems in Bowie

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow both make lining systems we use, each with its own resin chemistry and installation equipment. Both are built to ASTM standards for cured-in-place pipe.     Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Pricing in Bowie, MD

    Compare total cost, not just the plumbing line on the invoice. An open-trench replacement can look cheaper on paper, until you add saw-cutting the driveway and pouring new concrete over a trench that may run 50 feet. Trenchless avoids most of that. Sod and sprinkler repair add up too.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Replacement in Bowie

    Orangeburg is bituminous fiber pipe that was laid in houses mostly from the late 1940s into the early 1970s. It goes oval and blisters, so a liner has nothing round to bond to and bursting is usually the better fix. Clay is different. Clay that still holds its round shape is a good candidate for a liner.
