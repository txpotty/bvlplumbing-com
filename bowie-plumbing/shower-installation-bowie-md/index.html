    How We Install Showers in Bowie, MD

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Bowie

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator within the maker's distance limits, plumb its supply and drain and run the steam line to the head inside the enclosure. The circuit is an electrician's job. The enclosure needs a sealed door and a sloped ceiling.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Bowie

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. A wood floor gets dropped joists or a recessed pan, while a slab gets cut and lowered so the floor flows straight in. A linear drain often works best. We plan the slope with your contractor before demo.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Bowie

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. Valve, head, handheld and drain go in first, then the pan is sloped a quarter inch per foot to the drain. Testing follows. We pressure-test the valve and flood the pan for a day before tile.

    New Construction Shower Installation in Bowie, MD

    New homes get their shower plumbing during framing. We rough in valves, supply lines and drains once the walls are framed, set each valve to the depth of the planned finish and locate the drain for the pan you picked. Then the rough-in inspection. Trim goes on once tile is done.

    Shower Valve and Fixture Installation in Bowie, MD

    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower. Thermostatic valves hold a set temperature and add volume control, which works well with rain heads and handhelds. Either one protects against scalding. We help you choose based on how many outlets you plan.

    Shower Stall Installation in Bowie

    A one-piece stall needs a level, fully supported base. We set fiberglass and acrylic bases in a bed of mortar or setting compound so the floor of the unit does not flex underfoot, since flex is what cracks a base within a couple of years. Then the drain goes in. Flow and temperature get tested after trim.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Bowie, MD

    Prefab units are the fastest route to a new shower. The base goes on mortar, the drain and valve get connected and the walls are fastened so they stay rigid. They suit guest baths well. Most installs finish in a day.

    Custom Shower Enclosure Plumbing in Bowie

    Body sprays and steam units add lines and planning. Every body spray needs a supply line, and the sizing has to be right so the farthest outlet still has good pressure. Pressure varies by house. We test static pressure before designing the layout.
