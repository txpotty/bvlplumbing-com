    Water heaters in garages or attics have exposed supply lines that can freeze. Tankless heaters on outside walls freeze easily. Freeze protection on tankless units needs power, so after an outage we check the heat exchanger and connectors.

    Emergency Burst Pipe Repair in Bowie, MD

    Water spraying from a wall or ceiling is a burst pipe. Close the main shutoff right away. We replace the split section and test the whole branch, since a freeze often cracks more than one spot.

    Pipe Insulation and Heat Cable Installation in Bowie, MD

    Insulation slows heat loss, but it does not add heat. For truly exposed runs, we add heat cable. Self-regulating cable on a GFCI outlet, covered with insulation, keeps the pipe above freezing.

    Thawing Drain Lines and Sewer Pipes in Bowie

    Drain lines can freeze too, usually where a line runs through an unheated crawlspace or an exterior wall. You will see the backup first. Hot water through the cleanout thaws it, and then we check for a sag that holds water.

    Post-Thaw Pipe Inspection and Repair in Bowie, MD
