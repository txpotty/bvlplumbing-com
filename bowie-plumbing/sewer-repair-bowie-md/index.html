    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Bowie, MD

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Two access holes, sometimes one. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall. Either way, the driveway and the trees stay put. We tell you which one your pipe can take.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Bowie, MD

    Roots enter through hairline cracks and loose joints, then grow into a mat that catches paper and grease. They come back fast. We clear the line with a root cutting head or a jetter with a chain flail, camera it afterward to see the condition of each joint, and tell you whether sealing or replacing those joints makes more sense than cleaning every spring. Mature trees in Bowie yards make this a common call.

    Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in Bowie

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease and wipes cause most of it.     Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Bowie

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. A fully collapsed section is one example. We see this most in older neighborhoods with clay pipe. The dig is staged so the house is without a sewer for as little time as possible.
