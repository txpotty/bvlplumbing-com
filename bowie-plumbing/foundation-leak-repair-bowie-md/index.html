    Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in Bowie, MD

    Drain leaks under a slab are harder to find. They aren't pressurized. A hydrostatic test shows whether the line holds water, and a camera run shows us the cracked fitting, offset joint or roots causing it. Fixes include tunneling, slab cuts or a pipe liner, and we lay out the trade-offs.

    PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in Bowie

    Newer houses use PEX or PVC under the slab. It still leaks. Usually the culprit is a fitting that was not crimped all the way or a glued joint that separated when the ground shifted. BVL Plumbing makes new crimp, expansion or solvent-weld joints so the line under your Bowie foundation is sound again.

    Water Line Reroute in Bowie

    A reroute leaves the old pipe under the slab and runs new line through the attic or walls. No concrete gets broken. PEX is the usual choice since it flexes around framing and doesn't pit, and drywall openings stay small, mostly at fixture walls, for quick patching.
