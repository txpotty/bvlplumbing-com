    Pipe Bursting in Bowie, MD

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. It needs two pits. The head fractures the old pipe as it travels, with new HDPE trailing behind, fused into one length with no joints.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Bowie, MD

    Bursting works when the pipe has collapsed or is too broken to line. It also upsizes lines. With an entry and exit pit dug, the winch pulls the head and new HDPE through, replacing the run in a day or two in most homes. BVL Plumbing joins the new pipe by heat fusion.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Bowie, MD

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. The old pipe gets split. As a winch drags the head through, the old pipe fractures into the soil and new HDPE is pulled into the same path behind it. HDPE does not rust or leak at joints in Bowie, MD soil.
