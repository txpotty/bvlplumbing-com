    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Bowie

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Soils in Maryland range from heavy clay in the central counties to sandy, low-lying ground near the shore, and each affects buried lines differently. Meter and pressure tests confirm it, and acoustic gear plus thermal imaging narrow the spot down to a foot or so. Then we decide. Some leaks are best fixed with a small cut through the slab, while others are cheaper to abandon and reroute overhead. We price both so you can compare.
