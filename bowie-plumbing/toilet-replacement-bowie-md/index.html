    What a Toilet Replacement Covers in Bowie, MD

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Bowie, MD

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are noticeably louder than gravity toilets. Replacement means matching the vessel brand for parts later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.

    Water-Saving Toilet Upgrades in Bowie, MD

    Older toilets are one of the largest indoor water users in a house, and replacing a 3.5 gallon model with a 1.28 gallon one cuts toilet water use by more than half. Dual-flush models go lower for liquids. We recommend models with high MaP scores so the savings do not come with double flushing.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Bowie, MD

    Late-night failures happen, usually a tank that splits along a seam or a bowl that cracks at the base and keeps seeping no matter how many towels go down. Closing the angle stop stops the water. A replacement can usually go in first thing the next morning, and we will tell you if the floor needs to dry before we set the new one.

    Comfort-Height Toilet Replacement in Bowie

    Going from a standard bowl to a comfort-height model raises the seat two or three inches, which matters a lot for seniors and anyone recovering from knee or hip surgery. The drain location stays the same. Pair it with a grab bar if you can, and we can anchor that into a stud during the same visit.

    Commercial Toilet Replacement in Bowie, MD

    Office and retail restrooms wear out toilets faster than any house, and a replacement there usually means a floor-mount or wall-hung bowl with a flushometer instead of a tank. We schedule around your hours. Bowls are matched to the flush valve's gallons per flush, since a mismatch leaves waste behind or wastes water.

    Standard Toilet Replacement in Bowie

    Two-piece toilets are what most houses have, and after twenty or thirty years the tank bolts corrode, the porcelain gets crazed and the flush never quite clears the bowl. At that point a swap makes more sense than another repair. We pull the old toilet, scrape the flange and check it for cracks, then set the new one on a fresh wax ring with new closet bolts.

    High-Efficiency Toilet (HET) Upgrades in Bowie, MD

    High-efficiency toilets carry a WaterSense label and a MaP score, which tells you how many grams of waste the toilet clears in one flush. Look for 800 or higher. A high score means fewer double flushes and fewer clogs, and that is where the real water savings come from.

    Emergency Toilet Replacement in Bowie

    A toilet that cracked overnight and is still seeping into the floor cannot wait for next week, so we bring a standard round and elongated model on emergency calls. First, close the valve behind the toilet. Most emergency swaps are done the same day, and we check the subfloor for soaked plywood before setting it.
