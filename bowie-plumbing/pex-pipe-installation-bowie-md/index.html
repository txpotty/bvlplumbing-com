    Full Home PEX Repiping in Bowie

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside out. Give it four or five decades and the inside is so packed with rust that a half-inch pipe barely flows, and the first draw in the morning runs brown. A full PEX repipe replaces all of it. You get real pressure back, and the discolored water goes away with the old pipe.

    Commercial PEX Pipe Installation in Bowie

    Commercial PEX work in Bowie gets scheduled around your hours. For a small office, a restaurant restroom or a retail tenant space, we can stage the work after closing so the water is only off when nobody needs it. Bigger sizes join the same way. Larger jobs may call for one-inch or bigger trunk lines and commercial-grade manifolds.

    Emergency PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Bowie, MD

    PEX rarely fails on its own. When it does, the cause is usually a nail or screw driven through it, a rodent chewing on an attic run, or a fitting that was never crimped all the way. Our trucks stock half-inch to one-inch pipe and fittings. Most emergency PEX repairs in Bowie, MD come down to a cut and a splice, usually under an hour after the wall is open.

    Whole-Home PEX Repiping in Bowie

    Low pressure at every faucet, across the whole house, usually points at the pipe and not the fixtures. In an older house that usually means rust-choked galvanized or polybutylene with tired fittings, and a new faucet cartridge will not touch it. Repiping does fix it. Every line from the main valve to each angle stop gets replaced, and the system is tested before anything gets closed up.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Bowie

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. It is a five-second check. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Bowie, MD

    With PEX-A, the tech slips an expansion ring onto the pipe and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting is pushed in while the pipe is open, and the plastic's memory shrinks it back around the barb, sealing tighter during the first minute. In the cold, it takes longer to shrink back. We let joints set before testing.

    PEX Main Water Line Installation in Bowie

    The main from the meter to the house is the one line everything else depends on. When an old galvanized or polybutylene service starts leaking underground, the yard stays soggy, the water bill climbs, and the pressure drops at every tap. We trench and lay new PEX. At the foundation we install a new full-port ball valve as the main shutoff.
