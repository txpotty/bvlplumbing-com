    Dripping Faucet Repair in Bowie

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Bowie

    Check the slip nuts first. Overtightened plastic slip nuts crack, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until each joint weeps. We replace the trap as one kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Emergency Sink Repair in Bowie, MD

    Shut the angle stops first. Turn the two valves under the sink to the right, and if they will not hold, close the main shutoff for the house and then call. We send active leaks to the front of the line. The plumber brings stops and supply lines to stop it on the first visit.

    Bathroom Sink Repair in Bowie, MD

    Bath sinks have small parts that wear. The pop-up linkage sits in a damp cabinet for years, and the pivot nut where the rod enters the drain is the most common leak we find on a bathroom sink. We check pedestal and wall-hung sinks the same way. Vanity sinks just hide the leak better.

    Pop-Up Stopper Repair in Bowie, MD

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. The clevis strap slips on the lift rod, the pivot ball loses its seal, or hair wraps the stopper so tightly it cannot seat on the flange. Half the time, a cleaning does it. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Bowie, MD

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. After five to ten years the core can split without warning, and old multi-turn stops often seize open. We swap both in one visit. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Repair or Replace? Sink Costs in Bowie, MD

When it is close, you get both prices. Some people repair now and remodel next year.
