    Sewer Cleaning Work in Bowie, MD

    Residential Sewer Cleaning in Bowie

    Household sewers clog slowly. Grease, hair and wipes build up over months. Each flush adds a little more to the pile at a rough joint or a low spot, until the 4 inch line is down to a trickle and the lowest drain in the house starts to back up, and we clear it with a sectional cable or a small jetter sized for the pipe.

    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Bowie, MD

    Grease hardens as it cools. Then it narrows the pipe. A greasy main slowly loses diameter until it cannot carry a full washer load or a bathtub draining at once, and jetting is the right fix.

    Emergency Sewer Backup Cleaning in Bowie

    Sewage coming up through a shower or floor drain? Shut off the washer and dishwasher. A tech arrives ready to clear the main and start cleanup, with the goal of restoring flow before anything else, then we disinfect the area and take photos for an insurance claim.

    After-Hours Sewer Backups in Bowie

    Backups happen at bad times. We answer anyway. The after-hours rate is quoted on the phone before we dispatch, and the tech arrives ready to clear the main, set up a wet vac and photograph the damage for your insurance company.

    Root Removal From Sewer Lines in Bowie

    Trees send roots toward any moisture they can find. Leaky joints feed them all year. We use a sectional cable with a root saw sized to the pipe to cut the mass free and pull it out, then follow with a jetter if fine root hairs are left behind, and a camera shows where roots are entering so you can choose between lining and repair.

    Preventative Sewer Maintenance in Bowie

    Roots come back after cutting. Usually within a year or so. A scheduled cleaning every year or two keeps roots and grease from closing the line and lets you plan around it.

    Jetting a Sewer Line in Bowie, MD

    Grease and scale are jetting work. A cable only scratches them. The rear jets drive the hose forward while the spray scours the wall, so the line ends up much closer to its original size.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Bowie

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Both have weak spots. Clay joints invite roots, and Orangeburg, a tar-paper pipe from the mid-1900s, flattens under the weight of the soil over time, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.
