    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Baltimore

    Hard water builds scale on tankless heating elements, which cuts efficiency and can trigger error codes. Most municipal water across Maryland runs moderately hard, so scale slowly builds inside water heaters, faucet aerators and showerheads over the years. We recommend a softener or a scale inhibitor ahead of the unit, and every install gets service valves so the heater can be flushed with a descaling solution once a year. Flushing takes an hour.
