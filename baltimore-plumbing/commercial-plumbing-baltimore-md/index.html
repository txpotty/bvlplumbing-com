    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Baltimore

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Soils in Maryland range from heavy clay in the central counties to sandy, low-lying ground near the shore, and each affects buried lines differently. We confirm it with a meter test and pressure test, then use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to pinpoint the leak within a foot or two. Then we decide. A spot repair through the floor makes sense for some, and an overhead reroute makes more sense for others. You get a price on each option.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in Baltimore, MD

    Big tank heaters fail in a few common ways: a failed gas valve, a cracked flue baffle, a stuck T and P valve or a tank full of scale. Each sounds different. We check draft and combustion, test water temperature at the far end of the building and flush the tank, then tell you whether repair or replacement makes more sense. Tankless racks get descaled. Recirculation pumps on long runs get tested as part of the visit.

    Commercial Restroom Plumbing in Baltimore

    A broken flushometer in a busy restroom gets noticed by every customer who walks in. We repair and replace Sloan and Zurn flush valves, sensor faucets, wall-hung toilets and carriers. We also handle ADA upgrades. That includes setting fixtures at the right heights, adding blocking for grab bars and wrapping exposed drain lines under lavatories.

    Grease Trap Installation in Baltimore

    A new restaurant or a kitchen expansion usually needs a grease interceptor sized from the fixtures that drain to it. Under-sink traps work for small kitchens, while larger operations need in-ground interceptors of 500 gallons or more. Sizing is set by code. We size the unit from the equipment schedule, set it at the right grade and vent it properly so it passes inspection the first time.

    Kitchen and Floor Drain Cleaning in Baltimore

    Floor drains in commercial kitchens collect grease, rice, mop water and the odd utensil. They clog on a schedule you can almost set your watch by. We open the line with a sectional cable, then jet the 2-inch and 3-inch branches so the grease does not just close back up in a week. Trap primers get checked too.

    Multi-Family and Property Management Plumbing in Baltimore

    Apartment complexes need a plumber who can handle many units and quick turns between tenants. We repair leaks, replace water heaters, clear stacks that serve several floors and repipe whole buildings while keeping residents informed about every shutoff. Pricing stays consistent by unit. Invoices list each unit number, so your accounting team can bill back to the right account.

    Commercial Restroom Fixture Repair in Baltimore, MD

    Restroom calls are the most common commercial work we do. Running flush valves, stuck urinals and dripping sensor faucets usually need a diaphragm, a solenoid or fresh batteries. We stock those. Clogged wall-hung toilets sometimes need the carrier checked, because a loose bowl on a carrier can leak behind the wall. Weak flow at one sink often comes down to a plugged stop valve.

    Commercial Pipe Repair and Repiping in Baltimore, MD

    Older commercial buildings often have galvanized supply lines that are rusted nearly shut, or copper that has pinholed along hot water runs. Patching works for a while. Once leaks start showing up in different spots every few months, a sectional repipe in Type L copper or commercial-grade PEX usually costs less than the next three emergency calls. The first step is mapping the risers and branches. Then we phase the work by floor or by tenant so the building stays open.
