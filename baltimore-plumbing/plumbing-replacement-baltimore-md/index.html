    Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Baltimore, MD

    Switching to tankless when the old tank fails is common in Baltimore, MD, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. The gas draw can be four times a 40-gallon tank, so the meter line usually gets upsized, and the venting moves from B-vent to stainless or PVC. We price both up front.

    Sump Pump and Well Pump Replacement in Baltimore, MD

    A dead sump pump on a wet night means water on the floor, and a dead well pump means no water at all. In Baltimore, MD, sump pumps get sized for inflow with a check valve and an optional battery backup, while well pumps get matched to depth, flow and switch settings. We run each one through cycles.

    Water Line Replacement in Baltimore, MD

    A leaking water service line wastes water around the clock and can soften the soil next to the foundation. In Baltimore, MD, we swap old galvanized, poly or failing copper service lines for PEX or copper from meter to house, plus a new full-port main shutoff. One day covers most.

    Sewer Line Replacement in Baltimore

    Sewer lines in Baltimore fail from roots, cracks, collapses and bellies where the pipe sank and now holds waste. Clay that swells when wet and shrinks when dry can shift sewer laterals and water service lines over time. A camera inspection comes first. That footage shows exactly where the line failed and how much of it is bad, so you know whether a spot repair, a trenchless liner or a full replacement makes the most sense for your yard.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Baltimore

    A well pump that clicks on and off every few seconds usually points to a failed pressure tank. A ruptured bladder lets the tank waterlog, the air cushion disappears, and the pump starts with every tap, wearing the motor out years early. A new tank fixes it. We also check the switch.

    Repair vs Replacement Cost Comparison in Baltimore, MD

    A common rule of thumb is to replace when a repair costs more than half the price of new, or when the part is near the end of its life. Take a 12-year-old heater with a bad gas valve, where the valve alone costs a third of a new unit and the tank is already near the end. You get both numbers.

    Water Softener Replacement in Baltimore, MD

    Softeners wear out as the resin breaks down and the control valve stops cycling properly. A water test is a smart first step around Baltimore before choosing a treatment system, because hardness and minerals vary from one supply to the next. Once scale returns, dishes spot or salt stops dropping, we test hardness, size a new unit and plumb it with a bypass so the house keeps water during service. Salt use often drops too.

Tank leaks are not repairable. We match the fuel and size, update the venting and add an expansion tank or drain pan if code calls for one. Hot water is back by evening.
