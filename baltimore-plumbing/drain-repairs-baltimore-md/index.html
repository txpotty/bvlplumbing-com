    Clogged Drain Repair in Baltimore

    Grease, hair, food scraps and mineral scale cause most clogs. Some come back fast.     Main Line Drain Repair in Baltimore

    A problem in the main line shows up at every drain in the house. Basement and first-floor drains go first.     Kitchen Branch Line Repair in Baltimore

    This branch takes more grease and food than any other line in most homes. The grease goes far down the pipe.     Vent Pipe Repair in Baltimore

    Sewer gas leaking into a room can point to a missing vent. Old renovations often skipped it. Bird nests, debris and roofing work can block existing vents, while fixtures that never had a vent can sometimes get an air admittance valve if code allows. Good venting keeps all trap seals intact.

    Outdoor Drain Repair in Baltimore

    Downspout lines and yard drains fail without much warning. Water pools at the house. We clear the line, camera it for crushed or disconnected sections, and repair broken runs so downspout water gets carried away from the footing. We check the grade at the outlet too.

    Exterior Drain Tile Repair in Baltimore, MD

    Drain tile, sometimes called a French drain, carries groundwater away from the footing. It clogs with silt. When a tile line is crushed, clogged or was laid without fabric, water builds up against the foundation, so we camera the tile, find the failed section and replace it with perforated pipe wrapped in filter fabric and clean gravel. The outlet gets checked as well.
