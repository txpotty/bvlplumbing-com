    Understanding Backflow Preventer Installation Costs in Baltimore, MD

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Baltimore, MD

    Pressure vacuum breakers are the low-cost answer for lawn irrigation in Baltimore, MD. They let air in when supply pressure drops, which breaks the siphon, but they only protect against back-siphonage, so they must sit above every downstream outlet and cannot be used where back pressure is possible. We check clearance and drainage too.

    Pool and Spa Fill Backflow Protection in Baltimore, MD

    Pool auto-fill lines and hose connections give chlorinated water a straight path back to the supply. The fill line gets the correct backflow device, check that every hose bibb has a vacuum breaker, and can fold the work into a pool build or remodel so it is done before the deck goes in. Hose bibb breakers are cheap.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Baltimore

    Double check assemblies work for low-hazard connections such as many fire lines and some irrigation. Two spring-loaded checks and four test cocks are inside, and since they do not discharge water, they can go in a vault or below grade where local rules allow, sized so the pressure loss stays small. Flow drives the size.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in Baltimore, MD

    High-hazard connections in Baltimore, MD call for a reduced pressure zone assembly. Think chemical injection or medical equipment, where a backflow event could put something toxic in the supply, so the RPZ adds a relief valve between two checks that dumps water if either check leaks. It has to drain somewhere.
