    Line Jetting Services in Baltimore, MD

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Baltimore, MD

    Restaurant kitchen lines send warm grease downstream where it cools and sticks. By the time it reaches the interceptor, a lot of it has already coated the pipe. Short jetting passes keep loosened grease from plugging the line downstream. Quarterly works for most.

    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Baltimore, MD

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. A jet scours the entire pipe wall. For a single clog in an otherwise clean line, snaking is cheaper and works fine, but for a line that clogs every few months, jetting removes the buildup that keeps catching debris.

    Residential Hydrojetting in Baltimore

    Most home jetting calls involve a greasy kitchen line or a root-filled sewer. Whenever possible, we work from the outside cleanout and keep the hose out of the house. Some kitchen lines get jetted from under the sink. We lay down floor protection first.

    Emergency Hydrojet Services in Baltimore, MD

    A full sewer backup cannot wait for a weekday slot. We clear it first. A cable opens it, then the jetter removes the grease, roots or wipes that caused it.

    Same-Day Jetting for Backups in Baltimore

    Sewage in a tub or floor drain means the main is blocked. Stop using water first. We open the line, jet out the cause, and camera it to see if a repair is needed.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Baltimore

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. A cable cuts a channel through it. A spinning nozzle removes the grease all around the pipe so it flushes away cleanly.

    Storm and Yard Drain Jetting in Baltimore, MD

    Yard drains, channel drains and downspout lines fill with sand, silt and leaves between storms. Low-lying waterfront areas also face tidal flooding, which can surcharge drains and affect septic systems near the shore. A clogged yard drain sends that water back toward the house, so we jet the lines from the outlet end and flush the catch basins before the wet season starts. Pop-up emitters get checked too.

    Hydrojetting for Shops and Offices in Baltimore, MD

    Retail and office buildings have long horizontal drain runs that collect buildup. Flat runs collect more debris. After-hours jetting keeps equipment out of hallways while tenants are working.
