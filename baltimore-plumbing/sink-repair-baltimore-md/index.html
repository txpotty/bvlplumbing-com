    Garbage Disposal Repair in Baltimore

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is usually jammed. From below we turn the flywheel with a 1/4 inch hex key, hit the reset button and look for a bent impeller. Leaks are a different story. A cracked housing means a new disposal, usually an InSinkErator or a Waste King.

    Commercial Sink Repair in Baltimore

    Restaurants and salons cannot wait a week for a sink. Three-compartment sinks and hand sinks have to work for the health inspector, so we schedule around your hours and bring commercial-grade faucets and basket strainers. Grease is the usual enemy. We also check the grease interceptor connection when the drain backs up.

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in Baltimore

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. That points to worn O-rings on the spout or a bad base gasket, and sometimes to a cracked faucet body that cannot be rebuilt. Every faucet type rebuilds differently. We identify yours before ordering parts.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Baltimore

    Check the slip nuts first. Plastic slip nuts crack when they are overtightened with pliers, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until water seeps out at every joint along the trap. The whole trap gets replaced as a kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Pop-Up Stopper Repair in Baltimore, MD

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. Either the clevis slipped on the lift rod or hair has wrapped the stopper so it will not seat. Half the time, a cleaning does it. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.

    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Baltimore

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. Disconnecting garden hoses and shutting off outdoor spigots before the first hard freeze prevents many burst-pipe calls each winter. UV light cracks plastic traps and supply lines left outdoors, and dirt from the yard clogs the drain. We fix leaks at the hose bib too. A frost-free sillcock is worth it on exposed walls.

    Emergency Sink Repair in Baltimore, MD

    Shut the angle stops first. Turn the two valves under the sink to the right, and if they will not hold, close the main shutoff for the house and then call. We send active leaks to the front of the line. The plumber brings stops and supply lines to stop it on the first visit.

    Cracked or Loose Basin Repair in Baltimore

    Undermount clips let go more often than people think. When the epoxy under the counter fails, the bowl drops a quarter inch, the caulk line splits, and water from the counter runs straight down into the cabinet. New clips go in and the bowl gets pulled tight. Hairline cracks in porcelain near the drain mean replacement.
