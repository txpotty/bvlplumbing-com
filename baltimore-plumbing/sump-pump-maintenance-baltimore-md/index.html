    Pit Cleaning and Liner Checks in Baltimore

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, leaves and gravel settle at the bottom of the basin. That debris gets pulled into the intake and jams the impeller.     Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Baltimore, MD

    Float trouble causes more no-start calls than anything else. A tethered float can tangle on the pump body or catch on the side of the basin. Vertical floats stick when mineral crust builds up on the rod. In tight pits where nothing has room to swing, we can switch you to an electronic sensor switch with no moving float at all.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Baltimore, MD

    Grinding, clicking and a hum with no water moving each point to a different failure. We start with the outlet and the cord, then the float, then the motor. A tripped GFCI is a quick fix. A seized motor calls for a new pump, so we carry replacements and never leave the pit unprotected overnight.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Service in Baltimore, MD

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the water, so the wear shows up in the shaft, the bearings and the float rod. We check the rod for bends and the stops for slipping. Bearings get a listen. A motor that whines or rattles on startup is usually close to done.
