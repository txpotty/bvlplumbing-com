    What Our Baltimore, MD Sewer Crews Handle

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Baltimore

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. Cabling just opens a hole. A jetter sends high-pressure water around the full circumference of the pipe, removing buildup that a cable would leave behind.

    Video Inspection Before Cleaning in Baltimore, MD

    Some lines need a look first. Fragile pipe gets the camera before the cutter. A camera run before cleaning shows whether the line has roots, a broken section or a grease plug, which tells us whether to cable, jet or stop and talk about repair.

    Commercial Sewer Cleaning in Baltimore, MD

    Commercial lines take a beating. Restaurants fight grease every day. In offices and shops, paper and odd objects flushed in restrooms are the usual cause of a stopped lateral, and our heavy jetters handle 6 inch commercial pipe without much trouble.

    Apartment and Strip Center Sewers in Baltimore, MD

    Shared laterals carry heavy loads. Many tenants use a single line. We set up jetting on a schedule that fits your pump-out cycle and do the work when the building is quiet, with a written report and a video file on request.

    Clogged Main Sewer Line Solutions in Baltimore, MD

    Several drains backing up at once is a main line problem. One pipe, many fixtures. After the line is open, a camera run tells us if the blockage was debris or something structural that will keep catching waste.

    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Baltimore, MD

    Cleaning cannot fix a broken pipe. It only removes what is inside. When a line keeps backing up, we put the cost of regular cleaning next to the cost of a repair so you can see which makes sense, with the numbers in writing.

    After-Hours Sewer Backups in Baltimore

    Backups happen at bad times. We answer anyway. The after-hours rate is quoted on the phone before we dispatch, and the tech arrives ready to clear the main, set up a wet vac and photograph the damage for your insurance company.
