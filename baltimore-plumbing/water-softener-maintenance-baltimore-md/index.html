    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in Baltimore, MD

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. The beads turn rusty brown and stop grabbing hardness. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we set the valve to regenerate more often so iron cannot load the bed again.

    Emergency Softener Repairs in Baltimore, MD

    A softener stuck in backwash can run water to the drain all night, and a cracked brine tank can empty across the floor. Both are same-day problems. First, put the valve in bypass so the house still has water, then call (443) 719-1650 and we will get a plumber out.

    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repairs in Baltimore, MD

    A bypass valve that does not seat fully lets raw water blend into the softened supply, and the hardness test is usually the first clue. We rebuild or replace the bypass and check the drain line for kinks. Code requires an air gap at the drain.

    Softener Maintenance Plans and Pricing in Baltimore, MD

    The plan is one flat yearly fee. It covers the service visit and both hardness tests, and any parts we install during plan visits are billed at a lower rate. For most homes it pays for itself the first time it catches a stuck float.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Service in Baltimore

    Salt-free conditioners, usually template assisted crystallization units, do not remove hardness. A hardness test still reads high afterward. They turn the hardness into microscopic crystals that mostly pass through without sticking. The media needs replacing every three to five years, and the sediment prefilter ahead of it needs changing every few months, which is the part most owners skip.

    Commercial Softener Service in Baltimore

    Commercial units work harder. Restaurants and laundromats can burn through a softener's capacity in a single day, so twin-tank systems swap tanks, keeping one in service while the other regenerates. We set a service schedule around your hours so the water never goes hard during a rush.

    Hardness Testing at the Tap in Baltimore

    We test with a titration kit, counting drops until the sample changes color, because test strips are too rough for setting a valve. Raw water gets tested from an unsoftened hose bib or with the valve on bypass. The softened sample comes from a kitchen faucet.

    Full-System Softener Inspections in Baltimore

    An inspection covers more than the two tanks. The inlet and outlet piping, bypass, drain line and brine overflow all get traced and checked. Green crust on copper fittings, a drip at the bypass or a drain line jammed down into a standpipe all go on the written report.
