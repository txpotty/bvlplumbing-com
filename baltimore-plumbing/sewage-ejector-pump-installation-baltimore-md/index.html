    What Goes Into an Ejector Install in Baltimore, MD

    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in Baltimore, MD

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for a very long time, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements. We install Zoeller for both new basins and drop-in replacements, and its standard models often fit existing pits and discharge piping without changes. Parts are easy to find.

    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Baltimore, MD

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes often need duplex systems with an alternating control panel. Alternating the lead pump spreads wear, and when one fails or flow spikes, the second starts on its own and the panel sounds an alarm. Two pumps, one alarm.

    Upflush and Macerating Toilet Systems in Baltimore

    Macerating systems like Saniflo let you add a toilet and sink without breaking the concrete floor. It sits behind the toilet, grinds waste and pumps it through a small line, often three-quarter or one inch, to a drain above, which suits a small half bath. They need a vent too.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin & Pit Installation in Baltimore

    Digging the pit starts with a saw cut through the floor, deep enough to set the basin flush with the finished floor. Soils in Maryland range from heavy clay in the central counties to sandy, low-lying ground near the shore, and each affects buried lines differently. We bed the basin on gravel and backfill around it so it cannot float when groundwater rises, then pour new concrete to seal the floor and lock the basin in place. A floating basin breaks pipe.

    Liberty Pumps Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Baltimore, MD

    Liberty builds single and dual pump packages that arrive with the basin, pump, lid and fittings matched. Matched packages speed up the install, and Liberty's float design is less likely to snag on the basin wall, a common reason older pumps fail to start. Switch failures cause many calls.

    Commercial Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Baltimore, MD

    Businesses in Baltimore, MD with restrooms, kitchens or break rooms below grade need ejector systems that keep up with heavier, steadier use than a house. Commercial setups often run duplex pumps with an alternating panel, so one pump can fail and the other keeps the restrooms open. Downtime costs money. Each pump can carry the load alone.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin Installation in Baltimore, MD

    The basin is where the system lives, and a 30-gallon size is common for a single basement bathroom. A larger basin means longer, less frequent cycles that spare the motor and switch, so we go bigger when the pit takes a laundry or several fixtures. Lids must be gas-tight.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Baltimore

    Grinder pumps chop waste into a fine slurry first, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. They are the right choice when the sewer is far away, uphill or under pressure, like on a pressure sewer system where the pump feeds a shared force main in the street. They cost more. For a basement bath near the drain, a standard ejector usually works.
