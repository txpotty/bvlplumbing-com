    Control Valve Rebuild in Baltimore

    Service, backwash, brine and rinse are the four cycles the control valve directs every time the softener regenerates, usually in the middle of the night. Over the years, seals, spacers and pistons wear down. Common signs are leaks at the head or constant drain flow. A matching rebuild kit fixes it.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Baltimore, MD

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Its small ports clog with sediment and salt. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. The injector and screen get cleaned or replaced, and the brine line gets checked.

    Resin Testing and Replacement in Baltimore

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Chlorine and chloramine in city water slowly break resin down until it turns to mush. We test resin capacity, then clean it with resin cleaner or replace it, and a solid tank and valve with fresh resin works like new for years. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Water Softener Leak Detection & Repair in Baltimore, MD

    Cracked tanks, loose fittings and failed O-rings are behind most softener leaks. Even a slow drip wastes water and soaks the floor. Once we find the source, from the bypass to the drain connection, the failed seal or fitting gets replaced. Then it gets a pressure test.

    Water Softener Leak Repair in Baltimore, MD

    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Baltimore, MD
