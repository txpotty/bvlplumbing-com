    What Goes Into a PEX Job in Baltimore, MD

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Baltimore, MD

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A bends the easiest and uses expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and uses crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C handles more like B. All three are code-listed for drinking water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.

    Leak Repair with PEX in Baltimore, MD

    A single bad section is not always a reason to repipe. For one split or pitted stretch of copper, we cut it out, transition to PEX with a listed fitting, and usually restore water within a couple of hours. That works for one leak. By the third leak, though, we will talk to you honestly about whether the rest of that copper is close behind.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Baltimore, MD

    Opening a wall for a kitchen remodel is the cheapest time to swap out the old pipe. With the studs bare, we can run PEX back to a manifold or to the nearest good trunk, set new stub-outs for the sink and dishwasher, and add a line for the fridge ice maker. Additions get their own sizing. A new bath at the far end of the house may need a three-quarter inch feed to keep pressure up.

    PEX for Water Line Replacement in Baltimore

    Your service line runs from the meter or well to the house, often a foot and a half to three feet underground. Because PEX comes on a long coil, the whole run from the meter to the foundation can go in with zero buried fittings. Basements are common in much of the state, while homes on the low coastal plain more often sit on crawlspaces or slabs because of a high water table. It gets bedded in sand, not rocky backfill. Rock pressing on the pipe is the usual cause of an early failure.

    Emergency PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Baltimore, MD

    PEX rarely fails on its own. When it does, the cause is usually a nail or screw driven through it, a rodent chewing on an attic run, or a fitting that was never crimped all the way. Our trucks stock half-inch to one-inch pipe and fittings. Most emergency PEX repairs in Baltimore, MD come down to a cut and a splice, usually under an hour after the wall is open.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Baltimore

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. It is a five-second check. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Baltimore, MD

    Gray polybutylene went into millions of homes from the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. Chlorinated water breaks the pipe and its plastic fittings down from within, which means it can look perfectly fine until a fitting suddenly fails. Insurers ask about it. Swapping it for PEX can help with insurance and with resale.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Baltimore, MD

    With PEX-A, the tech slips an expansion ring onto the pipe and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting is pushed in while the pipe is open, and the plastic's memory shrinks it back around the barb, sealing tighter during the first minute. Cold weather slows it. We let joints set before testing.
