    Electric and Gas Tankless Repair in Baltimore, MD

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Repair in Baltimore, MD

    Noritz heaters hold up well, but their flow sensors and vent connections still wear out. A code 90 points to combustion trouble, often a dirty fan or a restricted intake. Our tech cleans the burner and fan, checks every vent joint, and compares the flow sensor reading to the spec sheet. Gaskets get replaced if they leak.

    Navien Condensate and Fan Repair in Baltimore

    Navien's E016 is a heat exchanger overheat, and scale or a backed-up condensate line is usually behind it. We also read fan speed from the service menu and compare it to spec, since a fan that cannot reach target RPM will cause flame and exhaust faults on the same unit. We clean the trap and screen, then retest.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Repair in Baltimore, MD

    Whole-house electric tankless units pull a lot of current, often 100 to 150 amps on two or three dedicated breakers. A breaker that trips under load can mean a shorted element or a loose lug that has been arcing, and both get tested with a meter before anything is replaced. Element resistance and supply voltage get measured. Scorched wire gets cut back.

    Commercial Tankless Repair in Baltimore, MD

    Hotels and gyms burn through hot water all day, so their tankless racks scale up two or three times faster than a home unit. We service rack systems with manifold kits, pulling the fault log and testing the flow sensor on every heater in the rack, and set a descaling schedule by run hours. Quarterly is common.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Repair in Baltimore

    Restaurants and laundromats in Baltimore often run two to six tankless units on a common manifold. When one unit drops out, the rest carry the load and scale up sooner, so we check the cascade controller, the isolation valves on each heater and the water quality coming in. A down kitchen loses money. We schedule around service hours when we can.

    Igniter and Flame Rod Repair in Baltimore, MD

    Gas tankless heaters light with a hot surface or spark igniter, then prove flame with a flame rod. A rod coated in oxide reads low on a microamp meter and the board shuts the gas valve after a few seconds. We clean or replace the rod, check the igniter for cracks or weak spark, and confirm the gas valve opens on call. Five-minute fix sometimes.

    Electric Tankless Element Repair in Baltimore, MD

    Electric units fail in their own way. Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart models use two to four heating elements in separate chambers, and one burned element means weak heat at high flow, while a failed flow switch means no heat at all. We check each element's resistance, measure voltage under load and check amperage on each leg, then confirm the panel and wire size match the unit's rating.

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Baltimore

    Rinnai shows an LC code and Navien posts a scale warning when the exchanger runs hot from mineral buildup. Clear the code without flushing and it comes right back, because the scale is still in there. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves, run descaler through the exchanger for about an hour, then flush and clean the inlet screen. Yearly is right for most hard-water homes.
