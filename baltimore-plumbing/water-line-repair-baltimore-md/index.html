    Main Water Line Replacement in Baltimore, MD

    Replacement beats repair once a line has leaked two or three times, or when it is galvanized or polybutylene. Patches on those materials rarely hold. We run new Type K copper or PEX from the meter to the house, usually in one day with a trench or a trenchless pull. We pull the permit, set up the inspection and pressure test the new line before it is covered.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Baltimore

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. Straight runs are ideal. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, while new HDPE comes through right behind the head. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Baltimore, MD

    One leak on a newer copper or PEX line is a spot repair. Two or more leaks on galvanized is a replacement. Age matters too, since most service lines past 40 years old are living on borrowed time. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars while a full replacement costs more and resets the clock for decades. We show you the pipe we exposed.

    Main Water Line Repair in Baltimore

    Your main line is the pipe from the meter to the house, usually 3/4 inch or 1 inch, buried anywhere from a foot to several feet down depending on frost depth. When it fails you tend to see wet ground, a spinning meter and weak pressure at every tap. We start by closing the house shutoff and watching the dial. If it still moves, the leak is buried. Then a ground microphone narrows the spot, so the crew opens one small pit rather than trenching the whole run.

    Commercial Water Line Repair in Baltimore, MD

    Commercial service lines are often 1 1/2 to 4 inch pipe feeding a meter bank, a backflow assembly and sometimes a fire line. A break shuts the business down. BVL Plumbing can schedule repairs early in the morning or after closing, with large-diameter couplings and fittings already loaded. We also arrange the backflow test and inspection that most water providers require after work on the commercial side of the meter.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Baltimore, MD

    PEX itself rarely fails underground, but the joints and the first few feet near the house take a beating. Rats and gophers sometimes chew it. A shovel or a fence post auger can nick it, and sunlight exposure before burial weakens the plastic over time. We cut out the damaged section and install new pipe with crimp, clamp or expansion fittings, matched to the PEX brand already in the ground.

    Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Baltimore, MD

    A full break can flood the yard and leave the house dry at the same time. Shutting the water off comes first. The crew pumps the hole, finds the break and decides whether a coupling will do or whether a section needs replacing, and if the repair will run past a few hours we set a temporary bypass to the house. Most breaks are fixed the same day. We photograph the damage for your insurance or HOA.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Baltimore, MD

    The meter box is where a lot of leaks start. Compression fittings there work loose over time. Before we touch anything, we figure out whether the leak is before or after the meter, since the utility is in charge of the meter and the pipe feeding it. When it is on your side, we repair it. That often means new fittings and a new customer shutoff valve on the same visit.
