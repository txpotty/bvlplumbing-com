    Burst Copper Pipe Repair in Baltimore, MD

    Copper in an exterior wall or attic can split when the water inside freezes. Pipes in exterior walls, unheated crawlspaces and garages are the most common trouble spots when a deep cold snap settles in. Close the main valve. The split often does not leak until the ice thaws, which is why many bursts are found hours after the cold. We cut out the split and solder in new pipe. Then we add insulation.

    Copper Fitting and Valve Repair in Baltimore

    Angle stops, shutoff valves and copper fittings under sinks and behind toilets can weep or seize. Scale locks them up. Hard water buildup inside a valve can keep it from closing when you need it most, and quarter-turn valves go in to replace the old multi-turn stops. We test each one.

    Preventive Copper Pipe Inspection in Baltimore, MD

    An inspection catches thin spots before they leak. Plan on an hour. We look at exposed copper for green crust and pitting, test static pressure, check the bond wire at the water heater and main, and run a thermal scan on walls where pipe runs if the house has had leaks before. You get a written summary.

    Preventive Copper Pipe Maintenance in Baltimore, MD

    Copper gives warning signs before it leaks. They are easy to overlook. Green crust at fittings, a stain on a basement ceiling and high static pressure all point to trouble ahead, so we look over the pipe you can see and test the pressure. A regulator often helps.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Baltimore

    Pinholes start inside the pipe where the copper wall pits and thins. The first sign is often a spot. They are small and easy to miss behind drywall, so we locate them with a thermal camera and moisture meter before cutting a single opening in the wall or ceiling. We replace the pitted length with Type L. Nearby pipe gets checked.

    Hot Water Copper Line Repair in Baltimore, MD

    Hot water lines corrode faster than cold lines. Heat speeds it up. Recirculation pumps that run around the clock push water through the copper at high velocity and wear it from the inside, which is why we often add a timer to the pump after fixing the leak. Less run time, less wear.

    Solder Joint Leak Repair in Baltimore, MD

    Older solder joints can fail from poor original work, thermal expansion or vibration. Look for white or green crust. A leaking joint usually weeps for a while before it drips, so we either resolder it or replace the fitting and a short length of pipe. The joint gets tested.

    Copper Slab Leak Repair in Baltimore

    Many slab homes have copper water lines running under the concrete. Sometimes you hear it. When those lines leak, you may notice warm spots on the floor, the sound of running water or a meter that turns with every fixture off, so we find it electronically first and then quote a slab repair or a reroute. We price each option.
