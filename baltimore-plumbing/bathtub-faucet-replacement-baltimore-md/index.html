    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Baltimore, MD

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. A new valve makes sense for discontinued parts, a cracked body or an old faucet with no scald protection. We quote both options. Most people choose repair first.

    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Baltimore, MD

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We replace Moen and Delta cartridges or old compression stems and seats, and we grease the O-rings with silicone plumber's grease so the handle turns smoothly for years. Old seats need a seat wrench. About an hour, usually.

    Replace or Keep Repairing in Baltimore

    Repeated repairs add up. A valve on its third cartridge in five years is probably worn inside, so one replacement beats more repairs. Your service history helps us decide. Newer valves often come with lifetime cartridge warranties.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Baltimore

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. Supplies have to rise through the floor, from a slab cut or a crawlspace, with a bracket anchored below. Deck-mount fillers sit on the rim. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.

    Budget Tub Faucet Options in Baltimore, MD

    Tub Faucet Style Upgrades in Baltimore

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Baltimore, MD

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. They sense real water temperature, not just pressure, which helps kids and older adults avoid hot spikes. Many have separate heat and flow controls. They cost more than pressure-balance.
