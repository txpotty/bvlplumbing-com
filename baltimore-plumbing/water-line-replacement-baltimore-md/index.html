    Service Line Replacement Options in Baltimore, MD

    Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Baltimore

    Galvanized steel was common in homes built before the 1960s. Inside, the pipe rusts once the zinc is gone. After a few decades the opening can be half its original size, which is why pressure drops and the water runs orange after a shutoff, and no amount of cleaning brings it back. It has to come out.

    PEX Water Line Installation in Baltimore, MD

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house. Disconnecting garden hoses and shutting off outdoor spigots before the first hard freeze heads off many burst-pipe calls each winter. PEX can swell a little in a freeze and go back to shape, where copper splits. We still bury it below the frost depth the local code sets, and keep it covered from sunlight at the meter box, since UV light breaks it down. Short exposure only.

    Commercial Water Service Replacement in Baltimore

    Strip centers, churches and small apartment buildings each come with their own wrinkles. Some have a shared meter feeding several units. We map where each branch goes before cutting anything, bring the utility in for the meter side, and handle the permit, backflow test and inspection so the owner gets one point of contact. Paperwork included.

    Commercial Water Line Services in Baltimore, MD

    Commercial services run bigger, often 1-1/2 to 2 inches, and almost always have a backflow assembly that needs testing after any work. Shutdowns get scheduled for when you are closed. For a restaurant or an office building that might mean an early morning start, temporary water to the restrooms, and coordination with the water utility on the meter side. Tenants get notice.

    Water Line Materials We Install in Baltimore, MD

    Our trucks carry Type K copper, PEX-a and HDPE. Each has a place. Copper suits short runs and high pressure, PEX handles long runs with bends, and HDPE is the usual pick for pipe pulling and directional boring, where we need a tough, fused pipe that can be dragged a long way. All of it is listed for drinking water, carrying the NSF 61 listing.

    Traditional Water Line Excavation in Baltimore

    Sometimes the open trench wins. A collapsed line, a route with sharp bends, or solid rock can rule out trenchless. We cut the sod and set it aside, dig to depth with a mini excavator or by hand near other utilities, lay the new pipe on a sand bed, and backfill in layers with compaction so the trench does not sink after the first good rain. Sod goes back on top.

    Burst Main Line Response in Baltimore, MD

    A burst main is a fast-moving problem. We get the water off, dig to the break, and decide on site whether a clamp or a short section of new pipe will hold until a full replacement can be scheduled, or whether the line is too far gone to bother patching. You will not be without water overnight if we can help it.

    Copper Water Line Installation in Baltimore

    Type K copper is the thick-wall grade made for burial. Street pressure does not bother it. We bed it in sand, keep it away from sharp rock, and sleeve it where it passes through a foundation wall, because concrete and copper rubbing together for twenty years eventually wear a hole. It costs more than PEX.
