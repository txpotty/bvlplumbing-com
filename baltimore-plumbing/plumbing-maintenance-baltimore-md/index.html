    Slab Leak and Hidden Leak Checks in Baltimore

    Homes built after the war usually have copper supply lines, while newer construction typically uses PEX and plastic drain piping. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried lines until they leak out of sight for months. Warm floors are a clue. So is a meter that spins with everything off.     Sump Pump Testing in Baltimore, MD

    Basements flood when the pump fails during a storm. A working sump pump and a backwater valve are cheap insurance for any home with a finished basement. We pour water into the pit to make sure the float lifts and the pump kicks on, check the discharge line for clogs or freezing risk, and inspect the check valve so water does not drain back in. Battery backups get load tested.

    Repipe Assessments in Baltimore, MD

    Polybutylene and old galvanized pipe leak more. We identify the pipe type, look for signs of failure and tell you whether repiping is urgent or can wait. PEX and copper are the options. Work can be phased.

    Drain Cleaning as Maintenance in Baltimore

    Sewer and Drain Camera Inspection in Baltimore, MD
