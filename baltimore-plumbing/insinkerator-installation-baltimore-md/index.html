    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Baltimore

    A standard install replaces the unit under an existing sink. The old disposal comes down, the flange gets scraped clean and a new one goes in on fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. We hang the unit, turn the lower ring until it locks and connect the discharge tube to the trap with a new slip-joint washer so nothing weeps. We connect power through a cord kit or the old hardwire and confirm the ground first.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Baltimore

    An air switch puts a push button on the countertop or sink deck instead of a wall switch. The button pushes a puff of air down a thin tube. A control box under the sink reads that pulse and flips the power, keeping live wiring well away from the water. We drill the counter or use the spare faucet hole.

    Septic-Friendly Disposal Installs in Baltimore, MD

    Homes on septic can run a disposal, but food solids add load to the tank.
