    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Germantown

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. ADA units need a rim at or below 17 inches, a 13-1/2 inch projection and 30 by 48 inches of clear floor. The flush handle has to be easy to reach. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Commercial Urinal Installation in Germantown

    Offices and restaurants want urinals that survive heavy use. Each unit goes on a carrier with new gaskets and stainless bolts, paired with a matched Sloan or Zurn valve. We check partition spacing too. Most codes want 15 inches from centerline to the side.

    Home Gym and Garage Urinals in Germantown, MD

    A garage urinal saves tracking dirt into the house. The drain ties to the nearest stack, a vent or AAV goes in where allowed, and a compact bowl fits the space. Unheated garages need freeze planning. Ask us about the layout.

    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Germantown, MD

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. A flushometer from Sloan or Zurn delivers a measured volume from a 3/4 inch supply with no tank, and it recovers instantly for the next user, which matters in a stadium or school restroom. We set the volume to the bowl. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    Rough-In Plumbing for Urinals in Germantown, MD

    Rough-in decides whether the finish goes smoothly. The carrier, 2 inch waste, 3/4 inch supply and vent all go in to the spec sheet before drywall. Inspectors see it open. Then the walls close.

    Sensor Urinal Installation in Germantown, MD

    Sensor valves stop missed flushes and double flushes. Battery or hardwired valves get a tuned sensing range and an optional sanitary flush for idle periods. Expect years between battery changes. Hardwired units need an electrician first.

    Urinal Replacement and Upgrades in Germantown
