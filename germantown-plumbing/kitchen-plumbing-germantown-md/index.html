    Grease Trap Installation and Cleaning in Germantown

    Grease traps are sized by flow, and a three-compartment sink typically calls for a trap rated at 20 gallons per minute or more. We set the trap level, install the flow control fitting and vent it so it does not siphon. How often it gets cleaned depends on volume. Busy fryer kitchens may need it every few weeks.

    InSinkErator Installation in Germantown, MD

    Trading an older InSinkErator for an Evolution model is a common request because the newer units are quieter and grind finer. The mount often carries over.     Disposal Repair and Replacement in Germantown, MD

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is almost always jammed. We cut the power, work the flywheel loose with a 1/4 inch hex key from underneath, pull out the bottle cap or bone, and press the red reset button before testing it again. Leaks are a different story. Water from the bottom of the housing means the seals are gone and the unit needs replacing.

    Kitchen Sink Installation in Germantown

    Undermount sinks hang from clips and epoxy under the stone, so the first thing we check is how the bowl is held up. Supply lines are new every time. Both bowls get filled and drained while we watch every joint.

    Replacing an Old Kitchen Sink in Germantown

    Pulling an old cast iron sink takes two people and a putty knife, because they weigh a lot and are usually sealed hard to the counter. The counter cutout gets measured first. Your new sink must fit the existing opening or the countertop needs cutting, so we confirm the size before you order. The drain gets rebuilt to suit the new bowl depth.

    Disposal Replacement in Germantown, MD

    Disposals last about eight to twelve years. When the housing starts weeping from the bottom, it is done. We drop the old unit, check the mounting ring, install the new one and reconnect the dishwasher hose with a new clamp so it does not leak at the inlet. We haul the old one away.
