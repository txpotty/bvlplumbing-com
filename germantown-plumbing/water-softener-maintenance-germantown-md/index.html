    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repairs in Germantown, MD

    A bypass valve that does not seat fully lets raw water blend into the softened supply, and the hardness test is usually the first clue. We rebuild or replace the bypass and check the drain line for kinks. The drain line has to end above its receptor with an air gap.

    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in Germantown, MD

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. The beads turn rusty brown and stop grabbing hardness. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we set the valve to regenerate more often so iron cannot load the bed again.

    Control Valve and Timer Diagnostics in Germantown, MD

    The control valve runs every stage of regeneration, from backwash through brine draw to the final refill. We advance it manually through each cycle and watch for a motor that stalls, a drive gear that skips, a piston that drags or a drain that never shuts off. Worn pistons turn up a lot.

    Softener Brands We Work On in Germantown

    Most softeners sold in the last 20 years run on a handful of valve platforms, mainly Fleck, Clack, Autotrol and a few proprietary heads. The valve matters more than the brand. Kinetico and Culligan use their own designs, so we check part availability before quoting.

    Full-System Softener Inspections in Germantown

    An inspection covers more than the two tanks. We trace the inlet and outlet plumbing, the bypass, the drain line and the brine overflow. Green crust on copper fittings, a drip at the bypass or a drain line jammed down into a standpipe all go on the written report.

    Resin Rebedding in Germantown

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water slowly swell and soften resin beads, and iron or sediment can coat them until capacity drops off. We try a resin cleaner first. If the bed is spent, we empty out the old resin and refill the tank with new high-capacity media, which runs far less than a new softener.
