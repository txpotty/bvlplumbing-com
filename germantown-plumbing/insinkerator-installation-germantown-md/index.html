    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Germantown, MD

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. Expect the new unit to sit lower in the cabinet. That extra height can put the discharge outlet below the trap arm, so we measure the drop before install day and lower the trap or the arm if the numbers do not work. We also confirm the circuit can carry the larger motor.

    Badger Series Installation in Germantown, MD

    The Badger line runs from the 1/3 HP Badger 1 up to the Badger 5XP. All of them use the same quick-lock mount. Because of that, a Badger-for-Badger swap can take under an hour, and the flange often stays if it is still clean. Badgers ship without a cord, so we bring the cord kit.

    Commercial Disposer Installation in Germantown, MD

    Restaurant disposals are a different class of machine. A commercial disposer runs 1 to 5 HP on its own circuit, and many come with a wall control panel and a water solenoid. We set the unit, plumb the water feed and coordinate with the electrician on the circuit. Health inspectors look at these. Local code may also limit where a disposer can discharge relative to the grease interceptor, so we confirm that before the job starts.

    Evolution Series Installation in Germantown

    Evolution units grind in two or three stages and carry extra sound insulation around the motor housing, which is why they sit taller and weigh more than a Badger. Clearance under the bowl matters here. We measure from the sink bottom to the trap arm before the install so the unit does not crowd the trap. The Compact fits tight cabinets, and the Excel needs the most room.

    Badger Replacement for Germantown Homes

    Builder-grade kitchens almost always started with a Badger, and a worn one usually gets replaced with another. We pull the old unit first. If the upper mount is the same quick-lock design and is not pitted, we reuse it, which saves time and keeps the seal surfaces that already match your sink. Then the new Badger hangs, locks and gets tested.

    Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Germantown

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. It runs while the switch is on. A batch feed unit needs its stopper locked in to run, keeping hands out of the chamber, and it needs no wall switch. We wire each one to match.

    Evolution Upgrade Installs in Germantown, MD

    Quiet is the main thing people notice after an Evolution install. Insulation around the motor drops the noise a lot. What they notice later is fewer clogs downstream, because the multi-stage grind breaks fibrous scraps into much smaller pieces before they reach the trap and the branch line. With the motor running, we check the mount for vibration.

    Evolution Disposal Upgrades in Germantown, MD

    Stringy food is where these units earn their price. Corn husks and celery will stall a light disposal. A Compact or Excel grinds in stages with more torque, so fibrous scraps get cut down instead of binding the impellers and tripping the reset. We check cabinet clearance first.
