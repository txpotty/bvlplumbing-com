    Water Line Repair We Handle in Germantown, MD

    Commercial Water Line Repair in Germantown, MD

    Commercial service lines are often 1 1/2 to 4 inch pipe feeding a meter bank, a backflow assembly and sometimes a fire line. No water means no open sign. BVL Plumbing can schedule repairs early in the morning or after closing, with large-diameter couplings and fittings already loaded. We also arrange the backflow test and inspection that most water providers require after work on the commercial side of the meter.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Germantown

    A trench through a paver driveway or a mature garden can cost more to restore than the pipe itself. That is where trenchless pays off. We dig a pit at each end and pull HDPE or PEX through with a boring tool or pipe puller, either along the old route or through fresh soil next to it. Most replacements finish in a day. You also get the manufacturer warranty on the new pipe.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Germantown

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Some older neighborhoods still have lead service lines, so replacing one is worth considering when other plumbing work is already underway. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. Acoustic gear and a thermal camera tell us where it is. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or skip the old under-slab pipe and repipe overhead with PEX. You get a price on every option.

    Water Pressure Restoration in Germantown

    Low pressure at every fixture points to the service line or the regulator, not the faucets. We put a gauge on a hose bib first. Then we check the pressure reducing valve, the house shutoff and the meter-side valve for a partly closed gate, and look at the pipe itself. Old galvanized can be choked to half its size with rust. The fix might be a new valve or a new line.

    Main Water Line Repair in Germantown

    Your main line is the pipe from the meter to the house, usually 3/4 inch or 1 inch, buried anywhere from a foot to several feet down depending on frost depth. A leak here shows up as a soggy strip of lawn and a meter dial that never rests. We start by closing the house shutoff and watching the dial. If it still moves, the leak is buried. Then a ground microphone narrows the spot, and we dig one hole instead of a trench across your yard.

    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Germantown

    Underground leaks hide well. Many people find out from the bill before they see anything in the yard. Others notice a patch of grass that grows greener than the rest, or ground that stays soft long after the sprinklers shut off. We confirm the leak at the meter, trace the pipe route and listen along it. We dig where the noise is loudest.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Germantown

    Buried copper is tough, but it is not forever. Rocks pressing against the pipe wear pinholes over time, and acidic soil or high water velocity can thin the wall from the inside. A two-foot section is typical. The new piece goes in with soldered or pressed couplings, then we look over the pipe on either side for more pitting before we backfill.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Germantown

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. Straight runs are ideal. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, while new HDPE comes through right behind the head. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.
