    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Germantown, MD Home

    No Hot Water Calls in Germantown

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. Those get tested before anything else. An old tank with a bad valve usually gets replaced.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Service in Germantown, MD

    Rheem tanks and their Ruud twins share most parts, which helps when a gas valve fails a few years in. We install both. We set them up per the factory manual and test draft.

    A.O. Smith Gas Water Heater Service in Germantown

    A.O. Smith makes tanks under its own name and also builds the State line, so parts overlap a lot between the two brands. Sizing and gas hookup are part of the job. Exhaust venting gets checked against their specs.

    Commercial Gas Tank Replacement in Germantown

    Laundromats and multi-unit buildings work commercial tanks so hard that replacement comes on a predictable schedule rather than as a surprise. We plan the swap around your slow hours. Venting and gas work may be extra.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Upgrades in Germantown, MD

    Condensing gas tanks run around 90 percent efficient or better and vent through PVC, while a standard atmospheric tank sits closer to 60 percent. You will need a condensate drain and a nearby outlet. Big households see payback sooner.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Replacement in Germantown

    Atmospheric vent heaters last 10 to 12 years on average, and the end usually shows up as rusty hot water or a slow drip from the bottom. The new tank ties into your existing gas line and flue. Combustion air matters. We confirm a closet install has louvers or ducted air before we light the burner.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Germantown, MD

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. We never swap a natural gas tank onto propane without the right conversion. We confirm pressure with a manometer.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Service in Germantown, MD

    Higher BTU inputs on commercial units mean bigger gas lines and larger vent sizes, plus a permit and inspection in most jurisdictions. We handle all of it. Downtime stays short.
