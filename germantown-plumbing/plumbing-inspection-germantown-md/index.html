    Pre-Purchase Plumbing Inspections in Germantown

    A house can pass a general inspection and still have a sewer full of roots. Our report tells buyers what pipe the house has, what the sewer looks like and how old the heater is, while there is still time to act. That leaves room to negotiate. Galvanized supply lines, a cracked clay sewer or a water heater past fifteen years are all things a seller can credit for or fix before closing.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspections in Germantown, MD

    A sewer camera shows the inside of the main drain without digging. We push the camera in from a cleanout, watch for cracks, roots, offsets and low spots and record the whole run to the street or septic tank. We mark problem spots from above. Clay that swells when wet and shrinks when dry can stress sewer laterals and water service lines over time.

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Germantown, MD

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. Too much pressure wears parts. Pressure above 80 psi shortens the life of fixtures, supply hoses and water heaters, and we also identify the supply pipe material, from copper and CPVC to polybutylene, Kitec and galvanized.

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Germantown, MD

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. Our plumber identifies the supply pipe, whether copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, poly or Kitec, and explains how each usually fails. Certain types need a plan. Polybutylene and Kitec are known for sudden failures, and galvanized slowly closes up with rust, so those get flagged with replacement options.

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Germantown, MD

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. The visit covers flushing the heater, working the shutoffs, reading pressure and checking for slow drains and small leaks. Plan on an hour or so. Doing this every year or two adds years to a water heater and keeps the shutoffs working for the day you really need them.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Germantown

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. We test the T and P valve, confirm its discharge line runs to a safe place, look for an expansion tank where one is needed and check the drain pan. We check gas units for draft. Backdrafting heaters can push combustion gases indoors, which is why we test the vent and report problems before we leave.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Germantown, MD

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. Shutoffs get tested too. Stops and the main valve tend to seize with age, and gurgling drains can mean a vent problem, both of which are simple fixes once found.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Germantown

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. We confirm proper venting. We also look at the expansion tank, drain pan and softener, and a hardness test shows whether the softener is working or just eating salt.
