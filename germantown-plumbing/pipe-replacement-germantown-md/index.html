    What Goes Into a Repipe in Germantown, MD

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Germantown

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout. Pipes in exterior walls, unheated crawlspaces and garages are the usual trouble spots when a deep cold snap settles in. It often survives freezing where copper would crack. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings where possible, which give a full-bore connection and hold up well over time. Faster install.

    Emergency Pipe Replacement in Germantown, MD

    A burst pipe needs fast action. Shut off the main valve first. Then call, because water spreading through walls and floors does damage every minute, and our crews aim to stop it fast. Drying comes next.

    Commercial Pipe Replacement in Germantown

    Commercial buildings often have larger supply lines and more fixtures. Downtime costs money. We plan pipe replacement in phases whenever the layout allows it, keeping restrooms and kitchens running while we work on other sections. Night work available.

    Whole-Home Re-Piping in Germantown

    A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture, including the water heater connections. Most single-story homes take two to three days. We run new trunk lines through the attic or crawlspace, branch down to fixtures through small wall openings, with service restored each evening when possible. Then patching.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Germantown

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. Scale and rust do not affect it. Homes on private wells, especially in the low coastal counties, often deal with rusty mineral staining, odors or acidity that call for filtration or treatment. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, and we run it in home runs from a manifold or in trunk-and-branch layouts. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Germantown, MD

    Drain lines fail too. Cast iron cracks and scales. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, and making sure vents and fall are correct. Camera first.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Germantown, MD

    Pinhole leaks start small. A drip, then a stain. They usually mean the copper wall has thinned from corrosion, and when one shows up, more are often on the way along the same lines. Repipe or patch, we explain.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Germantown, MD

    Copper still has fans. It is durable and heat tolerant. We install Type L copper for most interior supply lines, sweat or press the joints, and support every run with hangers at the spacing code requires, so the new pipe does not rattle or sag. Costs more than PEX.
