    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Germantown, MD

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. After five to ten years the core can split without warning, and old multi-turn stops often seize open. We swap both in one visit. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in Germantown

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. That points to worn O-rings on the spout or a bad base gasket, and sometimes to a cracked faucet body that cannot be rebuilt. Each faucet style rebuilds its own way. We identify yours before ordering parts.

    Burst Supply Line Repair in Germantown

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute. Close the angle stop, or the main if the stop fails, because every minute adds water to the subfloor. Then lay towels down. We replace the line and the stop and moisture-check the cabinet.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Germantown, MD

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. Kitchen clogs are grease and food that cooled and stuck, and bathroom clogs are hair wound around the stopper with soap scum holding it together. A hand auger does most of the work. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.

    Commercial Sink Repair in Germantown

    Restaurants and salons cannot wait a week for a sink. A down hand sink can cost you an inspection, so we work around your hours and carry commercial faucets and basket strainers. Most commercial clogs are grease. We also check the grease interceptor connection when the drain backs up.

    Sink Parts Replacement in Germantown, MD

    Most sinks do not need replacing. A new tailpiece or pop-up assembly costs a fraction of a new sink and installs in an hour, and the bowl and faucet you already like stay right where they are. We match finish where we can. Discontinued faucets sometimes force a full faucet swap.

    Loose or Wobbly Sink Repair in Germantown

    A sink that moves when you lean on it is pulling on the drain. Undermount sinks hang on clips glued under the counter, and pedestal sinks hang on a bracket that can pull loose from drywall. The clips get reset and the rim resealed. Pedestals get blocking behind the wall when needed.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sink Repair in Germantown

    Most sink repairs in Germantown land between $150 and $400. A cleared clog or a new washer sits near the bottom of that range, while a disposal swap or a corroded drain body that has to be cut out sits near the top. You get a firm price first.

    Most take one to two hours. A trap or supply line swap can be done in under an hour, while a disposal replacement or a full drain rebuild with a stuck flange can stretch past two. We tell you the time up front.

    Pretty much all of them. We fix kitchen and bath sinks of every material and mount, plus laundry and utility tubs. We do commercial sinks as well.

    Most carry the manufacturer's warranty. Faucet cartridges from the major brands often have a lifetime warranty for the original owner, while traps and supply lines carry shorter terms printed on the package. The invoice lists each part's coverage.

    Swapping an aerator or tightening a slip nut is fair game for a handy homeowner. Seized angle stops and undermount bowls that have dropped are different, because one wrong turn can snap old pipe inside the wall. Those are plumber jobs.

    All the common ones. Kohler, Moen, Delta, American Standard and Pfister make up most of what we see, along with InSinkErator and Waste King disposals under kitchen sinks. Designer brands may need parts ordered.

    Most municipal water across Maryland runs moderately hard, so scale slowly builds inside water heaters, faucet aerators and showerheads over the years. Calcium and magnesium settle in aerators and valve seats, choking flow and wearing seals. We clean or replace the scaled parts, and if the buildup comes back every year we can talk through a softener or a sediment filter for the house. Soaking the aerator in vinegar helps.
