    Same-Day Jetting for Backups in Waldorf

    Sewage in a tub or floor drain is a main line blockage. Hold off on running water. Our technician opens the line, then jets it to remove the cause, and a camera pass after jetting shows whether the backup came from buildup alone or from a broken or root-filled section that needs repair.

    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Waldorf, MD

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. A jet scours the entire pipe wall. For a single clog in an otherwise clean line, snaking is cheaper and works fine, but for a line that clogs every few months, jetting removes the buildup that keeps catching debris.

    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Waldorf

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. Jetting the main clears roots, grease and sludge all the way to the city connection. Four-inch mains get a heavy nozzle and high flow to push debris downstream.

    Emergency Hydrojet Services in Waldorf, MD

    A full sewer backup cannot wait for a weekday slot. We clear it first. A cable opens it, then the jetter removes the grease, roots or wipes that caused it.

    Sewer Lateral Jetting in Waldorf, MD

    The lateral from your house to the street handles every drain in the house, so buildup there causes backups everywhere at once. We jet it from the cleanout to the tap. Low spots where the pipe has settled hold water and collect sludge, so we spend extra passes there and mark the location on the camera footage in case a repair makes sense later.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Waldorf, MD

    Restaurant kitchen lines send warm grease downstream where it cools and sticks. By the time it reaches the interceptor, a lot of it has already coated the pipe. We jet the line from the kitchen to the interceptor in short passes, which keeps loosened grease from plugging the pipe farther down, and we coordinate with your pump-out company. Quarterly works for most.
