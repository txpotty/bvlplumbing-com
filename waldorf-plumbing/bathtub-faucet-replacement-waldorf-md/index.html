    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Waldorf

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. A single-handle valve often takes new trim and a cartridge from the front, while a three-handle valve usually has to be cut out of the wall and replaced with a modern pressure-balance body. We check before quoting. Two-handle models fall in between.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Waldorf

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. The supply lines come up through the floor under the filler, which means cutting the slab or working from a crawlspace or basement, and the filler needs a mounting bracket anchored to the subfloor. Deck fillers mount on the tub deck. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.

    Brand Tub Faucet Replacement in Waldorf

    Brand matters more on tub valves than anywhere else. Each maker uses its own cartridges and trim threads, so a same-brand trim kit usually bolts on without plumbing changes. Mixing brands rarely works. We find the logo or the casting mark first.

    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Waldorf, MD

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. Replacement inside the wall makes sense when parts are discontinued, the valve body is cracked or pitted, or you want anti-scald protection on an old three-handle faucet that has none. We quote both options. Most people choose repair first.

    Same-Day Tub Faucet Replacement in Waldorf, MD

    Some faucet failures cannot wait. A handle that snapped off in the open position or a spout that broke loose from its pipe can leave water running or the tub unusable, so we treat those as urgent and send a plumber the same day when we can. Shut off the water first. Leave the tub drain open.

    Tub Faucet Style Upgrades in Waldorf

    Wall-mount tub fillers are a popular upgrade. A long-reach filler or a slide-bar handheld changes the tub, and both usually need a new valve or diverter. Rough-in gets planned before trim. The trim goes on last.

    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Waldorf, MD

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We replace Moen and Delta cartridges or old compression stems and seats, and we grease the O-rings with silicone plumber's grease so the handle turns smoothly for years. Old seats need a seat wrench. About an hour, usually.

    Replace or Keep Repairing in Waldorf

    Repeated repairs add up. If a faucet has had three cartridges in five years, the valve body is likely worn where the cartridge seals, and replacing the whole valve once costs less than another round of service calls. Your service history helps us decide. Newer valves often come with lifetime cartridge warranties.

    Tile matters to us as much as to you. We reach valves from behind when possible, and any tile cut stays small enough to cover. Most jobs never need new tile.
