    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Waldorf

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water leaking behind the trim slips into the wall cavity at the valve opening and soaks the drywall while the tile face stays dry. The trim comes off first. Worn O-rings or a loose bonnet nut are the usual cause.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Waldorf

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. Water that seeps from the arm connection runs back along the pipe into the wall, and you only find out when the paint on the other side starts to bubble. We reseal the threads. We can add a handheld or slide bar while we are there.

    Mixing Valve Repair in Waldorf

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. A sticking cartridge or spool shows up as temperature that drifts or a handle that has to go much farther than it used to. We replace the cartridge. Valves with no parts left get a replacement plan and an honest look at the tile.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Waldorf, MD

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. A soak can bring it back. If the cartridge is clogged too, we replace it and test the flow again.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Waldorf, MD

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. We pull the strainer and run a hand auger through the trap, and if the clog sits past the trap in the branch line, we switch to a drum machine with a smaller cable. Drain cleaner usually misses the clog. A hair catcher over the strainer keeps the next one from forming.

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Waldorf

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. Single-handle showers drip from a worn cartridge or O-rings, while old two-handle valves drip from a washer and a pitted seat. The parts are on the truck. Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister cartridges cover most of the valves we open, so the repair is usually done that same visit.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Waldorf, MD

    Stains on the ceiling below are often the first clue. We use a moisture meter on the walls and a flood test on the pan to tell a leaking valve from a cracked pan, a failed grout line or a loose drain body. The source gets repaired. With slab floors, the water can travel under the flooring of nearby rooms.
