    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Waldorf, MD

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Paper and solids settle there. Soils in Maryland range from heavy clay in the central counties to sandy, low-lying ground near the shore, and each affects buried lines differently. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line. A shallow belly sometimes just needs regular cleaning.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Waldorf

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and we always jet a line before lining it. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We camera first to make sure the pipe can take the pressure.
