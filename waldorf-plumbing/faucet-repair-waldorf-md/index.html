    Low Water Pressure Faucet Fix in Waldorf, MD

    Weak flow at one faucet usually points to a clogged aerator or a cartridge packed with scale, not a problem with the house pressure.     Compression Faucet Repair in Waldorf

    Two-handle faucets with separate hot and cold handles are often compression faucets, which seal by pressing a rubber washer down onto a brass seat inside the valve.     Leaky Faucet Repair in Waldorf

    Most drips come from the part that seals the water inside the faucet, which is a rubber washer on older two-handle faucets and a cartridge or ceramic disc on newer ones. We figure out which type you have first. It matters. With the right part on the truck, most drips are fixed in one visit.
