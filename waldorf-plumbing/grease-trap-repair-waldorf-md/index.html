    Grease Line Clog Repair in Waldorf, MD

    Lines downstream of the trap should stay clean if the trap is working. When we find grease past the outlet, the trap is not doing its job.     Flow Control Device Repair in Waldorf

    Hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, like 20 or 50 gallons per minute, and the flow control fitting is what holds the water to that rate. Take it out and a three-compartment sink dump blows grease right past the baffles. Inspectors look for it. We also clear the air intake, since a plugged intake on the flow control fitting lets the trap siphon.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Waldorf

    Inside a concrete gravity interceptor the baffle is usually a poured or precast wall with a crossover opening. When that wall spalls, grease slips through to the second compartment. We can rebuild the wall with form work and a sulfate-resistant mix or set a polyethylene baffle panel, depending on the tank. Both require a full pump-out and a dry tank.

    Grease Trap Leak Repair in Waldorf

    Plastic and fiberglass trap bodies do not rust, but they can crack from a dropped lid or a dishwasher dumping water hotter than the trap is rated for. A hairline crack can sometimes be welded. Anything through the floor of the tank or along a molded seam we treat as a replacement, because patches there fail under the weight of a full trap.     Preventative Grease Trap Maintenance & Repair in Waldorf, MD

    Most trap repairs we do trace back to pumping intervals that stretched too long. Pretreatment programs commonly set the limit at 25 percent of the liquid depth for grease and solids combined. After a repair, we measure the grease cap and solids with a sludge judge and suggest a pumping schedule based on how fast your trap actually fills. Busy fryers fill a trap fast.

    Restaurants, school cafeterias, hospital kitchens and bakeries all push more grease than a small trap can hold. Undersized traps fail in predictable spots.     Grease Interceptor Clog Removal in Waldorf

    Grease does not stay liquid for long once it cools in a pipe. Within a few feet of the sink it hardens like candle wax along the top of the pipe.
