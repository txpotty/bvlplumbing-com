    Why Choose BVL Plumbing for Sewer Replacement in Aspen Hill, MD?

    A broken sewer line does more than clog. Waste seeps into the dirt. That wet soil can wash out the bedding, settle a walkway or attract roots from across the yard, and in bad cases it leaks toward the foundation. Replacing it stops all of that.

    • Permits Pulled, We file the permit and schedule the inspection before the trench is closed.
    • Camera Verified, A final camera pass confirms the new line so you know it is clean and properly graded.

    Citywide Coverage

    Septic or city sewer, we replace the line either way. On septic properties the run ends at the tank inlet, and we make sure the new line enters it at the right height and with a proper sanitary tee or baffle. Rural runs are often long.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Aspen Hill

    Bursting works best on clay, cast iron and Orangeburg. A hydraulic rig at the receiving pit pulls the bursting head through the old line, splitting it into the soil, while a continuous length of fused HDPE follows behind so the new pipe has no joints at all between the pits. Roots cannot get into a joint that does not exist.

    Advanced Diagnostics for Sewer Issues in Aspen Hill, MD

    Diagnosis comes before price. Video tells us the problem and the sonde tells us the spot. We sometimes add a hydro-jet cleaning first, because a line full of grease can hide a crack, and a clean pipe gives a much more honest picture of its condition. Guesswork costs money.

    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Aspen Hill, MD

    Roots find sewer lines by following moisture. A hairline crack is enough. Once inside, they grow into a mat that catches paper and grease, and cutting them out with a machine only buys a few months, because the opening is still there. New PVC with glued joints gives them no way in.

    Code-Compliant Sewer Replacement in Aspen Hill

    Code sets the minimum slope, the cleanout spacing and the pipe type. Inspectors check all three in an open trench. We set fall at a quarter inch per foot unless the run requires otherwise, place cleanouts at the house and at major direction changes, and only bury pipe rated for it. Paperwork stays on file.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Aspen Hill

    Digging is still the right method for a lot of lines. If the old pipe has caved in, trenchless has nothing to follow. Sharp bends, a belly that needs regrading, or a route that should change all point toward an open trench, and we restore the sod or gravel afterward. Grass grows back.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Aspen Hill, MD

    A lot of older houses have no outside cleanout. Without one, the plumber has to pull a toilet or go through the roof vent. When we replace a sewer line we add a two-way cleanout near the foundation, bringing a capped riser up to grade where you can reach it, so future cleaning or camera work is quick and cheap.

    Emergency Sewer Line Replacement in Aspen Hill, MD

    Sewage coming up a floor drain or tub is an emergency. Stop running water in the house right away. We open the cleanout to relieve the backup, run a camera to find the break, and if the line has collapsed we dig to it the same day, putting in a section of new pipe to get you flowing before planning the full replacement.

    Under-Slab Sewer Replacement in Aspen Hill

    Sewer pipe under a slab is the hardest kind to reach. We have two ways in. We can cut and remove a strip of floor inside the house and patch it after, or tunnel under the slab from outside so the floors stay intact, and the right choice depends on the length of the run, the flooring, and what sits above the pipe.
