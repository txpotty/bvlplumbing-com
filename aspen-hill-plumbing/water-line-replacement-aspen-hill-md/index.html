    Copper Water Line Installation in Aspen Hill

    Type K copper is the thick-wall grade made for burial. It handles high street pressure without complaint. We bed it in sand, keep it away from sharp rock, and sleeve it where it passes through a foundation wall, because concrete and copper rubbing together for twenty years will wear through the pipe.     Commercial Water Line Replacement in Aspen Hill

    Larger buildings bring larger pipe, deeper trenches and more inspection steps. Backflow testing and the report to the water provider are part of the job.     Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Aspen Hill, MD

    Polybutylene is the gray or blue plastic pipe installed from roughly the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water. The failures tend to be sudden splits rather than slow drips, and some home insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Worth replacing.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Aspen Hill, MD

    Bursting fits old cast iron or galvanized lines that will shatter under the head. Pulling suits softer pipe like poly or copper. Either way the new pipe follows the old path, which matters when the route runs under a patio or a neighbor's fence, and the yard work shrinks to two pits. Rock can stop it.

    Damage Assessment Before Replacement in Aspen Hill

    Copper Service Line Installation in Aspen Hill, MD

    Copper is still the choice for some homeowners. Plenty of copper mains have lasted 60 years. We flare or use brass compression fittings at the ends, keep the run in one piece between them, and pack sand around it so rocks do not touch the pipe.     Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Aspen Hill

    One leak on a twenty-year-old copper line? We will usually just fix that spot. Three leaks on a galvanized line from the 1950s is different, because the pipe is thin everywhere and the next leak is already forming, so replacing the whole run is cheaper over five years. We tell you which camp you are in and show you the pipe we cut out.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Aspen Hill

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. Scale and corrosion are not a problem with it. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, use only fittings listed for burial, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.
