    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Aspen Hill

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Clay that swells when wet and shrinks when dry can stress sewer laterals and water service lines over time. We replace corroded sections with polyethylene pipe laid with tracer wire, using an approved transition riser where the line comes up out of the ground at the house. Exposed sections get coated steel. Everything is tested before the trench is backfilled, so a bad joint gets caught while it is still easy to reach.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Aspen Hill

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. We replace them with new coated stainless connectors sized for the appliance and add an accessible shutoff valve if the old one is missing or seized. Usually under an hour per appliance. Furnaces, water heaters, ranges and dryers each need a sound connection, and most need a drip leg before the appliance valve.

    Gas Appliance Connector Repair in Aspen Hill, MD

    Weak flames or a smell near one appliance often point to the connector or shutoff behind it. Uncoated brass connectors from decades ago crack at the flare fittings and should be replaced on sight. We replace them with coated stainless connectors. We also add a reachable shutoff and a drip leg where needed, and confirm the new connector is rated for that appliance's BTU input.

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Aspen Hill, MD

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. A whole-house tankless heater can draw 150,000 BTU or more, and that often means upsizing the gas line all the way back toward the meter. We calculate the full load first. The new pipe gets sized so every burner in the house still has full pressure at once.

    CSST Repair in Aspen Hill

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing is a flexible gas pipe with a plastic jacket, often yellow, that runs through attics and walls in newer homes. It can be damaged. Common damage comes from nails, rodents or lightning, and repairs require cutting out the bad section and using fittings from the same manufacturer. Bonding gets checked on every system.
