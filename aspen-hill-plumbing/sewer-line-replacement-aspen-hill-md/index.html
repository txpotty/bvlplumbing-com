    Upgrading Old Sewer Pipe Materials in Aspen Hill

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all fail in their own ways. Clay joints let roots in every few feet, cast iron scales and rusts through along the bottom, and Orangeburg tar paper pipe slowly flattens into an oval. Modern plastic pipe avoids all three problems. Fused HDPE has no joints at all, so roots have nowhere to start.

    How Pipe Bursting Works in Aspen Hill

    A winch pulls the bursting head. The head is slightly bigger than the old pipe, so it splits clay or cast iron outward into the soil while fused HDPE trails behind it in one continuous piece. You get a pit at each end. The fused pipe has no joints, so roots cannot get in again.
