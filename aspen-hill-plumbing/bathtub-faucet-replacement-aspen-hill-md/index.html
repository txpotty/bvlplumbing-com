    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently.     Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in Aspen Hill, MD

    Water running from the spout during a shower means a failing diverter. It is usually the gate in the spout, or the middle stem on three-handle faucets, and both wear out over time. A new diverter spout takes minutes. Middle stems need a seat too.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Aspen Hill

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. They adjust flow when a toilet flushes so the mix holds, and thermostatic valves hold an exact temperature. Both meet anti-scald code. Thermostatic costs more.
