    Why Choose BVL Plumbing for Grease Trap Cleaning in Severn, MD?

    A grease trap works by slowing the water down long enough for fat to float and food to sink. Past a quarter full, that stops. Grease rides straight through the outlet and coats the sewer lateral, where it cools into a hard white crust that a snake just punches a hole through.

    • Prevent Backups, Pumping on schedule keeps grease from leaving the trap and hardening in the lateral.

    Outdoor Grease Interceptor Pumping in Severn, MD

    Grease Trap Hydro-Jetting in Severn, MD

    Some traps have years of grease baked onto the walls and inlet piping. Pumping alone leaves that crust in place. Jetting the empty tank and its inlet line strips it back to bare walls and gets back the capacity that crust was taking. It adds about an hour.

    Grease Trap Pumping and Hauling in Severn

    Trap waste is regulated as a liquid waste, so in most places it has to be hauled by a licensed hauler to an approved receiving facility, not dumped down a manhole or into a dumpster.
