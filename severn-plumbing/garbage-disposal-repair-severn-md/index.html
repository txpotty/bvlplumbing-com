    Disposal Repair in Severn, MD: The Details

    Disposal Odor and Buildup Cleaning in Severn, MD

    Food buildup and grease on the chamber walls and under the splash guard are behind most disposal odors. Most municipal water across Maryland runs moderately hard, so scale slowly builds inside water heaters, faucet aerators and shower valves over the years. Scale gives that food something to stick to. We clean the splash guard and chamber, check the drain for buildup, and replace a worn guard, which often clears up a smell that months of lemon peels never touched.

    Emergency Disposal and Sink Backup Repair in Severn

    When a disposal fails, water can back into the sink and the dishwasher. With water rising or on the floor, turn off the disposal and dishwasher and call, because running them only adds water to the cabinet. We move quickly on these.

    Commercial Disposal Repair in Severn

    Restaurant disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and often have a separate control panel and solenoid water valve. When one stops, the dish line backs up fast. We check the controls first. A failed solenoid or a tripped overload in the control panel stops the unit just as completely as a jam, and it is a much faster fix.

    Electrical Problems and Resetting in Severn

    No hum at all usually means no power reaching the motor. The red reset button on the bottom is the first stop, then check the breaker, the wall switch and the outlet or junction box under the sink with a meter. It is wet under a sink. Corroded wire nuts and loose cord connections are common in that damp space, and we replace them rather than just tightening them back down.

    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Severn, MD

    Fine grinding with slow draining usually means the discharge tube or the trap arm is packed. Coffee grounds and rice cause most of it. We pull the trap, clear the tube and the arm with the right size cable, and check that the arm has at least a quarter inch of fall per foot toward the wall.

    Leak Detection and Repair in Severn, MD

    There are four usual leak points on a disposal: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Most leak points can be repaired. When the housing leaks, the unit has to go.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Severn, MD

    Leaks show up at the flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube or body. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed, and in that case replacement is the better option. That seal cannot be replaced.

    Grinding Noise Repair in Severn

    Loud metal-on-metal sounds may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove any debris, then run the unit empty to hear whether the motor itself is making the noise. Failing bearings are not worth fixing.
