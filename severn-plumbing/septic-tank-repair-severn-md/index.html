    Septic Alarm Troubleshooting in Severn

    A septic alarm sounding is the system's way of saying the water is too high in the pump chamber or the aerobic unit. Press the silence button and cut back on water. We check whether the pump runs and whether a float is hung up on its cord. A tripped breaker is the cheapest answer.

    Effluent Pump Replacement in Severn, MD

    Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. A pump that hums but moves no water has a stuck impeller or a bad capacitor. Our tech pulls the old unit and checks the discharge before the new one goes in. Floats get replaced at the same time.
