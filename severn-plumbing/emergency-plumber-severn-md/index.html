    Sump Pump Failure Repair in Severn, MD

    Nor'easters, tropical storm remnants and summer downpours can overwhelm storm drains across Maryland and push water toward basements. A pump that hums but does not move water usually has a jammed impeller. One that never kicks on points to the float. Our plumbers in Severn pull the pump, clear or replace it with a Zoeller or Wayne unit sized for your pit, and add a battery backup if power outages are what keep flooding you. Before leaving, we pour water in the pit to prove it cycles.

    Emergency Sump Pump Service in Severn

    A basement pump that quits halfway through a heavy rain can let several inches of water in within an hour. Common causes are a stuck float switch, a burned-out motor or a check valve that let go. We check the float, motor and check valve and swaps the pump if needed. Then we trace the discharge line outside. If the discharge line is crushed or frozen, water just circles back to the pit.
