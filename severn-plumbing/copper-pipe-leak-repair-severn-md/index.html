    Hot Water Copper Line Repair in Severn, MD

    Hot water lines corrode faster than cold lines. Heat speeds it up. Recirculation pumps that run around the clock push water through the copper at high velocity and wear it from the inside, which is why we often add a timer to the pump after fixing the leak. Less run time, less wear.

    Commercial Copper Pipe Repair in Severn

    Many commercial buildings use larger diameter copper for domestic water supply. Leaks affect more than one unit. Leaks in these systems require careful shutdown planning, and press fittings shorten the shutdown and skip the torch. We can add shutoffs for next time.

    Thin-Wall Copper Pinhole Repair in Severn

    Older copper, especially Type M, pinholes more than heavier pipe. There is less metal to lose. We locate the leak precisely and either solder in a new section or use a press fitting near framing, replacing the damaged length with Type L copper so the repair has a thicker wall than the pipe it replaced. We check the rest of the run.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Severn

    Pinholes start inside the pipe where the copper wall pits and thins. You see a drip or a stain. They are small and easy to miss behind drywall, which is why we use thermal imaging to find them before we open anything. We replace the pitted length with Type L. We look at the copper around it.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Severn

    Pinholes form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. Hot lines get it worst. They often appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher, which we fix by replacing that length with heavier pipe. One usually means more are coming.

    Underground Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Severn

    Buried copper service lines can leak for months before anyone notices. The meter gives it away. We locate the leak with acoustic gear and tracer gas, then repair it through a small pit or replace the line by pulling new PEX or Type K copper through the old path. We test before backfill.

    Solder Joint Leak Repair in Severn, MD

    Older solder joints can fail from poor original work, thermal expansion or vibration. Crusty fittings are a clue. A leaking joint usually weeps for a while before it drips, so we either resolder it or replace the fitting and a short length of pipe. Then we pressure test it.

    Preventive Copper Pipe Inspection in Severn, MD

    An inspection catches thin spots before they leak. Plan on an hour. We look at exposed copper for green crust and pitting, test static pressure, check the bond wire at the water heater and main, then scan suspect walls with a thermal camera on homes with a leak history. You get a written summary.
