    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Severn, MD

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. When someone flushes a toilet, the valve cuts hot and cold flow in proportion so the mix stays steady, and a thermostatic valve goes further by holding a set temperature even as the water heater cools. Either one satisfies scald rules. Thermostatic costs more.

    Tub Faucet Style Upgrades in Severn

    Wall-mount tub fillers are a popular upgrade. Swapping a standard spout for a long-reach filler or a handheld shower on a slide bar changes how the tub works, and both usually need a new valve or a diverter added inside the wall. We plan the rough-in first. The trim goes on last.

    Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in Severn, MD

    Water running from the spout during a shower means a failing diverter. It is usually the gate in the spout, or the middle stem on three-handle faucets, and both wear out over time. A new diverter spout takes minutes. Middle stems need a seat too.

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Severn

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. The old valve comes out, a pressure-balance valve goes in, and a remodel plate covers the extra holes. We usually work from behind the wall. Tile around the plate stays intact.

    Replace or Keep Repairing in Severn
