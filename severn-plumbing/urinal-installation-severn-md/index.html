    Frequently Asked Questions About Urinal Installation in Severn

    Most urinal installs run $400 to $1,500. A waterless or flush valve unit set on an existing carrier with good lines sits near the low end, while a commercial job that needs new drain, vent or supply work lands closer to the top. The price is set before work starts.

    A replacement on an existing carrier takes two to four hours. New installs need rough-in and an inspection before finish, so they usually take two visits. We schedule both up front.

    Two to four hours, usually. Removing the old fixture, cleaning the outlet flange, setting the new urinal with a fresh gasket and connecting the flush valve all fit in one visit when the carrier and lines are in good shape. Seized hardware can stretch it.

    Clean it daily with a neutral cleaner. Avoid acid bowl cleaners on waterless units because they damage the cartridge seal, pour water down floor drains nearby so their traps stay full, and have the flush volume checked if stains build up quickly. Smells often trace to a dry floor drain.

    Start with water use and parts support. Low-flow fixtures from brands with local parts support cost less to run and to fix. Tamper-proof parts help in public restrooms.

    Water, and upkeep. A flush urinal rinses the bowl from a flush valve on a 3/4 inch supply, while a waterless urinal uses a cartridge or sealing liquid that blocks sewer gas and has to be replaced on a schedule. Waterless saves the most water.

    Flushing action. Washdown units rinse from the rim and drain by gravity, and siphon jet units pull the trap clear with more water. Washdown dominates low-flow models.

    American Standard, Kohler, Zurn and Sloan cover most jobs. We also set Toto, Gerber, Crane and Bradley fixtures, and for waterless units we install Falcon and Waterless Co models along with Kohler and Zurn versions. We favor brands with easy parts.
