    Sewer Lateral Lining in Bethesda

    Laterals are the most common candidates for trenchless repair. Old materials crack and sag. Over time, clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe crack, sag or fill with roots, so we jet, scope and then pick lining or bursting. The new line restores full flow and resists roots.

    Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Bethesda

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Both matter. Before lining, Picote heads mill out roots and scale, and after curing they reopen the lateral connections so each drain flows. A clean pipe holds a liner best.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Repair Cost in Bethesda, MD

    Per foot of pipe, trenchless can cost more than open trenching. Look at the full cost. Once you add restoring driveways, patios, landscaping and irrigation, trenchless is often the better value, and it is faster and far less disruptive for the household. We compare both with real numbers for your home.

    Pipe Bursting in Bethesda, MD

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. It needs two pits. A bursting head is pulled through, fracturing the old pipe outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind, and that HDPE is fused into one continuous length with no joints at all.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Bethesda, MD

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. The old pipe gets split. A winch pulls a bursting head through the line, cracking the old pipe outward into the soil while new HDPE follows right behind it, so the replacement pipe ends up in the same path. HDPE does not rust or leak at joints in Bethesda, MD soil.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Systems in Bethesda

    Perma-Liner is one of the CIPP systems our crews install. It's rated for sewer lines. Its liners cure into a structural pipe that holds even if the old host pipe keeps falling apart around it. They handle harsh soil and flow.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Costs in Bethesda, MD

    On paper, the two methods can price out close. Then add restoration. Open trenching under a drive or garden adds concrete, plant and sprinkler replacement to the bill, which trenchless mostly avoids. We price both so you can compare.

    Lateral and Spot Lining in Bethesda

    Not every pipe needs lining end to end. A few bad joints need a short fix. When damage is limited to a few joints or a short section, a sectional liner costs less than a full-length one and goes in quickly, but we confirm the rest of the line is sound by camera before recommending it.
