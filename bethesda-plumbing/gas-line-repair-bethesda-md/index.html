    Buried Yard Line Repair and Replacement in Bethesda, MD

    Many older homes have steel gas lines running from the meter to the house or out to a pool heater. The wrap fails eventually.     Gas Appliance Connector Repair in Bethesda, MD

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Bethesda

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Soils in Maryland range from heavy clay in the central counties to sandy, low-lying ground near the shore, and each affects buried lines differently. Rusted buried steel gets replaced with polyethylene and tracer wire, and we use a listed transition riser where it rises to the house. Above-ground runs stay in steel. Everything is tested before the trench is backfilled, so a bad joint gets caught while it is still easy to reach.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Bethesda, MD

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. Our work covers propane yard lines, regulator replacement and fitting repairs, plus a check that every appliance has its propane orifices installed. A natural gas burner running on propane will burn rich and soot up. We test the system the same way we test natural gas piping.
