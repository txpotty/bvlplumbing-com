    Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Bethesda, MD

    Polybutylene is the gray or blue plastic pipe installed from roughly the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. Chlorine in the water breaks it down from the inside. The failures tend to be sudden splits rather than slow drips, and some home insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Worth replacing.

    Main Water Line Replacement in Bethesda

    Your service line runs from the meter or curb stop to the main shutoff inside the house, usually as 3/4-inch or 1-inch pipe. Once it has leaked in two different places, patching stops making sense. We dig at both ends, pull or cut out the old pipe, and run new line at the depth local code calls for, with fresh connections at the meter and at the shutoff. Most homes are done in a day.

    Copper Service Line Installation in Bethesda, MD

    Copper is still the choice for some homeowners. Plenty of copper mains have lasted 60 years. We flare or use brass compression fittings at the ends, keep the run in one piece between them, and pad it with sand to keep stones off. Where it goes through a concrete wall, it gets a plastic sleeve and a seal to keep water and bugs out.

    Commercial Water Line Replacement in Bethesda

    Larger buildings bring larger pipe, deeper trenches and more inspection steps. We handle the backflow test and certification paperwork. Trench shoring comes into play below five feet, and we plan the work so the outage lasts hours instead of days, often running a temporary supply while the new line is tied in. Night work is possible.

    Copper Water Line Installation in Bethesda

    Type K copper is the thick-wall grade made for burial. It handles high street pressure without complaint. We bed it in sand, keep it away from sharp rock, and sleeve it where it passes through a foundation wall, because concrete and copper rubbing together for twenty years will wear through the pipe. It costs more than PEX.

    PEX Service Lines for Long Runs in Bethesda

    We buy PEX in long coils. That makes it the easy pick when the meter sits out at the road and the house is set far back, because one coil covers the whole run without a joint. Fewer joints underground means fewer places a leak can start, and it is the joints that usually fail first.

    Commercial Water Line Services in Bethesda, MD

    Commercial services run bigger, often 1-1/2 to 2 inches, and almost always have a backflow assembly that needs testing after any work. We schedule the shutdown around your hours. For a restaurant or an office building that might mean an early morning start, temporary water to the restrooms, and coordination with the water utility on the meter side. Tenants get notice.

    Burst Main Line Response in Bethesda, MD

    Burst lines do damage fast. We get the water off, dig to the break, and decide on site whether a clamp or a short section of new pipe will hold until a full replacement can be scheduled, or whether the pipe crumbles when we touch it. You will not be without water overnight if we can help it.
