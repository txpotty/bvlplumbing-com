    Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in Bethesda, MD

    Water running from the spout during a shower means a failing diverter. It is usually the gate in the spout, or the middle stem on three-handle faucets, and both wear out over time. A new diverter spout takes minutes. Middle stems need a seat too.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Bethesda, MD

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. They sense real water temperature, not just pressure, which helps kids and older adults avoid hot spikes. Volume and temperature often get separate handles. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Bethesda, MD

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. When someone flushes a toilet, the valve cuts hot and cold flow in proportion so the mix stays steady, and a thermostatic valve goes further by holding a set temperature even as the water heater cools. Both meet anti-scald code. Thermostatic costs more.
