    Rheem and Bradford White Tank Service in Bethesda

    Rheem, Bradford White, AO Smith and State make most of the tanks in local garages, and each has quirks a tech should know before opening a drain valve.     Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Bethesda, MD

    Garage and attic tanks live through bigger temperature swings than closet units, which wears out plastic drain valves and wiring insulation faster. A pan under an attic tank is not optional. We check that the pan drain line runs to a safe spot and is not cracked or plugged, because one slow leak over a bedroom can bring down a ceiling. Gas tanks in garages need their ignition source at least 18 inches off the floor unless they are FVIR models.

    Commercial Tank Maintenance in Bethesda, MD

    Restaurants, salons, laundromats and small offices run tanks much harder than a household does, often 75 to 100 gallons cycling all day. Sediment builds up two or three times faster. We usually set commercial tanks on a quarterly or twice-yearly flush, with a dated log taped to the jacket that your insurance inspector can read. Downtime gets scheduled outside business hours.

    Electric Element Check in Bethesda

    Electric tanks have two elements, usually 4,500 watts at 240 volts, and the lower one does most of the work and takes most of the scale. Each element gets an ohm reading. A failed lower element shows up as hot water that runs out halfway through a shower. We replace it with the matching wattage and voltage.

    Gas and Electric Tank Checks in Bethesda, MD

    Gas and electric tanks share the same steel shell, but the parts that wear out are different. Gas units get a burner, thermocouple and vent check. On electric units we meter both elements and check each thermostat terminal for scorching. Both get a flush.

    Emergency Tank Inspections in Bethesda

    Sudden changes call for a look the same day: a tank that suddenly rumbles, hot water that turns brown, a puddle forming under the pan, or a relief valve that will not stop dripping. We diagnose first. The fix may be a flush and a drain valve, or the liner may be gone.

    T&P Valve Replacement in Bethesda

    Replacing a relief valve is a short job, but it has to be done right. We match the new valve to the heater's input rating. We drain the tank below the valve opening, thread in the new one with pipe dope rated for potable water, and reconnect a discharge pipe in copper, CPVC or another approved material. Never plug or cap that pipe.
