    Residential Basement Sump Pump Installation in Salisbury

    A typical house pit holds an 18 by 22 inch basin with a 1/3 horsepower pump, and that covers most homes. We size the pump to your inflow and lift, not to the sale sticker.     Sump Pump Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Salisbury, MD

    High water alarms cost little and buy you time. A standalone unit screams when water rises above the pump float, while a Wi-Fi monitor sends a text if the level rises or the power drops, which homeowners who travel tend to like. You hear it go off before we leave.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Salisbury

    Outages and heavy rain tend to show up together. Without house power, a battery backup keeps a second pump going, and a fully charged 75 amp-hour battery can run a typical backup pump for several hours of on-and-off cycling, longer on a light night. We load test the battery.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Salisbury, MD

    Battery backups earn their keep during outages. Nor'easters, tropical storm remnants and summer downpours can overwhelm storm drains across Maryland and push water toward basements. We add a second pump in the same pit with its own float, wired to a sealed AGM battery on a smart charger that tests itself and beeps when the battery gets weak. Your primary pump still handles the everyday load.

    Same-Day Sump Pump Installation in Salisbury

    Same-day emergency installs cover pumps that quit in the middle of a storm.     Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Salisbury
