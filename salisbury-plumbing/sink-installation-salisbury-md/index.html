    Double Vanity Sink Installation in Salisbury

    A second bowl means a second drain and two more supplies. Each bowl drains through its own trap or through a continuous waste to a shared trap, and the second faucet gets new stops. We check the vent size too. Both bowls should drain without gurgling.

    Bathroom Vanity Sink Installation in Salisbury, MD

    Vanity tops with integrated bowls are the easiest bath install. The top sits on the cabinet in a bead of silicone, the faucet and pop-up go on first, and the trap arm usually needs a small adjustment to meet the new tailpiece. Backsplash comes last. We caulk it to the wall.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Salisbury, MD

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. Since the rim sits higher, the faucet has to be taller or wall-mounted, and the drain must match whether the bowl has an overflow. No overflow means no slotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Restaurant and Office Sink Installation in Salisbury

    Breakroom and prep sinks get used hard. Downtime gets planned around your hours. Night installs are possible.

    Pedestal Sink Mounting in Salisbury

    The pedestal is mostly for looks. The basin itself hangs on a bracket lagged into 2x blocking behind the drywall, and if the old wall has no blocking we open it, add a board between the studs, and patch it back. Anchors in drywall will fail. A loose basin cracks the trap.

    Kitchen Sink Installation in Salisbury

    Kitchen sinks come in four mounts: drop-in, undermount, farmhouse and flush-set.
