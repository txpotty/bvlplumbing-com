    American Standard and Kohler Urinal Installation in Salisbury

    American Standard and Kohler make the china we install most. The spec sheet makes matching easy. We order both together.

    Home Gym and Garage Urinals in Salisbury, MD

    A garage urinal saves tracking dirt into the house. Freeze protection matters in garages. Ask us about the layout.

    Urinal Replacement and Upgrades in Salisbury

    Cracked or stained urinals are worth replacing. A hairline crack in vitreous china will eventually leak, and a bowl stained by years of hard scale often cannot be cleaned back to white, so a new fixture on the old carrier is usually the fastest fix. Carrier bolts get inspected first. Rusted bolts get replaced.

    Flushometer Urinal Installation in Salisbury

    Manual flushometers are still the most common setup. We install Sloan Royal or Zurn AquaFlush valves on a 3/4 inch supply, adjust the control stop for proper flow, and set the vacuum breaker the right distance above the bowl so it protects the drinking water. Handle side follows the layout. ADA units need the handle within reach.

    Residential Urinal Installation in Salisbury, MD

    Basement and garage bathrooms are where most home urinals go. We tie into the existing drain and vent with a 2 inch line, run a 3/4 or 1/2 inch supply depending on the flush valve, and frame in blocking or a compact carrier behind the wall. Many use 0.125 gallons per flush. That barely touches the water bill.

    Waterless Cartridge Urinals in Salisbury

    Each waterless brand uses its own cartridge. Falcon, Waterless Co, Kohler and Zurn units all trap odor differently, and the cartridge or sealing liquid has to be replaced after a set number of uses, which in a busy restroom can mean every few months. We set up a schedule with you. Spare cartridges should stay on site.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in Salisbury

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. A cartridge or sealing liquid blocks sewer gas while letting urine through, and it must be replaced on schedule. Your custodial team needs training. We show them how.
