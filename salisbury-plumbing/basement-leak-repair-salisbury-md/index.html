    Clogged Drain Tile Repair in Salisbury

    Footing drains clog with silt and roots over the years, and once they stop flowing the water table at the wall rises. We camera the tile from the sump inlet to see where it is blocked. Jetting clears it. Crushed clay tile has to be dug up and replaced with perforated PVC or corrugated pipe in a sock.

    Outside Wall Waterproofing in Salisbury, MD

    Excavating the outside of a foundation lets us fix the leak where the water starts. Water heaters work hardest during the cold months, when incoming water temperatures drop and household demand for hot water climbs. We pick a membrane rated for that exposure, lay new perforated drain pipe at the footing in clean stone, and backfill so the grade slopes away from the house at least six inches over the first ten feet. Downspouts get extended too.

    Urgent Basement Leak Service in Salisbury, MD

    Basement leaks rarely wait for business hours. Tanks tend to split at night when nobody is watching. Our on-call plumbers carry repair couplings, shutoff valves and a wet vac, so the first visit usually ends with the leak stopped and the water back on in the rest of the house.

    Supply Line Leak Repair in Salisbury

    Water lines along a basement ceiling tend to leak at fittings, valves and pinholes. Homes on private wells, especially in the low coastal counties, often deal with rusty mineral staining, odors or acidity that call for filtration or treatment. We cut out the bad section and replace it with copper or PEX, and if we find more than two or three pinholes on one run, we recommend repiping that run instead of patching it again. Repiping a basement ceiling is usually a one-day job.

    Water Damage Assessment in Salisbury, MD

    Before any repair, we figure out where the water came from and how far it spread.     Sump Pit and Pump Installation in Salisbury

    Installing a new sump means breaking out a section of slab, setting a lined pit, and running the discharge out through the rim joist. Most of our installs get a cast iron pump, a check valve and 1-1/2 inch discharge. Discharge cannot go into the sewer in most places. It has to discharge outside, far enough from the foundation that it does not cycle back into the pit, often ten feet or more.

    Wall and Slab Seepage Repair in Salisbury

    Seepage at the wall or the slab joint is usually surface water pooling against the foundation. Soils in Maryland range from heavy clay in the central counties to sandy, low-lying ground near the shore, and each affects buried lines differently. Before anyone cuts the floor, we check the gutters, downspout extensions and the grade along that wall, because fixing those outside often stops seepage for a fraction of the cost.     Crack Injection for Basements in Salisbury

    Injection works on poured concrete walls, not block. We set ports along the crack, seal the face with epoxy paste, and pump in polyurethane or epoxy from the bottom port up until resin shows at the next port. Epoxy adds strength. Polyurethane is better for active leaks because it foams on contact with water.
