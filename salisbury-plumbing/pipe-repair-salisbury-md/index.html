    Pinhole Leak Repair in Salisbury

    Pinhole leaks form when the copper wall thins from the inside. Water chemistry causes most. High velocity, poor grounding and aggressive water all speed it up, and the leak starts as a tiny drip that stains drywall or a ceiling, which we fix by replacing that length with new pipe. More pinholes often follow.

    PEX and Copper Pipe Repair in Salisbury, MD

    PEX and copper fail in different ways. Copper gets pinholes. Copper thins from the inside and develops pinholes, while PEX usually fails at fittings or where it was kinked during installation, and our plumbers carry soldering, press and crimp tools so they can repair either one with the right connection. Each repair is pressure tested.

    Frozen and Burst Line Repair in Salisbury

    Pipes in exterior walls, attics and crawlspaces freeze first. Ice splits the pipe. The split usually does not leak until the ice thaws, which is why many burst pipes are found hours after the cold snap. We repair the split and add insulation. We can add heat cable where needed.

    Drain and Waste Pipe Repair in Salisbury

    Drain pipe made of ABS, PVC or cast iron can crack, separate or leak at joints. Stains give it away.     Corroded Pipe Replacement in Salisbury

    Galvanized steel and old copper both corrode with age. Rusty water is a sign.
