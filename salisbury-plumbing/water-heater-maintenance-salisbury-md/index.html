    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Salisbury, MD

    Gas units need a clean burner and a good draft. Leak checks, flame inspection, burner cleaning and a draft test are part of every gas visit. CO is the risk. The thermocouple or flame sensor gets checked as well.

    Maintenance for Every Brand in Salisbury

    Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White, Noritz, Rinnai and more. We follow each manufacturer's procedures so the service supports any warranty claim. Model details matter. We bring the right parts.

    Gas Burner and Venting Checks in Salisbury

    A blue flame is what we want to see. Yellow or lazy flames mean poor combustion, so we clean the burner and combustion chamber, check the thermocouple or flame sensor, and confirm the vent has a proper draft and tight connections. Loose vents leak CO. The gas line and shutoff get leak tested.

    Water Heater Leak Checks in Salisbury, MD

    Small drips become floods. We inspect the supply connections, the relief valve outlet, the drain valve and the tank seams for moisture or rust trails, and we check the drain pan and the floor around the unit for signs of a slow leak. Rust stains are a warning. Early repairs protect floors and drywall.

    Descaling Tankless Units in Salisbury

    Error codes often mean scale. Scale in the exchanger cuts flow and trips the unit's safety shutoff. We descale through the service valves. Most makers recommend descaling at least yearly with hard water.

    Tankless Water Heater Service in Salisbury, MD

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale inside the heat exchanger chokes flow until the unit throws codes or shuts off mid-shower. We flush it with descaler. Igniter, fan and exhaust venting get checked too.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in Salisbury

    Businesses cannot run without hot water. We schedule around your hours. Larger units get the same flush, anode and safety checks.

    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Salisbury

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Calcium and magnesium settle on the bottom of the tank and form a layer that traps heat under it, so water boils in pockets beneath the scale and pops every time the burner or lower element fires. We flush until it runs clear. Bad tanks need a few rounds.
