    Valve, Resin and Brine Repair in Salisbury, MD

    Control Valve Rebuild in Salisbury

    Service, backwash, brine and rinse are the four cycles the control valve directs every time the softener regenerates, usually in the middle of the night. Hard water and chlorine wear out the seals, spacers and pistons. Leaks at the valve or water running to the drain are common signs. A matching rebuild kit fixes it.

    Control Valve Troubleshooting and Repair in Salisbury, MD

    Stuck pistons, worn seals and failed drive motors cause most control valve trouble, and the symptoms are hard water, constant regeneration or a leak at the head. The valve gets tested in place. Our plumbers carry seal kits and pistons for Fleck, Clack and Autotrol heads, which covers a big share of the softeners installed in homes around here.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Salisbury, MD

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. The bridge gets broken up and the tank cleaned out. Older brine tanks can crack, and we repair or replace those too. Keep the salt about half full.

    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Salisbury, MD

    Age and parts availability decide whether a softener is worth repairing. BVL Plumbing gives honest advice on both counts. A ten-year-old unit with a sound tank and a rebuildable valve is usually worth fixing, while an obsolete head with no parts available is a different conversation. You get a clear breakdown.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Salisbury, MD

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. A clogged injector leaves the brine tank full of water and the resin never gets recharged. The injector and screen get cleaned or replaced, and the brine line gets checked.

    Hard Water Symptoms & Diagnostics in Salisbury

    Scale on faucets means trouble. BVL Plumbing checks hardness at the tap, looks at each part of the system and gives you a repair plan for your Salisbury home. A hardness test strip tells the story in seconds, and we run one before the repair and again after so you can see the drop in grains per gallon.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Salisbury

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. This is common with older systems on treated city water. BVL Plumbing pulls the old resin and fills the tank with new media. Capacity comes back right away.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Salisbury

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. A failed timer motor, a bad circuit board or a stuck meter turbine are the usual causes, along with settings lost in a power outage. Each part gets tested. After the repair, the valve is programmed for your household size and hardness so you have soft water by the next cycle.
